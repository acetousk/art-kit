This is art-kit, a safe art computer for your kid.
When it turns on, your kid is in Krita, drawing a cat with the pen.
Your kid saves it as my-cat in their own Art folder.
Next, your kid opens the Library from the dock.
The Tutorials shelf holds about three hundred real art lessons.
Today it is Drawing 41, a room at an angle.
Each lesson gives the idea, short steps, and new words.
A page from an old book shows how an artist did it.
When the room is drawn, your kid taps Done.
Inspire shows a surprise picture from a museum.
One tap keeps a favorite.
Another tap opens it in Krita, to study or paint.
Search finds every lesson about cats.
In Notes, your kid writes a short note to Mom.
In Sweet Home 3D, four walls make a room.
Blender opens, ready for drawing in 3D.
Back in Krita, the cat is still there.
You see every file your kid makes, through the backup.
No internet, no web browser, and one app at a time.
It runs on a laptop with two gigabytes of memory.
art-kit is free. Find it on GitHub.
