# 4. First day

For the kid and the setup adult. Do this after [3-run-the-kit.md](3-run-the-kit.md). Time: about 1 hour. Sit together at the device.

## 1. Say the rules first (5 min)

Say this to the kid:

> Everything on this computer, your family can see. Things we send you show up in the Library under From Family.

Say this plainly:

- Your family can see **everything** on this device. That includes every file, every drawing, and every app's work.
- There is no hidden folder.
- The device has no internet for you. You cannot browse, chat, or install apps.

## 2. Meet the dock (5 min)

The dock is at the bottom of the screen. Tap an app name to open it.

| Dock item | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Krita** | Drawing, painting, lettering |
| **Sweet Home 3D** | Drawing rooms. Seeing them in 3D. |
| **Blender** | Drawing in 3D |
| **Library** | Lessons, museum images, and From Family |

The installer adds Sweet Home 3D, Blender, the dock, and the Library app.

## 3. Check the pen in Krita (10 min)

1. Tap **Krita** in the dock.
2. Choose **File**, then **New**. Make a new canvas.
3. Pick any brush.
4. Draw a light line. Then draw a hard line.

**You should see:** the hard line is thicker than the light line.

5. Turn the pen over. Erase a line with the eraser end.

**You should see:** the line disappears.

6. Draw a slow, long line for 30 seconds.

**You should see:** no gaps in the line.

If the pressure does not work, choose **Settings**, then **Configure Krita…**, then **Tablet Settings**. Tell the setup adult if it still fails.

## 4. Open the Library (5 min)

1. Tap **Library** in the dock.
2. Tap **Tutorials** to see the topics. Each lesson is one page.
3. Tap **Inspire** to see one museum artwork. Tap **Next** for another one.
4. Tap **Save** to keep an image. Tap **Open in Krita** to draw on it.

## 5. First lesson: Drawing 001 (20 min)

1. In the Library, open **Tutorials**, then **Drawing**.
2. Open lesson **001 Set up Krita and your seat**.
3. Read the lesson. Follow the steps one at a time.
4. Save your file as the lesson says.

**You should see:** the file in your Art folder (see section 6).

## 6. Where files go

| What | Where | Kid can change it? |
|---|---|---|
| Your art | `Art` folder in your home folder | Yes |
| Lessons and books | Library (`~/Library`) | No. Read only. |
| Files from family | Library, folder **From Family** | No. Read only. |

Save Krita files into the `Art` folder. Ask the setup adult if you cannot find it.

- [ ] The kid knows the rule in section 1.
- [ ] The kid opened Krita and drew a line with pressure.
- [ ] The kid opened Drawing 001.
- [ ] The kid knows where files go.
