# 027. Create a Photo of Your Room

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/Bedroom-Photo-1.png  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 024 to 026
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF pages 121 and 123; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Creating photos of the 3D view"

## The idea
A photo from Sweet Home 3D is what you show a client. It uses the 3D view you built. At a fast quality level it looks like the 3D view. At the best levels it looks more real, and lights and the hour of the day matter. de Wolfe says dark colors take in light. A dark room needs stronger light to look good.
"if a room is decorated in dark colors the light will be more readily absorbed than in a light-colored room" (de Wolfe, PDF page 123)

## Look
- PDF page 121, top: find the words "never a glare."
- PDF page 123, bottom: find the lines about bead covers, fringes, and silk shades that "obscure the light."

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Choose 3D view > Virtual visit.
2. Stand in the doorway. Look at the bed and the window. Pick a view where one thing is the star.
3. Click the Create photo… tool in the tool bar.
4. Choose a size and proportions. Pick a wide picture, like 1280 by 720.
5. Choose the fastest quality level. Click the button to create the photo.
6. Look at the result. Is the view good? Is the room too dark or too bright?
7. If you need to, close the pane, change the view, and try again.
8. When the view is right, choose one of the two best quality levels. Choose the hour of the day.
9. Start it. Computing the best level can take a long time. You can keep working while it runs.
10. When the picture is ready, click the save button.
11. Save it as a PNG: Bedroom-Photo-1.png in the My-Bedroom folder.

You should see: a PNG picture of your room, with light and shadows.

## Check your work
1. Is the main thing in your photo easy to see?
2. Did you test at fast quality first?
3. Is the light good for the mood you want?

## Make it yours
- Make a second photo at a different hour. Compare the colors.
- Make a photo from the bed, as if you just woke up.

## New words
- **quality level** — how real the photo looks and how long it takes.
- **render** — to compute a picture of a 3D design.
- **hour** — the time of day used for the light in the photo.
