# 003. Draw the Bedroom Walls

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 001 and 002
**Time:** 25–40 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF pages 109–110; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Drawing walls"

## The idea
Walls are the first thing you see in a room. Wheeler says a wall should be more than a line that divides space. It is the surface that holds the color and the mood. In the plan, you draw walls first. Doors and windows come later. Sweet Home 3D cuts the holes for you.
"The first impression of a room depends upon the walls." (Wheeler, PDF page 110)

## Look
- PDF page 109, first lines: find "the boundary stand for colour and beauty."
- PDF page 110: find the sentence that says rooms are good or bad by their "wall-quality."

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d in Sweet Home 3D. Keep your Bedroom-Measurements sketch in Krita next to it.
2. Click the Create walls button in the tool bar.
3. Click in the home plan at one corner of your room.
4. Press Enter. Type the length of the first wall and the angle. Press Enter again.
5. If Enter does not work, watch the tooltip. Click when it shows your length.
6. Draw the second wall the same way. Each click ends one wall and starts the next.
7. Draw all four walls. Double-click at the last point to finish. You can also press Escape.
8. Click the Select button to stop drawing walls.
9. In the home 3D view, drag with the left button to look at your walls from different sides.
10. Use the Zoom buttons to see the whole room in the plan.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a closed box of walls in the plan, and the same box in 3D.

## Check your work
1. Do the walls form a closed shape?
2. Do the angles look square (15° magnetism helps)?
3. Did you skip the door and windows on purpose?

## Make it yours
- Hold Alt while you draw (Windows) to turn magnetism off. Try drawing a wall at an odd angle. Undo it.
- If your room has a bay or an alcove, add those walls too.

## New words
- **magnetism** — a help that snaps walls to 15° angles.
- **tooltip** — a small box that shows a number near the pointer.
- **Select** — the mode for picking and moving objects.
