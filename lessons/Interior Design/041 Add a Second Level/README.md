# 041. Add a Second Level

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 003, 004, and 006
**Time:** 30–40 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 199–200; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding levels"

## The idea
Many homes have more than one level. Sweet Home 3D shows each level as a tab above the plan. The level below shows in a light color to help you line things up. Wharton says the stairs serve the people who live in the house, and the hall serves visitors. So the stairs and the front door are two different ideas. Start your whole-home file with two levels.
"The staircase in a private house is for the use of those who inhabit it" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 200)

## Look
- PDF page 200, top: find the sentence about the vestibule and "persons in no way concerned with the private life of the inmates."
- PDF page 200, middle: find why the stairs should not rise from the front door.

## Steps
1. Open Sweet Home 3D. Click the New home button. Click the Save home button.
2. Save the file as My-Home.sh3d in a new folder, My-Home, in ~/Art/Interior Design.
3. Click Create walls. Draw the outer walls of a house 26 ft by 32 ft inside. Press Enter for lengths.
4. Click Select. Choose Plan > Modify walls… Set the wall height to 8 ft and the thickness to 6 in.
5. Choose Plan > Levels > Add level. A second tab appears above the plan.
6. Look at the new tab. The walls of the lower level show in a light color.
7. Click the first tab. Select all four walls. Copy them.
8. Click the second tab. Paste. The walls land in the same place.
9. Double-click the second tab. You can also choose Plan > Levels > Modify level…
10. Read the table of levels. Check the elevation, height, and floor thickness. Set the floor thickness to 8 in.
11. Click Select. Look at the 3D view. Choose 3D view > Aerial view. See two floors.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: two tabs and a two-story box in 3D.

## Check your work
1. Do both levels have the same outer walls?
2. Does the table of levels show the right elevations?
3. Can you switch between levels with the tabs?

## Make it yours
- Add a third level. Remove it after you look. Which tab makes the home taller?
- Try a negative elevation to see what a basement level would be.

## New words
- **level** — one floor of a building.
- **vestibule** — a small entry space inside the front door.
- **floor thickness** — the depth of the floor structure between levels.
