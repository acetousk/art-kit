# 035. Related Colors in a Guest Room

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/Room-Studies/Room-Studies.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 021 and 034
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 35 and 268; Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF page 87; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF page 214

## The idea
Related colors are neighbors on the wheel, or near-neighbors in the same family. Wood and Burbank say a small home gains space when it keeps one color scheme throughout: the same color or related colors. Their guest room has soft taupe walls, a taupe carpet, and no pictures except one beautiful mirror. It is simple and calm. Parsons says an analogous scheme can take small "accidentals" of a complement. A guest also needs space: the ADA asks for clear floor on both sides of a bed.
"the same colour or related colours" (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 35)

## Look
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 268, top: find the taupe walls, the one mirror, and the carpet.
- PDF page 35: find the family groups "wood browns, beiges and yellows" and "violets, mauves and pinks."
- ADA PDF page 214: find 806.2.3. Clear floor on both sides of a bed.

## Steps
1. Open Room-Studies.sh3d. Click Create walls.
2. Start 3 ft below the living room. Draw a guest room 11 ft by 13 ft inside.
3. Add a door and a window. Click Create rooms, double-click inside, and name it Guest Room.
4. Open Bedroom-Color-Schemes.kra. Choose a related scheme: three neighbors from one color family.
5. Set the walls to the middle tone, the ceiling to the lightest, and the floor to the darkest.
6. Add a bed, two nightstands, a dresser, and a mirror. Color them from the same family.
7. Pick one small accent in the complement. Use it on a pillow or a lamp shade only.
8. Place two 30 in by 48 in test pads, one on each side of the bed.
9. Hang one mirror. Leave the other walls bare, the Wood and Burbank way.
10. Add a text label: Related colors — the family I used.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a calm room in one color family, with one small accent.

## Check your work
1. Are all large surfaces in one color family?
2. Is the accent small?
3. Do both test pads fit?

## Make it yours
- Try the same room in a second family. Which is calmer?
- Add a second accent. Does the calm break?

## New words
- **taupe** — a soft gray-brown.
- **family** — a group of related colors.
- **accidental** — a small spot of a contrasting color.
