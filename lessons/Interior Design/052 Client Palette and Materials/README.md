# 052. Client Palette and Materials

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/Client-Project/Client-Palette-and-Materials.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 020 to 023 and 051
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF page 317; Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF page 90; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF page 108

## The idea
Now choose the real colors and materials. Start with the light. The sample room faces north, so its light is cool and dim. Wheeler says a north room needs warm, bright color. Wood and Burbank say do not mix warm and cold on the walls. A visitor in a wheelchair needs a firm, stable, non-slip floor, and carpet with a pile of ½ in or less.
"Try not to mix warm and cold colours when planning your walls." (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 317)

## Look
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 317: find the matches: gray walls, white walls, cream walls.
- Wheeler PDF page 90: find the north room and its warm colors.
- ADA PDF page 108: find 302.1 (stable, firm, slip resistant) and 302.2 (carpet pile).

## Steps
1. Read the brief. Note the light direction and the client's likes and dislikes.
2. Open Krita. Choose File > New. Set 2400 px by 1600 px. Name it Client-Palette-and-Materials.
3. Use the Rectangle tool. Make five large squares: Walls, Ceiling, Floor, Big fabric, Accent.
4. Fill them with your colors. For the sample, use warm light walls and the blue the client likes.
5. Under each square, write why you picked it. Use the light, the likes, and the rule from the books.
6. Make four material patches: floor, sofa cloth, table top, and curtain. Use texture patterns as in lesson 19.
7. Write the material notes. For the floor, write: firm, not slippery, low pile if carpet (½ in or less).
8. Check: warm and cold mixed on walls? Is the ceiling lighter than the walls?
9. Add the palette to the Palette docker. Name it Client.
10. Choose File > Save As… Save as Client-Palette-and-Materials.kra in the Client-Project folder.

You should see: a board with five colors, four materials, and the reasons.

## Check your work
1. Does the palette fit a north room?
2. Is every pick linked to the brief?
3. Is the floor firm, stable, and low pile?

## Make it yours
- Make a second palette in the opposite mood. Which fits the client better?
- Ask the client to pick between two. Write what they said.

## New words
- **material** — what a thing is made of: wood, cloth, tile.
- **finish** — the surface treatment of a material.
- **slip resistant** — not slippery when wet or dry.
