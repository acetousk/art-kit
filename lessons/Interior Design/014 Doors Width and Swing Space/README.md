# 014. Doors - Width and Swing Space

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 005 and 013
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 123–125 and 128

## The idea
A door must be wide enough to pass. It also needs free floor in front of it, so a person can reach the handle and move out of the swing. The ADA calls this maneuvering clearance. The size depends on how you come to the door. You pull a door that swings toward you. A pull side needs more floor than a push side.
"Door openings shall provide a clear width of 32 inches (815 mm) minimum." (ADA, section 404.2.3, PDF page 123)

## Look
- PDF page 125: find Figure 404.2.4.1 (a). The dashed box is 60 in deep. It reaches 18 in past the latch side.
- PDF page 125 (b): find the push side. The box is only 48 in deep.
- PDF page 124: find the table of numbers. Find the row "From front, Pull" and the row "From front, Push."

## Steps
1. Open your real door 90°. Measure from the door face to the stop. This is the clear width.
2. Write the number. Is it 32 in or more?
3. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Find the door. Decide which way it swings. Find the latch side (the handle side).
4. If the door swings into the room, the inside is the pull side. The hall is the push side.
5. Copy a test pad. Make two pads: one for each side of the door.
6. Pull side pad: depth 60 in. Width is the door width plus 18 in, on the latch side.
7. Push side pad: depth 48 in. Width is the same as the door.
8. Place each pad in front of the door, on its own side. No furniture may touch a pad.
9. Move furniture if a pad is blocked. The wardrobe and the bed are the usual problems.
10. Look at the threshold in real life. The ADA allows ½ in high at most (section 404.2.5, PDF page 128).
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a clear pad at your door on each side.

## Check your work
1. Is your real door clear width 32 in or more?
2. Is the pull side pad 60 in deep?
3. Is the push side pad 48 in deep?

## Make it yours
- Reverse the door swing in the plan. Which side becomes the pull side?
- Test your closet door and your bathroom door with the same pads.

## New words
- **maneuvering clearance** — free floor you need to reach and open a door.
- **pull side** — the side a door swings toward.
- **threshold** — the strip on the floor under a door.
