# 045. Upper Floor and Halls

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 044
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 35–36; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 121 and 123

## The idea
Halls join the rooms. A hall must be at least 36 in wide. The ADA asks for passing spaces where a narrow route is long. Wood and Burbank give advice for small homes. If each small room and the hall differ, the home looks cut up into a checkerboard. Keep one main color, and change it only in small things.
"to cut up your home into a restless, unmeaning checkerboard" (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 35)

## Look
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 35, top: find the "restless, unmeaning checkerboard" and the idea of the "same colour scheme throughout."
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 36, top: find how a main background repeats and accessories differ.
- ADA PDF page 121, bottom: find 403.5.1, the 36 in clear width.

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the second tab, the upper floor.
2. Write the list of rooms: three bedrooms, one bathroom, and an upper hall.
3. Click Create walls. Draw the inside walls. Keep the stair arrival clear.
4. Make the upper hall 42 in wide or more. It must reach each bedroom and the bath.
5. Put the bedroom doors on the hall. Put the bathroom door near the stairs.
6. Click Create rooms. Double-click inside each space. Name each room.
7. Click Create dimensions. Measure the hall at its narrowest point. It must be 36 in or more.
8. Measure the clear path from the stairs to each door.
9. Pick one color family for the whole floor. Set halls and bedroom walls to related colors.
10. Choose a different accent color in each bedroom. Use it for one object only.
11. Add a text label: Halls — 36 in or more. Add the narrowest number.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: an upper floor with named rooms, a clear hall, and one color family.

## Check your work
1. Is the narrowest hall 36 in or more?
2. Do all bedrooms open on the hall?
3. Does the whole floor use one color family?

## Make it yours
- Add a linen closet to the upper hall. Does the hall still pass 36 in?
- Add a window at the end of the hall. What does it do to the feel?

## New words
- **circulation** — the paths people take through a building.
- **checkerboard** — a look of unrelated squares. Wood and Burbank use it for choppy color.
- **linen closet** — a closet for sheets and towels.
