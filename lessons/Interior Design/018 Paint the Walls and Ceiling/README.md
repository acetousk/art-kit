# 018. Paint the Walls and Ceiling

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 004 and 017
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF pages 146–147; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, sections "Editing walls" and "Drawing rooms"

## The idea
Wheeler's rule is gradation: colors go from strong at the floor to light at the ceiling. The ceiling stands for light, so it should reflect light into the room. A cream-white ceiling goes well with almost any wall color. Do not run a flower pattern over a ceiling. It seems to pull the ceiling down to your head.
"The rule of gradation of colour from floor to ceiling prescribes for the latter the lightest tone of the gradation" (Wheeler, PDF page 146)

## Look
- PDF page 146: find "the ceiling stands for light."
- PDF page 147, top: find the advice about a cream-white ceiling.
- PDF page 147, middle: find the warning about flower designs on a ceiling.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Click Select.
2. Double-click the Bedroom room. Open Plan > Modify rooms…
3. Set the ceiling color to a very light version of your wall color. Click OK.
4. Select all four walls. Choose Plan > Modify walls…
5. Pick a wall color for the inside side. Keep it lighter than the floor.
6. Look at the 3D view. Floor, wall, and ceiling should step from dark to light.
7. Make the ceiling pure cream white. Compare it with the tinted ceiling.
8. Pick the one that feels better. Wheeler says cream goes with almost any wall.
9. Select the wall behind the bed. Give its inside side a different, slightly stronger color.
10. Look from far away and from near. Is the effect calm?
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: dark floor, middle walls, light ceiling.

## Check your work
1. Is the ceiling the lightest surface?
2. Is the floor stronger than the walls?
3. Does the bed wall stand out only a little?

## Make it yours
- Try a strong ceiling color. What happens to the room height?
- Try the reverse: a dark ceiling, a light floor. Does the room feel upside down?

## New words
- **gradation** — a smooth change from dark to light.
- **tone** — how light or dark a color is.
- **tint** — a color mixed with white.
