# 010. Move, Turn, and Resize Furniture

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 009
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 105–106; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding doors, windows and furniture"

## The idea
A room has horizontal and vertical lines. Parsons says furniture looks best when it follows those lines. A rug thrown at a slant makes a second, rival idea. Not every piece must touch the wall. But a piece should have a reason for its angle. You will use the four corner handles of a selected piece to move, turn, raise, and resize it.
"furniture must be placed either with the structural suggestion of the rugs, or with the original structural arrangement of the room" (Parsons, PDF page 105)

## Look
- PDF page 106, first lines: find "One must dominate."
- PDF page 106, second paragraph: find what the book says about a piano or dresser across a corner.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Click Select.
2. Click one piece. Four handles appear at its corners.
3. Drag the piece to a new place. Then press the arrow keys to nudge it.
4. Drag the rotation handle to turn the piece. It turns in 15° steps. Hold Alt to turn this off.
5. Put the bed with its head on the center of one wall.
6. Drag the size handle to change width and depth. Undo it if the shape looks wrong.
7. Drag the height handle to make a piece taller. Undo it.
8. Add a lamp. Drop it on the nightstand. It rises to the top of the nightstand.
9. Drag the elevation handle on the lamp. See it float. Undo it.
10. Place a rug so its sides run parallel to the room walls.
11. Turn the desk chair 15° only if you have a reason. Write the reason as a text label.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: furniture in straight lines with the walls, and a lamp on the nightstand.

## Check your work
1. Does the rug run parallel to the walls?
2. Does every turned piece have a reason?
3. Does the lamp sit on the nightstand, not in the air?

## Make it yours
- Make two layouts of the bed wall. Which one feels calm? Which feels busy?
- Place a chair at an angle for a conversation, and note why.

## New words
- **handle** — a small mark on a selected piece that you drag.
- **parallel** — running in the same direction, never meeting.
- **elevation** — height above the floor.
