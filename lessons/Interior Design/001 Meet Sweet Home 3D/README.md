# 001. Meet Sweet Home 3D

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d  (new file)
**Builds on:** nothing
**Time:** 20–30 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 23–24; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, sections "User interface" and "Starting a new home"

## The idea
An interior designer plans a room before she buys anything. Sweet Home 3D is your planning tool. It shows your room from above (the plan) and in 3D at the same time. Parsons says a person should not pile things into a room just because the room exists. First you need to know what the room is for. In this course you will build one design file for your own bedroom, then grow it.
"Decoration implies, first of all, something to decorate." (Parsons, PDF page 24)

## Look
- Parsons, PDF page 24: find the two wrong ideas about decoration. One says "put ornament anywhere." One says "more things means more decorated."
- Parsons, PDF page 23: find the words "a room in which to live." The book says a room is not a museum.

## Steps
1. Open Sweet Home 3D on your tablet.
2. Find the four panes: the furniture catalog, the home furniture list, the home plan, and the home 3D view.
3. Click in each pane. A colored rectangle shows which pane has focus.
4. Press Tab, then Shift + Tab. Watch the focus move between panes.
5. Open File > Preferences…
6. Find the unit, the default wall thickness, and the default wall height. Choose the unit your tape measure uses.
7. Click OK.
8. Drag one chair from the furniture catalog into the home plan. Watch the 3D view.
9. In the home 3D view, hold the left button and drag. The point of view turns.
10. Click the Undo button until the plan is empty. Click Redo once. Click Undo again.
11. Click the Save home button in the tool bar.
12. Make a folder named My-Bedroom in ~/Art/Interior Design. Save the file as My-Bedroom.sh3d.

You should see: an empty plan, and the file name My-Bedroom in the window title.

## Check your work
1. Can you name all four panes?
2. Does a change in the plan show in the 3D view at once?
3. Do you know which button undoes a mistake?

## Make it yours
- Drag three different pieces into the plan. For each one, name a room it belongs in. Undo them all.
- Write one sentence: what is my bedroom for? Keep it for lesson 2.

## New words
- **pane** — one of the four parts of the Sweet Home 3D window.
- **home plan** — the top view of your home, drawn on a grid.
- **catalog** — the list of furniture and objects you can add.
