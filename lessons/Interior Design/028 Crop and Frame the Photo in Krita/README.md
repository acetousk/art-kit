# 028. Crop and Frame the Photo in Krita

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/Bedroom-Photo-1-Framed.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 027
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 386–387; Krita Manual, Crop Tool

## The idea
Parsons says a frame should hold the picture and set it a little apart from the wall. It should not fight the picture. He also says to show one picture at a time. The same idea works for your photo. Crop out what does not matter, add a thin border, and add a title. Then the picture looks finished.
"They hang one picture at a time of the right size in the right place" (Parsons, PDF page 387)

## Look
- PDF page 387, bottom: find the job of a frame. It holds, it separates, and it relates the picture to the wall.
- PDF page 387, middle: find the lines about the Japanese way of showing pictures.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Choose File > Open… Pick Bedroom-Photo-1.png.
2. Choose the Crop tool. Click once on the canvas to select the whole picture.
3. In the Tool Options docker, open Decoration. Turn on the thirds lines.
4. Drag the frame so the main thing sits on a thirds line. Press Enter to crop.
5. Choose Image > Resize Canvas… (Ctrl + Alt + C). Add 40 px of border all around.
6. Fill the border with a quiet palette color. Use the Fill tool or Edit > Fill with Foreground Color.
7. Choose the Text tool. Click below the picture and type My Bedroom — view from the door.
8. Put a smaller line under it: your name and the date.
9. Check that the text and the border do not pull the eye from the picture.
10. Choose File > Save As… Save as Bedroom-Photo-1-Framed.kra in the My-Bedroom folder.
11. Choose File > Export… Save a PNG of the same name.

You should see: a cropped photo with a border and a title.

## Check your work
1. Is the main thing off center, on a thirds line?
2. Is the border quiet?
3. Can you read the title at a glance?

## Make it yours
- Try three crops of the same photo. Which one tells the story best?
- Make a second photo from the bed, and frame it the same way.

## New words
- **crop** — to cut away the edges of a picture.
- **frame** — a border that holds and sets off a picture.
- **thirds** — lines that split a picture into three parts each way.
