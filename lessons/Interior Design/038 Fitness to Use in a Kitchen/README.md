# 038. Fitness to Use in a Kitchen

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/Room-Studies/Room-Studies.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 014, 015, and 036
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF pages 81–83; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 211–213 and 175

## The idea
Fitness to use means a room does its job well. A kitchen is a work room, and Wheeler wants easy-to-clean walls that keep out moisture and fumes. The ADA gives numbers for the work space. In a galley kitchen the aisle is 40 in at least, with two entries. A U-shaped kitchen needs 60 in. One work surface must be 30 in wide and 34 in high at most, and every appliance needs clear floor in front.
"Tiled walls, impervious to moisture, and repellent of fumes, are ideal boundaries of a kitchen" (Wheeler, PDF page 81)

## Look
- Wheeler PDF page 82, middle: find the oilcloth walls.
- Wheeler PDF page 83, top: find the shelf of blue plates.
- ADA PDF pages 211–212: find Figure 804.2.1 (pass-through kitchen) and Figure 804.2.2 (U-shaped).
- ADA PDF page 212: find 804.3, the 30 in wide work surface.

## Steps
1. Open Room-Studies.sh3d. Click Create walls.
2. Start 3 ft below the reading room. Draw a kitchen 8 ft by 12 ft inside.
3. Put a door at each short end. That gives two entries.
4. Click Create rooms, double-click inside, and name it Kitchen.
5. Add base cabinets along both long walls. Make each run 24 in deep.
6. Click Create dimensions. Check the aisle between the runs. It must be 40 in or more. Aim for 48 in.
7. Add a sink, a range or cooktop, a refrigerator, and a dishwasher on the runs.
8. Set the sink rim and the work counter to 34 in high or lower.
9. Make one counter section at least 30 in wide, clear of appliances. Name it Work Surface.
10. Place a 30 in by 48 in test pad in front of each appliance. Pads may overlap.
11. Keep the open dishwasher door off the sink pad.
12. Set the walls to a light, easy-to-clean color. Use a tile-like texture if you have one.
13. Add a text label: Fitness — aisle width and work surface.
14. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a kitchen with a 40 in aisle, a work surface, and clear pads.

## Check your work
1. Is the aisle 40 in or more, and are there two entries?
2. Is there a work surface 30 in wide, 34 in high or lower?
3. Does every appliance have a clear pad?

## Make it yours
- Change the kitchen to a U-shape. Test the 60 in aisle.
- Add a table in the middle. Do you keep the aisle?

## New words
- **galley kitchen** — a kitchen with counters on two facing sides.
- **work surface** — a counter section free for chopping and mixing.
- **appliance** — a machine like a stove or fridge.
