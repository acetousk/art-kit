# 024. Walk Through Your Room in 3D

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/Bedroom-Visit-Notes.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 018 to 023
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF page 111; de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF page 98; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Editing 3D view"

## The idea
A plan shows where things are. A walk shows how the room feels. Wheeler says walls should not make a person feel shut in a box. Sweet Home 3D has two ways to look: aerial view looks down from outside, and virtual visit puts a small person in your room. Walk in at the door, look around, and write what you see.
"to take away the sense of being shut in a box" (Wheeler, PDF page 111)

## Look
- Wheeler PDF page 111, middle: find how decoration gives "freedom to thought."
- de Wolfe PDF page 98, top: find the line about a window that frames a view worth seeing.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d.
2. Choose 3D view > Aerial view. Drag with the left button and use the arrow keys. Walk around the outside.
3. Choose 3D view > Virtual visit. A small visitor shows in the plan.
4. Drag the visitor to your door. Use the body angle indicator to face into the room.
5. Use the arrow keys to walk in. Look left, right, up, and down with the head angle indicator.
6. Drag the eyes elevation indicator lower. See the room as a seated person or a child sees it.
7. Drag the field of view indicator wider and narrower. See what changes.
8. Look at your window. Does it frame something good?
9. Open Krita. Choose File > New. Set 1600 px by 1200 px. Name it Bedroom-Visit-Notes.
10. Use the Text tool. Write five notes: first thing I see, best view, worst view, crowded, empty.
11. Choose File > Save As… Save as Bedroom-Visit-Notes.kra in the My-Bedroom folder.

You should see: five notes about your real design, from a visitor's eyes.

## Check your work
1. Did you view the room from the door?
2. Did you try a low eye height?
3. Did you write both a good and a bad note?

## Make it yours
- Walk from the bed to the door. Imagine it is dark. Where is the path hard?
- Show your notes to someone and ask: did I miss anything?

## New words
- **aerial view** — the 3D view from above and outside.
- **virtual visit** — a 3D view from inside, as a walking person.
- **field of view** — how wide an angle you see at once.
