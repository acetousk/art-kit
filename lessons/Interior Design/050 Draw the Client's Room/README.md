# 050. Draw the Client's Room

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/Client-Project/Client-Project.sh3d  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 003 to 007 and 049
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 58–59; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, sections "Drawing walls" and "Drawing rooms"

## The idea
You draw the room first, exactly as it is. Wharton says comfort depends on a few plain facts. They are the doors and fireplace, the windows, the furniture, and the privacy. Draw those facts right. Use the numbers in your brief.
"the position of the doors and fireplace, the accessibility of the windows, the arrangement of the furniture" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 59)

## Look
- PDF page 59, bottom: find the list of facts that make a room livable.
- PDF page 58, middle: find why old habits and old rooms do not always fit new needs.

## Steps
1. Open Sweet Home 3D. Click the New home button. Click the Save home button.
2. Save as Client-Project.sh3d in the Client-Project folder.
3. Click Create walls. Draw the room from your brief with Enter. The sample is 16 ft by 20 ft.
4. Click Select. Choose Plan > Modify walls… Set height and thickness. The sample height is 8 ft 6 in.
5. Drag a door onto the south wall. Double-click it. Set its width to 36 in.
6. Place the door at the right distance from the corner. Use the arrow keys and a dimension line.
7. Drag a window onto the north wall. Set width 48 in, height 48 in, elevation 30 in. Repeat.
8. Click Create rooms. Double-click inside. Name the room Living Room.
9. Click Create dimensions. Add the room size, the door width, and the window widths.
10. Click Add texts. Write a north arrow label: N. Place it on the north wall.
11. Choose 3D view > Virtual visit. Stand in the doorway. Compare the view with your brief.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: an empty room with the right walls, door, windows, and numbers.

## Check your work
1. Do the sizes match the brief?
2. Is the door on the correct wall and corner?
3. Does the plan show north?

## Make it yours
- Add a second room, a hall, next to the living room.
- Draw your own living room, and compare the two plans.

## New words
- **site** — the place where you will do your design.
- **north arrow** — a mark that shows which way is north.
- **existing** — what is there now.
