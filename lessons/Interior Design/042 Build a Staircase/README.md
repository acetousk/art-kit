# 042. Build a Staircase

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 041
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 209–211; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 157–158; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding doors, windows and furniture"

## The idea
The stairs join the levels. They need a good place, a good shape, and safe parts. Wharton says the hall often becomes a passageway, so the stairs there need strong, simple lines. A stair carpet should be one color, with no pattern. The ADA gives the parts: handrails on both sides, between 34 in and 38 in high. You will place a staircase from the catalog and size it to your levels.
"a stair-carpet, if used, should be of one color, without pattern" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 210)

## Look
- Wharton PDF page 209, middle: find the hall that "reverts to its original use as a passageway."
- Wharton PDF page 211, top: find why a pattern on the walls of a staircase is "specially objectionable."
- ADA PDF page 158: find 505.2 and 505.4. Handrails on both sides, 34 in to 38 in.

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the first tab, the lower level.
2. In the furniture catalog, look for a staircase. Open the categories one by one.
3. Drag it into the plan. Place it along an inside wall, away from the front door.
4. Open Plan > Levels > Modify level… Read the upper level's elevation. This is the total rise.
5. Double-click the staircase. You can also choose Furniture > Modify…
6. Set the stair height to the total rise, for example 104 in. Set its width to 36 in or more.
7. Leave the depth for now. You will set it from the run in lesson 43. Click OK.
8. Look at the 3D view from the side. Do the stairs reach the upper floor?
9. Click the second tab. Click Create rooms. Click each corner of the upper floor, leaving a hole at the stairs.
10. Double-click the last point. Choose Plan > Modify rooms… and name the room Upper Hall.
11. Leave 36 in of clear floor at the top of the stairs. Check with Create dimensions.
12. Check the bottom of the stairs the same way.
13. Click the Save home button.

You should see: stairs that rise to the upper floor, with open floor at the top.

## Check your work
1. Does the staircase height match the total rise?
2. Is there 36 in of clear floor at the top and the bottom?
3. Are the stairs away from the front door?

## Make it yours
- Try an L-shaped stair. Which fits your plan better?
- Check the rail. Is it on both sides? Add a label with the height.

## New words
- **staircase** — a set of stairs with its rail and walls.
- **handrail** — a rail you hold when you climb.
- **rise** — the height from one floor to the next.
