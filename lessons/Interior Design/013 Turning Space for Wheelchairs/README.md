# 013. Turning Space for Wheelchairs

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 012
**Time:** 30–40 min
**Read:** ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 110–111

## The idea
A person in a wheelchair must be able to turn around in a room. The ADA asks for a turning space: a circle 60 in across, or a T-shape in a 60 in square with 36 in wide arms. Doors may swing into this space, but furniture may not. Space under a desk with knee room may count. Test your room with a square pad, 60 in by 60 in. If the square fits, the circle fits.
"The turning space shall be a space of 60 inches (1525 mm) diameter minimum." (ADA, section 304.3.1, PDF page 110)

## Look
- PDF page 111, top: find Figure 304.3.2, the T-shaped turning space. Find the 36 in arms and the 24 in base.
- PDF page 111, middle: find 304.4. Doors may swing into turning space.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d.
2. Copy one test pad from lesson 12. Paste it.
3. Double-click the copy. Name it TEST 60x60.
4. Set the width to 60 and the depth to 60. Use inches, or 1525 mm.
5. Move the pad to the open middle of the floor.
6. If the pad touches furniture, find the free spot closest to it. Try the pad there.
7. If no spot is free, move the bed, the desk, or the chair. Slide one piece at a time.
8. Check the door swing. A door may swing into the pad. That is allowed.
9. Check the 3D view from above. The pad must show clear red floor.
10. Test a T-shape too: a 60 by 36 pad with a 36 by 24 pad joined under its middle.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a clear red 60 in square on the floor.

## Check your work
1. Does the 60 in pad fit with no furniture on it?
2. Did you read that a door swing may enter the turning space?
3. Could a person turn around in the room?

## Make it yours
- Find the smallest bedroom size where the pad still fits. Move the walls to test it. Undo.
- Test the closet door: is there room to turn in front of it?

## New words
- **turning space** — clear floor, 60 in across, for turning a wheelchair.
- **T-shaped space** — a turning space made of three clear arms.
- **knee clearance** — open space under a desk for knees.
