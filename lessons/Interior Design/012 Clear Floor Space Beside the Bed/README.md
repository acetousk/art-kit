# 012. Clear Floor Space Beside the Bed

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 011
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 111–112 and 214; Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF page 358

## The idea
The ADA Standards are rules for public buildings and some housing. They give exact sizes so that everyone can use a space, including people in wheelchairs. Designers use the same numbers as a test for any room. The first test is the clear floor space. It is a patch of floor, 30 in by 48 in, where a person can stand or park a wheelchair next to something. In a bedroom, ADA 806.2.3 asks for this space on both sides of a bed.
"The clear floor or ground space shall be 30 inches (760 mm) minimum by 48 inches (1220 mm) minimum." (ADA, section 305.3, PDF page 111)

## Look
- PDF page 112, top drawing: find Figure 305.3. A dashed box 48 in long and 30 in wide.
- PDF page 112, middle drawings: find Figure 305.5. A forward approach and a parallel approach to a wall.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d with your sized furniture.
2. Add any flat piece from the catalog, like a rug. This is your test pad.
3. Double-click it. Name it TEST 30x48.
4. Set width to 30 and depth to 48 inches. If your unit is mm, use 760 and 1220.
5. Set the height to a low number, like 1, and pick a red color. Click OK.
6. Place the pad beside the bed with its long side along the bed. Keep it free of furniture.
7. Copy the pad. Place the copy on the other side of the bed.
8. Place a pad in front of the wardrobe or dresser door. Turn it if you need to.
9. If a pad overlaps furniture, move the furniture. Slide the bed or the nightstand until the pad fits.
10. Look at the bed wall in 3D. Check that the pads are clear.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: three red pads on clear floor, with no furniture on them.

## Check your work
1. Is the pad 30 in by 48 in?
2. Is there clear floor on both sides of the bed?
3. Does a pad sit in front of the wardrobe?

## Make it yours
- Test the desk. Place a pad in front of it. Then test the door.
- Parsons says each object must do its own work. Which object in your room has the least free floor?

## New words
- **clear floor space** — a free patch of floor, 30 in by 48 in.
- **approach** — the way a person comes up to an object.
- **test pad** — a flat piece used to check a space.
