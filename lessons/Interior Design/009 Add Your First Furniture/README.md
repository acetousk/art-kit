# 009. Add Your First Furniture

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 001 to 008
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 56–57; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding doors, windows and furniture"

## The idea
Wood and Burbank give the first rule of making a room: decide what the room is used for. Then choose furniture for that use. Comfort comes from simple things. Deep chairs, tables where you need them, and lights where you read. For a bedroom, the first jobs are sleep, store, and study. You will place one piece for each job.
"The first rule is to determine the actual use to which you intend putting the room." (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 56)

## Look
- PDF page 56: find the questions the book asks. Is it a bedroom only, or a bedroom and a study?
- PDF page 57, second paragraph: find the list of things that make comfort.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Click the Select button.
2. Write the jobs of your real bedroom: sleep, store clothes, study, relax. Keep the list beside you.
3. In the furniture catalog, open a category. Find a bed.
4. Drag the bed into the plan, near the wall where your real bed stands.
5. Look at how the bed turned its back to the wall. This is magnetism at work.
6. Select a piece in the catalog. Click the Add furniture button. This is the second way to add.
7. Add a nightstand, a desk, a chair, and a wardrobe or dresser.
8. Look at the home furniture list. Click a column title to sort it.
9. Look at the 3D view. Turn it with the left button. Check that nothing floats or crosses a wall.
10. Click the Save home button.

You should see: five pieces in the plan, the list, and the 3D view.

## Check your work
1. Is there one piece for each job you listed?
2. Does each piece appear in the list, the plan, and the 3D view?
3. Is every piece inside the room?

## Make it yours
- Add one piece for a job you wish your room had, like a reading chair.
- Add a rug. Notice how it lies on the floor.

## New words
- **home furniture list** — the table of all pieces in your home.
- **piece** — one item of furniture or one object.
- **comfort** — ease: good seats, good light, things within reach.
