# 047. Furnish Every Room

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 009 to 016 and 044 to 046
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF page 186; Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 56 and 331

## The idea
Wheeler says use must govern furnishing. Good things in the wrong place will not be beautiful. So for each room, write the use first. Then choose pieces for that use. Wood and Burbank say not to crowd a room, because order and space bring rest.
"the use to which a room is put must always govern its furnishing" (Wheeler, PDF page 186)

## Look
- Wheeler PDF page 186: find "fitness" and "appropriate to the place."
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 56, bottom: find the questions about a room's use.
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 331: find "Avoid crowding your rooms."

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the first tab.
2. For each room, write its use in one line, as a text label. Example: Living room — talk, read, watch.
3. Furnish the living room. Use pieces sized like real ones. Group seats around one focal point.
4. Furnish the dining room. Table top 28 in to 34 in high. 36 in clear around it.
5. Furnish the kitchen. Aisle 40 in or more. Work surface 30 in wide.
6. Furnish the half bath. Toilet clear space 60 in by 56 in.
7. Place a bench or hooks in the entry hall.
8. Click the second tab. Furnish each bedroom with a bed, a desk, and storage.
9. Furnish the bath with a tub, a sink, and a toilet. Check its clear floor space.
10. Add a test pad, 60 in by 60 in, in each main room. Move furniture until each pad fits.
11. Hide the test pads: use Furniture > Modify… and turn off visibility.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: every room furnished for its use, with space to move.

## Check your work
1. Does each room have a use label?
2. Does each main room hold a 60 in turning pad?
3. Is any room crowded?

## Make it yours
- Take out the one piece you like least in each room. Is the room better?
- Add one personal object to each room, something with a story.

## New words
- **furnishing** — the pieces that make a room ready for use.
- **crowded** — filled with too many things.
- **personal** — about one person's own life and taste.
