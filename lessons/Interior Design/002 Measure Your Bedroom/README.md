# 002. Measure Your Bedroom

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/Bedroom-Measurements.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 001
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 33–34

## The idea
Before you change a room, take everything out of it. You can do this in real life or in your mind. Then decide how the room should look. For that you need facts: how long, how wide, how high. Wood and Burbank call the lines of a room its anatomy. Measure that anatomy and write it in Krita.
"begin by taking everything out of it (in reality or in your mind), then decide how you want it to look" (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 33)

## Look
- PDF page 33, first paragraph: find the words "inherited eye-sores." These are things you did not choose but must live with.
- PDF page 34, top: find the sentence about "the anatomy of your room."

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Choose File > New.
2. On the Custom Document tab, set width 2000 px and height 1500 px. On the Content tab, name it Bedroom-Measurements.
3. Click Create. Pick a pencil brush preset in the Brush Presets docker.
4. Stand in your room with a tape measure. Draw the room from above, as a rough box.
5. Measure each wall along the floor. Click the Text tool, click the canvas, and type each number.
6. Measure the door opening. Write its width, and the distance from the nearest corner to the door frame.
7. Draw a curved line for the door swing. Write which way the door opens.
8. Measure each window: width, height, and sill height. Write the distance from a corner.
9. Measure the ceiling height. Write it in a corner.
10. Mark fixed things: closet, heater, outlets, and the light switch.
11. Measure both diagonals, corner to corner. Write them. If they match, your corners are square.
12. Choose File > Save As… and save the file as Bedroom-Measurements.kra in the My-Bedroom folder.

You should see: every wall, door, and window has a number beside it.

## Check your work
1. Does every wall have a length?
2. Does every window have a sill height?
3. Did you measure the thickness of the wall at the door opening? (You need it in lesson 4.)

## Make it yours
- Measure your closet the same way and add it to the sketch.
- Take a photo-free "before" note: list five things you like and five you want to change.

## New words
- **sill** — the bottom edge of a window opening.
- **diagonal** — a line from one corner to the opposite corner.
- **anatomy** — the basic structure under the decoration.
