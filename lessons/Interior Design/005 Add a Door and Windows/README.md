# 005. Add a Door and Windows

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 002 to 004
**Time:** 30–40 min
**Read:** de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF pages 96–98; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding doors, windows and furniture"

## The idea
A door is not something to hide. de Wolfe calls it a fine part of the building. A window is a "gay, animate thing": by day it lets in sun and the view. In Sweet Home 3D, doors and windows are pieces from the catalog. Drop one on a wall and it turns to fit. The program cuts the hole for you.
"A door is not an ugly object, to be concealed for very shame, but a fine architectural detail of great value." (de Wolfe, PDF page 96)

## Look
- PDF page 96, bottom: find the sentence about French and Italian doors.
- PDF page 98, top: find "By day it should be full of sunshine." Think of your own window at noon.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Open Bedroom-Measurements.kra in Krita to read your numbers.
2. In the furniture catalog, find a door. Click the triangle beside a category name to open it.
3. Drag the door onto the wall where your real door is.
4. Check that it turned to fit the wall and the wall thickness.
5. Double-click the door to open the furniture dialog. You can also choose Furniture > Modify…
6. Set the width to the door opening you measured.
7. Click OK. Press the arrow keys to move the door until its distance from the corner matches your sketch.
8. Drag a window from the catalog onto the correct wall.
9. Double-click the window. Set its width, its height, and its elevation. Elevation is the sill height.
10. Repeat for each window in your room.
11. Look in the 3D view. Check that every hole is in the right place.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: your real door and windows, in the right walls, at the right heights.

## Check your work
1. Does the door swing the way your real door swings?
2. Is each window sill at the height you measured?
3. Can you see the daylight in the 3D view through each hole?

## Make it yours
- Add a window seat idea: place a low piece under a window and name it Window seat.
- Move the door to the other wall in the plan. Does the room still work? Undo.

## New words
- **elevation** — the height of an object above the floor.
- **architrave** — the frame around a door or window.
- **swing** — the arc a door makes when it opens.
