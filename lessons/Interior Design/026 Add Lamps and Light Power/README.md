# 026. Add Lamps and Light Power

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 015 and 025
**Time:** 30–40 min
**Read:** de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF pages 121–123; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Adding doors, windows and furniture"

## The idea
de Wolfe says light is the hardest part of a modern house. The aim is a glow, never a glare. Plan lights for what people do: read, dress, write, eat. Plan them early, and add plenty of outlets. A lamp can move later only if an outlet is there. Dark colors take in light, so a dark room needs more.
"To have the light properly distributed so that the rooms may be suffused with just the proper glow, but never a glare" (de Wolfe, PDF page 121)

## Look
- PDF page 122, top: find the mistakes with wall light openings. Too close to a door frame, too close together, too high.
- PDF page 123, bottom: find that dark colors absorb light.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d.
2. List three jobs that need light: reading in bed, working at the desk, dressing at the wardrobe.
3. In the furniture catalog, look for lamps and lights. Open the categories one by one.
4. Drag a bedside lamp onto the nightstand. It rises to the top.
5. Drag a desk lamp onto the desk.
6. Add a ceiling light in the middle of the room.
7. Double-click one light. Furniture > Modify… shows a light power setting. Set it lower for the bedside lamp.
8. Note: power only changes the photo at the two best quality levels. You will see it in lesson 27.
9. Add a text label near each light: Read, Work, Dress.
10. Check the switch from lesson 15. Is it between 15 in and 48 in high?
11. Look at the 3D view. Do lights sit where the work is?
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: three lights, each at a job, with labels.

## Check your work
1. Is there light for each job?
2. Is the bedside lamp low enough to read by, but not in your eyes?
3. Is the switch within reach?

## Make it yours
- Add a light on each side of your mirror, as de Wolfe suggests for a dressing table.
- Add an extra outlet idea: write where you would put one more.

## New words
- **glare** — light so strong that it hurts the eyes.
- **glow** — soft, even light.
- **outlet** — the wall socket where a lamp plugs in.
