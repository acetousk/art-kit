# 004. Set Wall Thickness and Height

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 003
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF pages 64–66; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, sections "Editing walls" and "Drawing dimensions"

## The idea
A wall has thickness. Your tape shows the inside of the room, so the plan must match the inside. de Wolfe says you can judge a good room before any furniture is in it. Proportion comes first. A wall is also a flat surface. Set the height, thickness, and color of each side of every wall.
"A wall should always be treated as a flat surface and in a conventional way." (de Wolfe, PDF page 66)

## Look
- PDF page 64, second paragraph: find the room where "true proportion has been observed."
- PDF page 66, near the bottom: find the rule that a wall is "straight up and down."

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Make sure you are in Select mode.
2. Measure the thickness of your real wall at the door opening. Write it down.
3. Double-click one wall. The wall dialog box opens. You can also choose Plan > Modify walls…
4. Set the height to your measured ceiling height.
5. Set the thickness to your measured wall thickness.
6. Set the left side and the right side to two different colors for a moment. Click OK.
7. Look in the plan and the 3D view. Find which side is inside the room.
8. Select all four walls with a selection rectangle or Shift + click. Choose Plan > Modify walls…
9. Give the inside side a light color. Give the outside side a different color.
10. Click Create dimensions. Point at the room side of a wall. Double-click, then click a third time.
11. Compare the number with your tape. If it is wrong, select the wall and drag its end point.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: inside lengths that match your sketch, and a light color inside.

## Check your work
1. Does each inside wall match your tape measure?
2. Is the inside color different from the outside color?
3. Is the wall height the same as your real ceiling?

## Make it yours
- Try a very thick wall (12 in). See how the room gets smaller inside.
- Pick the inside color from a color you already see in your room.

## New words
- **thickness** — how wide a wall is from one face to the other.
- **dimension** — a line that shows a length.
- **extension line** — the short lines at each end of a dimension.
