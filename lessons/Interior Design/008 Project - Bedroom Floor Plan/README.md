# 008. Project - Bedroom Floor Plan

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom-Plan.pdf  (and save My-Bedroom.sh3d)
**Builds on:** lessons 001 to 007
**Time:** 45–60 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 34–35; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Print"

## The idea
You now have walls, a door, windows, a room, and numbers. This project checks them all against the real room. Then you print a PDF. A plan is the "anatomy" of the room. Wood and Burbank say color and cloth are the flesh on that anatomy. Get the bones right first.
"Colour and textiles are the flesh of interior decoration." (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 34)

## Look
- PDF page 34, top: find the lines about the "austere scheme of line."
- PDF page 35, top: find the idea of one color scheme through a small home.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Open Bedroom-Measurements.kra beside it.
2. Pick three measures: one wall, one window, one door. Check them in the plan against your sketch.
3. If a number is wrong, fix it with Select and the arrow keys, or with Plan > Modify walls…
4. Check the ceiling height. Open Plan > Modify walls… and read the height.
5. Check that every label is in a clear spot and nothing overlaps.
6. Add a title text: My Bedroom — Floor Plan. Add your name.
7. Choose File > Page setup… Pick paper size and orientation. Set a plan scale that fits the page.
8. Choose File > Print preview… Look at the plan page and the 3D view page.
9. Choose File > Print to PDF…
10. Save as My-Bedroom-Plan.pdf in the My-Bedroom folder.
11. In Print preview, check that every number is readable at page size.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a PDF with your plan, your numbers, and your title.

## Check your work
1. Does the PDF plan match the real room within one inch on three measures?
2. Is every text readable at full size?
3. Is the title on the page?

## Make it yours
- Print a second PDF with only the 3D view. Name it My-Bedroom-3D.pdf.
- Ask someone to read the plan with no help. Fix what confuses them.

## New words
- **floor plan** — a drawing of a room from above.
- **page setup** — the paper size, margins, and plan scale.
- **PDF** — a file that looks the same on every screen.
