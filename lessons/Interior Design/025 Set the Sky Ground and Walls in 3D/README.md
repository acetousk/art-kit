# 025. Set the Sky, Ground, and Walls in 3D

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 024
**Time:** 20–30 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF pages 139–140; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Editing 3D view"

## The idea
A room belongs to its place. Wheeler says the colors of a house should come from where it stands. A house by the sea can echo sea, sand, and sky. A house in woods can echo leaf and bark. In Sweet Home 3D you can set the color of the ground and the sky. You can also make walls see-through, so you can look inside from above.
"the walls should repeat with many variations the tones of sea and sand and sky" (Wheeler, PDF page 140)

## Look
- PDF page 139, top: find the list of places: town, country, sea, woods.
- PDF page 140, top: find the sea tones: gray-green, blue to green, pearl, brown.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Choose 3D view > Aerial view.
2. Choose 3D view > Modify 3D view…
3. Find the ground color or texture. Pick one that matches your place: grass, sand, snow, or street.
4. Find the sky color. Pick one that fits the weather you like.
5. Find the light brightness. Try one step up and one step down.
6. Find the wall transparency. Move it a little. Walls become see-through.
7. Click OK. Look from above. Can you see the furniture through the walls?
8. Set the transparency back, so you can see a normal room when you visit.
9. Check your wall and floor colors against the ground and sky. Do they belong?
10. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a ground, a sky, and a room you can see into.

## Check your work
1. Did you change the ground and the sky?
2. Can you make the walls see-through and then solid again?
3. Do the room colors suit the place?

## Make it yours
- Pick colors for a house by the sea. Then pick colors for a house on a mountain.
- Choose a sky for the mood of your room: sunny, calm, or stormy.

## New words
- **ground** — the surface outside the house in the 3D view.
- **transparency** — how much you can see through a wall.
- **brightness** — how strong the light is in the 3D view.
