# 015. Paths, Reach, and Sticking-Out Objects

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 012 to 014
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 115, 118, and 121

## The idea
A path is a line you walk. It needs a clear width of 36 in. A narrow spot of 32 in is allowed if it is 24 in long or less. Objects at head or hand height may stick out 4 in at most. A person in a wheelchair can reach 15 in to 48 in above the floor. Put switches, handles, and daily items in that band.
"the clear width of walking surfaces shall be 36 inches (915 mm) minimum." (ADA, section 403.5.1, PDF page 121)

## Look
- PDF page 121, bottom: find 403.5.1 and the exception for 32 in.
- PDF page 118, top: find Figure 308.2.1. The high reach is 48 in. The low reach is 15 in.
- PDF page 115: find 307.2. Objects between 27 in and 80 in above the floor stick out 4 in at most.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d.
2. Click Create dimensions. Draw a dimension for the path from the door to the bed.
3. Draw a dimension for the path from the door to the desk, and from the door to the wardrobe.
4. Check each number. Paths need 36 in. A 32 in spot may be 24 in long at most.
5. Move furniture to widen any path that is too narrow.
6. Find your light switch. Measure its height from the floor. It must be 15 in to 48 in.
7. Add a small piece near the door. Name it Switch. Set its elevation in Furniture > Modify…
8. Check the wardrobe: set the highest shelf you want to reach to 48 in or lower.
9. Look at wall lamps and shelves. If one sticks out over 4 in at head height, label it.
10. Click Select. Click the Save home button.

You should see: dimension lines on every path, all 36 in or more.

## Check your work
1. Is every main path 36 in wide or more?
2. Is your switch between 15 in and 48 in high?
3. Does anything at head height stick out more than 4 in?

## Make it yours
- Add a path from the bed to the window. Can you open the window from the bed?
- Check the path from the door to the closet at night, with no light.

## New words
- **clear width** — the open space between two objects in a path.
- **reach range** — the heights a person can reach, 15 in to 48 in.
- **protruding object** — a thing that sticks out into a path.
