# 044. Plan the Ground Floor Zones

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 041 to 043
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 57 and 59; Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF page 139

## The idea
A home is a set of rooms with different jobs. Plan the jobs first. Wharton says to ask what each room is for, and who will use it. Put rooms for guests near the front door. Put private rooms farther in, or upstairs. Wheeler adds that the place of the house also shapes its look.
"the privacy of the room and the absence of the superfluous" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 59)

## Look
- Wharton PDF page 57: find the first sentence. A room must be planned for its purpose, not just named.
- Wharton PDF page 59, bottom: find the list of what makes a room livable: doors, fireplace, windows, furniture, privacy.
- Wheeler PDF page 139, top: find how a house in town and a house in the country need different treatment.

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the first tab, the ground floor.
2. Write the list of rooms: entry hall with stairs, living room, dining room, kitchen, half bath.
3. Put the public rooms near the front. Put the kitchen at the back, next to the dining room.
4. Click Create walls. Draw the inside walls. Use Enter to type each length.
5. Keep every room at least 8 ft in its short side.
6. Leave a clear hall from the front door to the stairs. Keep it 42 in wide or more.
7. Click Create rooms. Double-click inside each space. Name each room with Plan > Modify rooms…
8. Set a different floor color for public rooms and for the service rooms.
9. Click Create dimensions. Add the size of each room.
10. Add a text label: Zones — public front, private upstairs.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a ground floor with five named rooms and a hall that links them.

## Check your work
1. Can a guest go from the front door to the living room without passing the kitchen?
2. Does the kitchen touch the dining room?
3. Is every room at least 8 ft wide?

## Make it yours
- Draw a second plan, with the kitchen in front. Which plan works better?
- Mark in color which rooms get morning sun, and which get evening sun.

## New words
- **zone** — a group of rooms with the same use.
- **public room** — a room where guests come.
- **service room** — a room for work, like a kitchen.
