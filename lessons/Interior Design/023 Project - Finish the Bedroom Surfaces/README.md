# 023. Project - Finish the Bedroom Surfaces

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/Bedroom-Palette.kra  (and save My-Bedroom.sh3d)
**Builds on:** lessons 017 to 022
**Time:** 45–60 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF pages 331 and 317; Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF page 154

## The idea
A good palette is small. Five colors is enough: floor, walls, ceiling, one accent, and one for cloth. Wood and Burbank give simple rules for beginners. Do not use a cold color in a north or shaded room. Make the ceiling lighter than the walls, and do not mix warm and cold on the walls. Plan the palette in Krita first, then apply it in the plan.
"do not use a cold colour in a north or shaded room" (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 331)

## Look
- PDF page 331, middle: find the rules about walls and ceilings.
- PDF page 317, middle: find "Try not to mix warm and cold colours when planning your walls."
- Wheeler PDF page 154: find how a rug restores balance.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Choose File > New. Set 2000 px by 1200 px. Name it Bedroom-Palette.
2. Use the Rectangle tool to draw five big squares. Label them Floor, Walls, Ceiling, Accent, Cloth.
3. Fill each square with your chosen color. Keep the ceiling the lightest and the floor the strongest.
4. Under each square, write why you picked it. Use your light test and your scheme.
5. Check: no cold color in a north room? Warm and cold not mixed on walls? Write PASS or FIX.
6. Open the Palette docker. Add all five colors as swatches. Name the palette Bedroom.
7. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Set the floor (Plan > Modify rooms…), walls, and ceiling.
8. Apply the accent to one piece, like the rug or a pillow. Apply the cloth color to the bed cover.
9. Apply your wall texture from lesson 19 to the bed wall only, if it still fits the palette.
10. Look at the room in 3D from the door and from above.
11. Return to Krita. If a color looks wrong in 3D, change the square. Match them again.
12. Choose File > Save As… Save as Bedroom-Palette.kra. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a palette board with five PASS marks, and a bedroom that uses them.

## Check your work
1. Are there five colors or fewer?
2. Is the ceiling lighter than the walls?
3. Does the palette match the room's light?

## Make it yours
- Make a second palette for a different mood. Swap it in. Which is better?
- Ask a friend to guess the mood of each palette.

## New words
- **palette** — the set of colors chosen for one design.
- **accent** — a small area of a strong color.
- **scheme** — a plan for how colors work together.
