# 006. Draw and Name the Room

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 005
**Time:** 20–30 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 31–32; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Drawing rooms"

## The idea
A room in the plan is a floor surface with a name. The name tells you the job of the room. Parsons says that function separates one room from another. Each room is its own problem to solve. He also says that every made thing has two parts: use and beauty. Use comes first.
"The dominating idea of function separates one from the other" (Parsons, PDF page 31)

## Look
- PDF page 31, top: find the list of room types. Each has its own function.
- PDF page 32, middle: find the old master's rule about use and decoration.

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d. Walls, door, and windows should be in place.
2. Click the Create rooms button in the tool bar.
3. Double-click anywhere inside your closed walls. A room fills the space.
4. Click the Select button.
5. Double-click the room. You can also choose Plan > Modify rooms…
6. Type the name Bedroom.
7. Pick a floor color or texture. Pick a ceiling color.
8. Click OK. Look at the 3D view. The floor now has color.
9. Find the room name and area text in the plan. Drag them to a clear spot.
10. Click once on the room. Find the small points at its corners. Drag one point a little, then Undo.
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a named floor with color, and a name in the plan.

## Check your work
1. Does the room include half of the door sill?
2. Does the plan show the room name?
3. Does the floor color show in 3D?

## Make it yours
- Draw a second room: your closet. Name it Closet.
- Write the main function of your bedroom in one phrase. Put it in the room name, for example "Bedroom (sleep and study)".

## New words
- **function** — the job a room or object does.
- **surface** — a flat area, like a floor or a wall.
- **doorstep** — the floor under a door opening.
