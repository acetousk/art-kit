# 030. Proportion in a Dining Room

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/Room-Studies/Room-Studies.sh3d
**Builds on:** lesson 029
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 79 and 129; de Wolfe, The House in Good Taste, PDF page 29; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF page 223

## The idea
Proportion is the good breeding of a building. Wharton says it gives a room repose and distinction. de Wolfe says doors, windows, and fireplace must be in the right place and balance each other. That matters more than costly curtains. A window can be too wide, and a table too long. The ADA gives a number for tables: the top must be 28 in to 34 in high.
"Proportion is the good breeding of architecture." (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 79)

## Look
- Wharton PDF page 79, top: find "repose and distinction."
- Wharton PDF page 129, bottom: find how wide a window may be: five feet, and three feet in a small room.
- ADA PDF page 223: find 902.3. Dining tops: 28 in minimum, 34 in maximum.

## Steps
1. Open Room-Studies.sh3d. Click Create walls.
2. Start 3 ft right of the living room. Draw a dining room 12 ft by 14 ft inside.
3. Add a door in a short wall. Click Create rooms, double-click inside, and name it Dining Room.
4. Add a window in the middle of the long wall. Set its width to 5 ft or less.
5. Draw a dimension to check the window is centered. Keep the door and window from crowding one corner.
6. Add a dining table and six chairs. Set the table top to 30 in high.
7. Size the table to the room. Leave 36 in or more around it, from table to wall.
8. Put a rug under the table. Its edge must run past the chairs when they are pulled out.
9. Add a sideboard on one wall. It must not block the door.
10. Add dimension lines: window width, table length, and the path around the table.
11. Add a text label: Proportion — openings balance. Write what you balanced.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a dining room where the window, table, and rug feel matched.

## Check your work
1. Is the window five feet wide or less?
2. Is the table top between 28 in and 34 in high?
3. Is there 36 in all around the table?

## Make it yours
- Move the window off center. What does it do to the room?
- Change the table to round. Does the room feel calmer?

## New words
- **proportion** — the pleasing relation of parts to a whole.
- **opening** — a door, window, or fireplace in a wall.
- **repose** — calm rest that a balanced room gives.
