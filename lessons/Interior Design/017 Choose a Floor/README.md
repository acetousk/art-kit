# 017. Choose a Floor

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Bedroom/My-Bedroom.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 006 and 016
**Time:** 25–35 min
**Read:** Wheeler, Principles of Home Decoration, PDF pages 152–154; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF page 108

## The idea
Wheeler calls the floor the foundation of the room. It must look strong enough to hold the room in color. A pale wood floor can look weak next to strong walls, and a rug can restore the balance. The ADA says carpet pile must be ½ in high at most. A firm backing rolls easier than a soft pad. A floor must also be firm, stable, and not slippery.
"the floor is the foundation of the room" (Wheeler, PDF page 153)

## Look
- PDF page 153: find the sentence that says light rooms do well with hard-wood floors.
- PDF page 154, second paragraph: find how a small rug restores the balance.
- ADA PDF page 108: find 302.2 Carpet. Find "Pile height shall be ½ inch (13 mm) maximum."

## Steps
1. Open My-Bedroom.sh3d.
2. Double-click the Bedroom room. You can also choose Plan > Modify rooms…
3. Find the floor color or texture setting. Open the texture choices.
4. Pick a wood texture. Click OK. Look at the 3D view.
5. Try two more floor choices. Look at each one from above and from a low angle.
6. Pick the floor that looks strongest but quiet. Wheeler says strong, yet not loud.
7. Add a rug from the catalog. Place it under the bed so the bed sits half on it.
8. Double-click the rug. Pick a color that picks up one color from the room.
9. Check the rug edge. It must lie flat. The ADA wants fixed edges and a trim on loose edges.
10. Look at the room from the door. Does the floor look like the base of the room?
11. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a floor that looks like a base, and a rug with one echo color.

## Check your work
1. Is the floor stronger or darker than the walls?
2. Does the rug sit at least half under the bed?
3. Is the rug flat, with a low pile?

## Make it yours
- Test a floor with a color wheel idea: one cool wall, one warm floor.
- Put two small rugs, like Wheeler says. Do they work better than one?

## New words
- **foundation** — the base a design stands on.
- **pile** — the raised threads of a carpet.
- **texture** — how a surface looks and feels, rough or smooth.
