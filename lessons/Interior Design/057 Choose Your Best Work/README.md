# 057. Choose Your Best Work

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/Portfolio/Portfolio-Selection.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 008, 016, 034, 040, 048, and 056
**Time:** 30–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 337–338

## The idea
A portfolio is a small set of your best work, not all of it. Wharton says works of art last because of what is left out. She calls it the tact of omission. Pick six or eight pieces. Each must show a different skill. Cut the rest.
"works of art may be said to endure by virtue of that which is left out of them" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 338)

## Look
- PDF page 337, bottom: find the plan to leave out the superfluous.
- PDF page 338, top: find the words "tact of omission" and "the master-hand."

## Steps
1. Open the folders in ~/Art/Interior Design: My-Bedroom, Room-Studies, My-Home, Client-Project.
2. List every finished piece: plans, PDFs, photos, boards. Write the list on a note.
3. Rate each piece from 1 to 5 on four things: clear idea, clear numbers, good color, good picture.
4. Add the scores. Mark the top ten.
5. Check that the top ten show different skills: measuring, clearances, color, light, ideas, whole home, client work.
6. Choose six to eight. Cut the rest.
7. Open Krita. Choose File > New. Set 3000 px by 2000 px. Name it Portfolio-Selection.
8. For each chosen piece, open its picture. Copy it and paste it as a small thumbnail.
9. Use the Text tool. Under each thumbnail, write the title and the skill it shows.
10. Put the strongest piece first and last. Put the middle pieces in the order of the course.
11. Choose File > Save As… Save as Portfolio-Selection.kra in a new folder, Portfolio, in ~/Art/Interior Design.

You should see: a contact sheet with six to eight thumbnails and a skill under each.

## Check your work
1. Does each piece show a different skill?
2. Did you cut weak pieces?
3. Are the strongest pieces first and last?

## Make it yours
- Ask two people to pick their favorite. Do they pick yours?
- Fix one piece to make it better before the final portfolio.

## New words
- **portfolio** — a set of your best work, shown to others.
- **selection** — the act of choosing the best.
- **omission** — leaving something out on purpose.
