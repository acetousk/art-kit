# 039. Fitness to Use in a Bathroom

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/Room-Studies/Room-Studies.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 013, 014, and 038
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF page 146; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 164–166 and 175

## The idea
A bathroom must be clean and easy to use. Wood and Burbank praise white enamel for looks and for cleanliness. A door must not swing into the floor space a fixture needs. The ADA puts the toilet center line 16 in to 18 in from the side wall. It asks for 60 in clear to the side and 56 in clear to the back. A sink rim is 34 in high at most.
"white enamel, which cannot be surpassed for dainty appearance and sanitary cleanliness" (Wood and Burbank, PDF page 146)

## Look
- ADA PDF page 164: find 603.2.3. A door may not swing into a fixture's clear space. It may swing into the turning space.
- ADA PDF page 165: find 604.2 and 604.3.1. The center line and the 60 in by 56 in clearance.
- ADA PDF page 175, bottom: find 607.2, the clearance in front of a tub.

## Steps
1. Open Room-Studies.sh3d. Click Create walls.
2. Start to the right of the kitchen. Draw a bathroom 8 ft by 10 ft inside.
3. Add a door. Click Create rooms, double-click inside, and name it Bathroom.
4. Add a toilet in a corner. Its center line must be 16 in to 18 in from the side wall.
5. Add a test pad 60 in wide and 56 in deep in front of the toilet. Keep it free.
6. Add a sink, rim 34 in or lower. Add a 30 in by 48 in pad in front of it.
7. Add a bathtub along one wall. In front of it, add a pad 30 in wide and tub-long.
8. Place a 60 in by 60 in turning pad on open floor. Pads may overlap.
9. Make the door swing out, or against a free wall. It must not cross a fixture pad.
10. Add a mirror above the sink. Its bottom edge must be 40 in or lower.
11. Set the room to clean white or a pale tint of the bedroom color.
12. Add a text label: Fitness — clear floor at every fixture.
13. Click the Save home button.

You should see: a bathroom with clear pads at toilet, sink, and tub, and a free turning space.

## Check your work
1. Does the toilet pad measure 60 in by 56 in and stay clear?
2. Does the door stay out of every fixture pad?
3. Is the mirror's bottom edge 40 in or lower?

## Make it yours
- Shrink the room by 12 in. Which test fails first?
- Move the sink to the toilet's back wall. Check the 66 in rule in section 604.3.2.

## New words
- **fixture** — a fixed part like a toilet, sink, or tub.
- **enamel** — a hard, smooth, glossy paint.
- **center line** — the line through the middle of an object.
