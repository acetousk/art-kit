# 048. Project - My Home, Two Levels

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home-Plan-Ground.pdf and My-Home-Plan-Upper.pdf  (and save My-Home.sh3d)
**Builds on:** lessons 041 to 047
**Time:** 50–60 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 357–358; Wood and Burbank, The Art of Interior Decoration, PDF page 35; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Print"

## The idea
Parsons says each house expresses one person's idea of fit, beauty, and good taste. Your home should show your ideas and still work. Bring the whole home together. Use one color family through the house. Check the stairs and the clear floor. Then print each level as a PDF.
"Each house is the natural expression of an individual's idea of functional fitness, beauty in environment and good taste." (Parsons, PDF page 357)

## Look
- Parsons PDF page 357, top: find "Function or fitness is the fundamental idea of the room."
- Wood and Burbank PDF page 35: find the advice to keep a main background and vary the accessories.

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the first tab.
2. Check the colors. All rooms should share one family, with small changes in accents.
3. Check the stair math from lesson 43. Check the clear floor at top and bottom.
4. Check each door width and each hall width. Fix any under 32 in or 36 in.
5. Add dimension lines for each room. Label each room with its name and its use.
6. Add the title text: My Home — Ground Floor. Add your name.
7. Choose File > Page setup… Set a scale so the plan fits the page.
8. Choose File > Print preview… Check the page.
9. Choose File > Print to PDF… Save as My-Home-Plan-Ground.pdf in the My-Home folder.
10. Click the second tab. Add the title My Home — Upper Floor. Repeat steps 5 to 9. Save My-Home-Plan-Upper.pdf.
11. Choose 3D view > Aerial view. Look at the whole home from the street side.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: two PDFs, one per level, each with titles, labels, and numbers.

## Check your work
1. Is each PDF readable at full size?
2. Do stairs and halls meet the numbers?
3. Does the home use one main color family?

## Make it yours
- Make a third PDF of the 3D view from outside. Use File > Print to PDF… with the 3D view.
- Show the plan to someone and ask them to walk it with a finger. Where do they stop?

## New words
- **whole-home plan** — plans of every level of a home.
- **expression** — a way to show who you are.
- **print preview** — a look at the page before you print.
