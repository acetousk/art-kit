# 046. Doors and Windows for the Whole Home

**You make:** add to ~/Art/Interior Design/My-Home/My-Home.sh3d
**Builds on:** lessons 005, 014, and 045
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Wharton and Codman, The Decoration of Houses, PDF pages 128–129; ADA 2010 Standards, PDF pages 123 and 128

## The idea
Openings are the leading features of a room. Wharton says they carry the three chief needs of comfort: light, heat, and a way in. Placed well, they leave a room needing little else. A window should be five feet wide at most, and three feet in a small room. Put the top of the window near the ceiling for more light and air. The ADA says doors are 32 in clear, with a threshold ½ in high at most.
"the three chief essentials of its comfort" (Wharton and Codman, PDF page 128)

## Look
- PDF page 128, top: find the three chief needs: light, heat, and means of access.
- PDF page 129, middle: find why a high window lights and airs a room better.
- ADA PDF page 128: find 404.2.5, the threshold height.

## Steps
1. Open My-Home.sh3d. Click the first tab.
2. Drag a door onto the front wall. Place the front door near the center.
3. Double-click it. Set the width to 36 in or more. Check the clear opening is 32 in or more.
4. Add doors between rooms. Place each on the wall nearest the path.
5. Add windows. Put at least one in each room. Give living areas wider windows, up to 5 ft.
6. Give small rooms and the half bath a window up to 3 ft.
7. Double-click a window. Raise its elevation and height so the top reaches near the ceiling.
8. Click the second tab. Add doors and windows the same way.
9. Make sure no door hits a wall or a piece of furniture when it opens.
10. Choose 3D view > Virtual visit. Walk from the front door to each room.
11. Add a text label near the front door: Front door — width, threshold.
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: every room has a way in and a window, and each door opens clear.

## Check your work
1. Does every room have a window?
2. Is the front door 36 in wide, with a low threshold?
3. Does each door swing clear?

## Make it yours
- Add a large window with a built-in seat. Does the view help the room?
- Move one door and see how it changes the path.

## New words
- **opening** — a door, window, or arch cut in a wall.
- **cornice** — the molding where the wall meets the ceiling.
- **ventilation** — fresh air moving through a room.
