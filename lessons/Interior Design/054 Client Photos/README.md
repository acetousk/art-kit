# 054. Client Photos

**You make:** ~/Art/Interior Design/Client-Project/Client-Photo-1.png, Client-Photo-2.png, Client-Photo-3.png, and Client-Photo-4-Top.png
**Builds on:** lessons 027, 028, 052, and 053
**Time:** 35–45 min
**Read:** Parsons, Interior Decoration, PDF pages 49–50; Sweet Home 3D User's Guide, section "Creating photos of the 3D view"

## The idea
The client will judge your design by pictures. Parsons says the color of an object stays the same, but the light that reaches the eye changes. A room that looks warm at noon may look gray at dusk. So pick the hour with care. Take three photos from eye height, and a fourth from straight above.
"The change in their appearance in each case is due to the change in light in which these observations are made." (Parsons, PDF page 50)

## Look
- PDF page 49, middle: find that color comes from the vibration of light.
- PDF page 50, middle: find the list of light conditions: bright sun, shade, dark day, before dark, darkness.

## Steps
1. Open Client-Project.sh3d. Apply your palette in Plan > Modify walls… and Plan > Modify rooms…
2. Use Furniture > Modify… to set the color of each piece to match your board.
3. Add lamps from the catalog by the reading chair and the homework table. Add a ceiling light.
4. Choose 3D view > Modify 3D view… Set the ground and sky. Set the brightness.
5. Choose 3D view > Virtual visit. Stand in the doorway. Look at the main seating group.
6. Click Create photo… Choose a wide size. Choose a fast level and test. Fix anything that looks wrong.
7. Switch to one of the two best quality levels. Choose the hour: noon for the north light. Make Client-Photo-1.png.
8. Move to the sofa. Look toward the window. Make Client-Photo-2.png.
9. Move to the window. Look back toward the door. Make Client-Photo-3.png.
10. Choose 3D view > Aerial view. Look straight down from above. Make a fast photo: Client-Photo-4-Top.png.
11. Check each photo. Is the main thing clear? Are the lights good?
12. Click the Save home button.

You should see: four PNG files in the Client-Project folder.

## Check your work
1. Does each photo show a different part of the room?
2. Do the colors match your palette board?
3. Is the top view clear enough to read the layout?

## Make it yours
- Make a photo at evening. Compare it with noon. Which one would you show the client?
- Make a photo of one detail: the reading corner.

## New words
- **eye height** — the height of a person's eyes, used as the camera height.
- **top view** — a view from straight above, like a plan.
- **detail** — a close view of one small part.
