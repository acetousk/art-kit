# 014. Project - Roman Capital Quotation

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/14-roman-quote.kra
**Builds on:** lessons 009 to 013
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Brown, PDF pages 28, 30, 32, 33; Johnston, PDF pages 273 and 262

## The idea
This project puts the capitals together. You will letter two lines. Use the width rules, thick and thin rules, serifs and overshoot. Johnston says "When in doubt, use Roman Capitals." Brown says the way to get even color is to keep the same area of white between letters. Space each pair by eye.

> "keeping as nearly as possible the same area of white between each letter and its neighbor" (Brown, PDF p. 30)

## Look
Brown page 28 and 29: see how well each letter fits its square. Page 30 and 32: read about spacing and the holes in a line of letters.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open lettering-sheet.kra and Save As 14-roman-quote.kra.
2. On Guides, draw lines for two rows of capitals: y = 240 and 560, then y = 720 and 1040.
3. Add a paint layer named Sketch. Pick Pencil-2 at 8 px.
4. Write line 1: WHEN IN DOUBT. Write line 2: USE ROMAN CAPITALS. Fit both between the lines.
5. Plan the widths: O round and wide, I narrow, M and W wide. Sketch light letters first.
6. Space the letters so the white between pairs looks even. Fix any big gaps.
7. Add a paint layer named Ink. Use Ink-2 Fineliner. Trace with 20 px thick strokes and 6 px thin strokes.
8. Apply the rules: thin horizontals, thick down-right slopes, thick stems.
9. Add serifs to every free end.
10. Add overshoot to O, C, S, Q and to the points of A and V.
11. Hide Sketch and Guides. Stand back. Do any letters look too dark or too light?
12. Fix up two letters. Then press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/14-roman-quote.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the white space between letters about even?
- Do your thick and thin strokes follow Brown's rules?
- Do the round letters overshoot the lines?

## Make it yours
- Letter a different quote of your own, maybe your favorite lyric.
- Space your words farther apart for emphasis, as Brown suggests.

## New words
- **color** — how dark or light a line of lettering looks overall
- **even spacing** — equal visible white between letters
- **emphasis** — making one word stand out
