# 035. Project - Lettering a Word as Art

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/35-word-art.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 034
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF page 9 (left half); Speedball, PDF page 34 (right half); Brown, PDF page 219; Johnston, PDF page 264

## The idea
Now make one word into a picture. Speedball's display plates show letters with decoration, such as dots, lines and curls. Brown's script shows swashes that fill the space. Johnston lists ways to fit letters: link them, set one inside another, or draw a stem up into another letter. Keep the word easy to read first. Add the art second.

> "Letters which are large enough may be linked or looped together" (Johnston, PDF p. 264)

## Look
Speedball page 9, left half: see the Display Style B letters with small ornaments inside each stem. Page 34, right half: see the Vanitie Roman with a double line. Brown page 219: see the swashes and loops that fill the space.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open 34-word-feelings.kra to see your best word. Press Ctrl+N and make a 2400 by 1600 px image.
2. Save it as 35-word-art.kra.
3. Choose your word, 3 to 6 letters long. Write it large with the Text tool and look at it.
4. Add a Sketch layer. With Pencil-2, sketch the word with the style you chose last lesson.
5. Decide on one idea to add: link letters, fill the counters, add swashes, or add a pattern.
6. Redraw the letters with a bolder outline on an Ink layer. Pick Ink-2 Fineliner.
7. Link two letters, or let a stem become part of the next letter.
   You should see: two letters that share a stroke.
8. Add one decoration inside the letters, such as dots or lines. Keep it simple and repeat it.
9. Add one flourish or swash that frames the word.
10. Hide the Sketch. Use the Fill tool to put one color in the counters and one in the background.
11. Check: can you still read the word in one second?
12. Fix any part that hurts reading. Then press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/35-word-art.kra.

## Check your work
- Can you read the word at a glance?
- Is the decoration the same in every letter?
- Does the style match the meaning of the word?

## Make it yours
- Make a word-art poster with your name.
- Make the same word in a very different style.

## New words
- **decoration** — a small design added to a letter
- **linking** — joining two letters with a shared stroke
- **framing** — a curve that surrounds the word
