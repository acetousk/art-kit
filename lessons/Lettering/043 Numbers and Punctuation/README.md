# 043. Numbers and Punctuation

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/font-extra.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 042
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 101, 102, 103, 104, 130 and 131

## The idea
FontForge says numerals are hard. They have many curves, many strokes in 8 and 5, and large white spaces in 1 and 7. Zero must look different from O. FontForge says the comma should be a little lighter than the period. Test several forms of the question mark before you choose one. Draw each in the same stroke weight.

> "Numerals are often difficult for font designers" (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 130)

## Look
FontForge page 130: read the reasons numerals are hard. Page 131: see proportional lining numbers. Page 102: see the two common forms of the comma. Page 103: see the question mark.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open font-sheet.kra. Save As font-extra.kra.
2. Pick Ink-2 Fineliner at 50 px. Add a paint layer named Glyphs. Use cap height lines for numbers.
3. Write 0 to 5 in row 1, 6 to 9 and the period in row 2.
4. Draw 0 as an ellipse narrower than your O.
5. Draw 1 with a stem and a small flag. Draw 7 with a bar and a diagonal.
6. Draw 2, 3 and 5 with the curve from your s. Draw 4 with straight strokes.
7. Draw 6, 8 and 9 from circles joined with your o shape.
8. Thin the 8 by 3 px if it looks dense.
9. Draw the period as one dab of the 50 px brush on the baseline.
10. Draw the comma: a dab and a short tail. Make the tail thinner, 44 px.
11. Draw the question mark: an open curve over a dab. Try two versions and keep the better one.
12. Write 1 7 8 0 2024 as a test line. Do all numbers look even?
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/font-extra.kra.

## Check your work
- Is your 0 different from your O?
- Does the 8 look as dark as the 1?
- Is the comma a little lighter than the period?

## Make it yours
- Add the exclamation mark.
- Make old style numbers that rise and fall.

## New words
- **numeral** — a number sign
- **proportional** — with each glyph a different width
- **punctuation** — marks like the period and comma
