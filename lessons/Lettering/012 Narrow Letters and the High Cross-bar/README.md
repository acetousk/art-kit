# 012. Narrow Letters and the High Cross-bar

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/12-narrow-letters.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 011
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Johnston, PDF pages 276 to 278; Brown, PDF page 30

## The idea
B, E, F, R, S and P are about half as wide as they are tall. Johnston says each looks like two small letters, one on top of the other, each half the height. The top part should be a little smaller than the bottom part. So the cross-bar sits just above the middle. This makes the letter look steady.

> "the eye seems to prefer looking upon the tops of things" (Johnston, PDF p. 278)

## Look
Johnston page 276: see the diagram of B, E, R, S as two half-height letters. Brown page 30: read how the center line is used as the bottom of the cross-bar in B, E, H, P and R.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open 11-wide-letters.kra and Save As 12-narrow-letters.kra.
2. On Guides, keep lines at y = 160 and 480.
3. Add a guide line at y = 320, the exact middle.
4. Add a paint layer named Narrow. Use Ink-2 Fineliner 20 px thick, 6 px thin.
5. Draw E 4 cells wide.
6. Put the middle bar so it sits just above the middle line, with its bottom edge on y = 320.
7. Draw F the same way. Draw P with the bow sitting on the middle line.
8. Draw B with two bows. Make the lower bow a little bigger than the upper.
   You should see: the top bow is smaller, the bottom bow larger.
9. Draw R like P with a tail. Draw S with the upper curve smaller than the lower.
10. Draw K and L about 4 cells wide.
11. Cover your letters with your hand, half up and half down. Do they look steady?
12. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/12-narrow-letters.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the top part of B smaller than the bottom part?
- Does the bar of E sit above the true middle?
- Are these letters about half as wide as tall?

## Make it yours
- Write the word BERLIN. Check the bars.
- Put the bar exactly in the middle. Does it look low?

## New words
- **narrow letter** — a capital about half as wide as it is tall
- **cross-bar** — the horizontal stroke in letters like E, H, A
- **waist line** — a guide line near the middle of a letter
