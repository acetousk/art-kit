# 011. Wide Letters, O, H, N and Friends

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/11-wide-letters.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 010
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Johnston, PDF pages 273 to 276; Brown, PDF pages 30 and 31

## The idea
Johnston sorts Roman capitals by width. Round wide letters are O, Q, C, G, D. Square wide letters are M, W, H, A, N, V, T, U and Z. These are about as wide as they are tall. Letters are not all the same width. Making every letter fill the same space looks poor. Brown's page shows a width chart.

> "Their mean width is properly about equal to their height." (Johnston, PDF p. 275)

## Look
Johnston page 273: see the list of wide and narrow letters. Brown page 31: see the chart of letters with widths marked under each.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open 10-thick-thin-serifs.kra and Save As 11-wide-letters.kra.
2. Hide the old letters.
3. Draw guide lines at y = 160 and 480 and again at y = 640 and 960 on Guides.
4. Add a paint layer named Wide. Use Ink-2 Fineliner 20 px thick and 6 px thin as before.
5. Row 1: draw O as a circle 8 cells wide. Draw Q like O with a tail.
6. Draw C, G and D next. Make them a little narrower than O.
   You should see: C, G and D look round but slightly narrower than O.
7. Row 2: draw H, N and V. Make each about 8 cells wide, the same as the height.
8. Draw M and W. Make them wider, about 10 cells.
9. Draw A and T with about the same width as H.
10. Draw I next to them. Is it much narrower than all the others?
11. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/11-wide-letters.kra.

## Check your work
- Is O as wide as it is tall?
- Is I much narrower than H?
- Are M and W wider than H?

## Make it yours
- Write the word ROMAN. Check each letter's width.
- Make O 12 cells wide. Does it look heavy and clumsy?

## New words
- **wide letter** — a capital about as wide as it is tall
- **round form** — a letter built on a circle
- **width** — the distance from the left to the right of a letter
