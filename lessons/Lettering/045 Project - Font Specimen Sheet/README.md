# 045. Project - Font Specimen Sheet

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/45-font-specimen.kra
**Builds on:** lessons 036 to 044
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 91 and 33; Speedball, PDF page 9 (left half); Brown, PDF page 231

## The idea
A specimen sheet shows a font to the world. It shows the name, the alphabet, the numbers and a real text. FontForge says to test with real words, and to avoid using the same text again and again. Speedball's Display plate shows a whole alphabet on one card. Brown says the first aim is readable letters, and the second is beauty. Put all your work on one sheet.

> "the draughtsman's first aim should be to make his lettering readable" (Brown, PDF p. 231)

## Look
Speedball page 9, left half: see a whole alphabet laid out on one card in rows. FontForge page 91: read about testing with real text and the stale text effect.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a 2400 by 3000 px image. Save it as 45-font-specimen.kra.
2. Add a Title layer. Write your typeface name with your own letters.
3. Copy the glyphs from your sheets and place them with your spacing.
4. Hide the Guides layers in font-lower.kra. Select All. Press Ctrl+Shift+C for Copy Merged.
5. Go to the specimen. Press Ctrl+V. Scale the pasted layer with the Transform tool so it is 2000 px wide.
6. Hold Shift to keep the shape.
7. Do the same with font-caps.kra. Place the capitals above the small letters.
   You should see: both alphabets in neat rows.
8. Copy the numbers from font-extra.kra. Place them below.
9. Write a real sentence with your glyphs. Use copies and your kerning values from lesson 044.
10. Write a line with a different real sentence. Do not reuse the same test word.
11. Add a small block of facts with the Text tool: stroke weight 50, x-height 250, cap height 350.
12. Look at the sheet. Are the alphabets and the sentence balanced on the page?
13. Fix the spacing of two glyphs in the sentence.
14. Press Ctrl+S to save. Choose File > Export and save a PNG copy to ~/Art/Lettering/45-font-specimen.png.

## Check your work
- Does the sheet show a name, an alphabet, numbers and a real sentence?
- Are the letters in the sentence spaced evenly?
- Is the sheet balanced, with clear groups?

## Make it yours
- Make a second specimen with a color.
- Make a poster using your own font.

## New words
- **specimen** — a sheet that shows a font
- **real text** — words that carry a meaning, not random letters
- **font** — the full set of letters, numbers and signs in one design
