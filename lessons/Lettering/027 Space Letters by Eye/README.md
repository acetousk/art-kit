# 027. Space Letters by Eye

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/27-optical-spacing.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 011
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF page 10 (left half); Brown, PDF pages 30 and 33; Johnston, PDF page 81

## The idea
Speedball says the space between letters is not a math problem. Measuring equal gaps gives uneven results. Straight stems and round letters need different gaps. Johnston says two curved letters should be very near each other, and two straight strokes well apart. Brown says I needs more space than the wide letters. The goal is the same area of white.

> "spaces optically equalized" (Speedball, PDF p. 10, left half)

## Look
Speedball page 10, left half: see the two rows MINNETWATA FALLS and POODLE DOG CLOWN. Each pair shows one row spaced by measure and one spaced by eye. Read the small captions. Brown page 33: read about the letter I.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open lettering-sheet.kra and Save As 27-optical-spacing.kra.
2. Add a paint layer named Block. Pick the Rectangle tool. Set Fill to Foreground color and Outline to No Outline.
3. Build the letters L, I, H, O, D from rectangles and ellipses. Make each 8 cells tall.
4. Keep each on its own layer.
5. Set the Move tool. In its Tool Options, set Shortcut move distance to 5 px.
6. Row 1: place the five letters with exactly 80 px of space between the edges. This is mechanical spacing.
   You should see: gaps that look uneven even though they measure the same.
7. Look at L and D. Is the white space after the L larger than between H and O?
8. Row 2: duplicate the layers. Move each letter by hand until the white looks equal.
9. Move H and O close together, but not touching. Leave more space around I.
10. Compare row 1 and row 2. Which reads more easily?
11. Write on the canvas: which pairs needed more space, which less?
12. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/27-optical-spacing.kra.

## Check your work
- Did equal measured gaps look uneven?
- Did the straight-to-round pair need less space than straight-to-straight?
- Did the corrected row look more even?

## Make it yours
- Try the word MINNETWATA with the same method.
- Try spacing the word LOOP.

## New words
- **optical spacing** — spacing by how it looks, not by measuring
- **mechanical spacing** — spacing that is equal by measurement
- **pair** — two letters next to each other
