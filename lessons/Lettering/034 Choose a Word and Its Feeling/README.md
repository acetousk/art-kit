# 034. Choose a Word and Its Feeling

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/34-word-feelings.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 033
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 5, 8, 10 and 33; Speedball, PDF page 9 (left half); Brown, PDF page 231

## The idea
FontForge says the most important thing about a type design is the feeling it gives. The feeling comes from choices: the weight, the width, the slant, the contrast, the round or sharp corners. Brown says a letterer who can only draw one style is limited. Speedball's Display plate shows a decorated style where trimming adds the feeling. Pick a word and match its letters to its meaning.

> "The most important thing about a type design is the feelings it evokes." (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 33)

## Look
FontForge page 8: see the narrow and wide letters and what each feels like. Page 10: see letters with and without contrast. Speedball page 9, left half: see how the ornaments change the feel of the same letters.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a 3200 by 2000 px image. Save it as 34-word-feelings.kra.
2. Pick three words: for example STORM, SOFT and SPEED. Write them on a Notes layer with the Text tool.
3. Under each word, write three feeling words, such as heavy, sharp, fast.
4. Make a grid of 3 columns on a Guides layer: one column for each word.
5. For STORM, letter the word in heavy block letters. Make the corners sharp.
6. For SOFT, letter the word in thin monoline round letters. Make the corners round.
7. For SPEED, letter the word in italic capitals, slanted and narrow.
   You should see: three words that each look like their meaning.
8. Make a second try for each word with the opposite style. Does the meaning break?
9. Circle the best version of each word.
10. Write one line on the canvas: which choice mattered most, weight, width, slant or corners?
11. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/34-word-feelings.kra.

## Check your work
- Does each best version match the meaning of its word?
- Did the opposite style change the feeling?
- Can you name the choice that mattered most?

## Make it yours
- Try the words QUIET, FIERCE and DREAM.
- Choose a word from your favorite song.

## New words
- **feeling** — the mood a style of lettering gives
- **weight** — how heavy or light the strokes look
- **width** — how wide or narrow the letters are
