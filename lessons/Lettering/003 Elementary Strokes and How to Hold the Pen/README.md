# 003. Elementary Strokes and How to Hold the Pen

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/03-strokes.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 002
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF page 3 (right half); Speedball, PDF page 6 (left half)

## The idea
Speedball says every letter is built from a few simple strokes. Each stroke is made in one move. The numbers on the plate show the order and direction. Practice rows of strokes first. Grip the pen lightly and move your whole arm.

> "each and every element must be produced with a single stroke of the lettering pen or brush." (Speedball, PDF p. 3, right half)

## Look
Page 3, right half: read the row Elementary Strokes, Roman letters. Count the nine strokes and follow the numbers. Page 6, left half, bottom: see the practice rows IIII, HHHH, EEEE, NMNM, VAVA, OOOO, SSSS.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open 02-five-lines.kra and Save As 03-strokes.kra.
2. Select Guides. Hide the old Letters layer by clicking its eye icon.
3. Add a paint layer named Strokes. Pick Ink-2 Fineliner, size 14 px.
4. Hold the stylus lightly, like a pencil. Move from the shoulder, not just the fingers.
5. Row 1: draw ten vertical strokes, top to bottom, between line 1 and line 4. Keep the spacing even.
6. Row 2: draw ten horizontal strokes, left to right. Keep each stroke the same length.
7. Row 3: draw H H H H H. Draw the two stems first, then the bar.
8. Row 4: draw E E E E E. Draw the stem first, then three bars from the left.
9. Row 5: draw N M N M, then V A V A, then W X W X.
10. Row 6: draw O O O O in one stroke each, then S S S S.
   You should see: rows of letters that look like one family.
11. Press Ctrl+Z to undo any stroke you do not like. Try again, a little slower.
12. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/03-strokes.kra.

## Check your work
- Is each vertical stroke the same height?
- Do your H letters have the bar in the same place each time?
- Did you draw each stroke in one smooth move?

## Make it yours
- Add a row of the strokes 4 and 5 from the plate (the flat bar and the curve).
- Time yourself for one row, then try to keep the same quality.

## New words
- **stroke** — one mark made by the pen in one move
- **stroke order** — the order and direction you draw the strokes of a letter
- **element** — one basic part that letters are built from
