# 032. Poster Layout With Balanced Groups

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/32-poster-layout.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 029
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF pages 14 (left half), 15 (right half) and 17 (right half)

## The idea
Speedball says a good layout is more than the lettering. Lines of lettering belong to a few groups. Each group is a block. The blocks need to balance on the optical center, with clear space between and around them. A bad layout has many blocks of the same size, or equal space everywhere. Plan on small sketches before you start the real work.

> "A Bad Layout" (Speedball, PDF p. 15, right half)

## Look
Speedball page 17, right half: see the card A Bad Layout Will Kill the Best of Lettering. Page 15, right half: see A Bad Layout and A Good Layout, and the layouts balanced with blocks. Page 14, left half: see the panels divided in two and three.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a 3200 by 2000 px image. Save it as 32-poster-layout.kra.
2. Show the grid at 40 px. Add a Guides layer.
3. Draw four poster frames, 700 by 1000 px each, side by side.
4. Mark a poster text: a title (BAKE SALE), a line of details (SAT 10 AM), and a place (OAK STREET).
5. Add a Blocks layer. Pick the Rectangle tool with Fill set to Foreground color.
6. Frame 1: stack the three blocks with equal gaps. Make all the blocks the same size.
7. Frame 2: make the title large and put it near the optical center.
8. Put the other two lines in small blocks.
9. Frame 3: group the details and the place together as one block. Leave a clear gap below the title.
10. Frame 4: use your best idea. Leave liberal margins around everything.
   You should see: one block clearly largest and the groups clearly apart.
11. Write a score from 1 to 5 under each frame with the Text tool.
12. Circle the best frame. Name why: size, space or balance?
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/32-poster-layout.kra.

## Check your work
- Is one group clearly the most important?
- Is there clear space between groups?
- Is the whole layout balanced on the optical center?

## Make it yours
- Make four layouts for a movie night poster.
- Try a layout with a diagonal title.

## New words
- **layout** — how the parts of a page are arranged
- **block** — a group of lines treated as one shape
- **liberal margin** — a wide border around the lettering
