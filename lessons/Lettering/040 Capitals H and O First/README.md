# 040. Capitals H and O First

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/font-caps.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 039
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 92, 93, 94 and 96

## The idea
FontForge says the first two capitals to design are H and O. They set the width and weight of the other capitals. The O tells you about C, G and Q. The H tells you about I, J and the left side of B, D, E, F, K, L, P and R. Capitals are often a bit wider than small letters and sometimes a bit heavier.

> "The first two letters to design are "H" and "O"." (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 92)

## Look
FontForge page 94: see how O guides C, G and Q, and how H guides I, J, B, D, E, F, K, L, P and R. Page 96: see how V helps Y, W and X.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open font-sheet.kra. Save As font-caps.kra. Hide the Labels layer.
2. Cells run A to F in row 1, then G to L, M to R and S to X.
3. Add a paint layer named Glyphs. Pick Ink-2 Fineliner at 50 px.
4. H is in row 2, second cell. Draw stems at x = 4 and x = 14.
5. Make each stem run from y = 3 to y = 15.
6. Add the bar from (4, 9) to (14, 9).
   You should see: a plain H as tall as the cap height line.
7. O is in row 3, third cell. Pick the Ellipse tool. Drag from (3, 3) to (15, 15).
8. Compare H and O. O should be wider than H.
9. Put H and O next to each other, then H O H O. Is O wide enough?
10. Draw I in row 2, third cell: a stem from (8, 3) to (8, 15).
11. Draw L, E and F with the same stem as H. Draw their bars at the same weight.
12. Check: do E and F bars line up with the H bar?
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/font-caps.kra.

## Check your work
- Is O wider than H?
- Do all letters use the same stroke weight?
- Do I, L, E and F use the same stem as H?

## Make it yours
- Make H wider and test with O.
- Try a capital height of 400 px.

## New words
- **cap height** — the height of capitals above the baseline
- **key letters** — letters that guide the shapes of others
- **weight** — how heavy the strokes look
