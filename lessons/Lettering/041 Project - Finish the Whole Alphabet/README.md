# 041. Project - Finish the Whole Alphabet

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/font-lower.kra  and  font-caps.kra  (add to)
**Builds on:** lessons 038 to 040
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 94, 108, 112, 113, 114 and 115

## The idea
Now finish all 52 letters. FontForge says to build from the parts you already have: clone, stretch, mirror and rotate. A d mirrored gives b. An n turned gives u. A group of letters shares one shape: the round family, the stem family, the arch family and the diagonal family. Save often. You can split this project across two sittings.

> "From this, you can easily derive the 'm', 'h', and 'u' by cloning, stretching, and rotating, respectively." (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 108)

## Look
FontForge pages 111 to 115: see g, f, t, e, v, w, x, y and z. Page 94 and 96: see the capital families. Keep the pages beside your work.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open font-lower.kra. Keep the 50 px stroke and the grid.
2. Round family: copy o for c, b, p, q and g. Open c by deleting a gap on the right.
3. Add stems for b, p and q. Add a hook for g.
4. Stem family: draw l, j, t and f. Give t a cross bar and f a short top curve.
5. Arch family: copy the n arch for m and r. Rotate n twice by 90 degrees for u.
6. Diagonal family: draw v, w, x, y, z and k with the Line tool. Let the points overshoot a little.
   You should see: 26 small letters in the sheet. Save with Ctrl+S.
7. Open font-caps.kra. Stem family: finish I, L, E, F, T and H with the H stems and bars.
8. Round family: copy O for C, G, Q and D. Add the cuts, bars and tails.
9. Diagonal family: draw A, V, W, X, Y, Z, K, M and N with the Line tool.
10. Mixed: draw B, P, R, S and J with stems, half circles and your s curve.
   You should see: 26 capitals in the sheet.
11. Hide Guides1 to Guides5. Look at both sheets. Which three letters look odd?
12. Fix those three letters. Press Ctrl+S in both files.

## Check your work
- Is every cell filled in both sheets?
- Do all glyphs have the same stroke weight?
- Do the round letters match o?

## Make it yours
- Write your name with the new font.
- Make a second version of one letter and keep the better one.

## New words
- **family of shapes** — letters that share one part, like round or diagonal
- **clone** — to copy a part and reuse it
- **mirror** — to flip a shape to make its opposite
