# 023. Project - Three Hands on One Card

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/23-three-hands-card.kra
**Builds on:** lessons 014, 020, 022
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Brown, PDF pages 229, 230 and 231; Speedball, PDF page 22 (right half)

## The idea
Brown urges the beginner to practice many styles. A letterer who knows one style is limited. Brown says you may mix styles if the result is harmonious. He also says Roman emphasis can be made by spacing letters farther apart. In this project, put Roman capitals, italic and script together on one card.

> "The beginner is urged, therefore, to practice the use of many styles" (Brown, PDF p. 231)

## Look
Brown page 230: read the paragraph about emphasis by spacing. Page 231: read the end of the book. Speedball page 22, right half: see how script and Roman capitals appear together.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a new image, 2400 by 1600 px. Save it as 23-three-hands-card.kra.
2. Turn on the grid at 40 px. Add a paint layer named Guides.
3. Draw a border 4 cells in from each edge.
4. Pick a short name or message, such as a friend's name and a wish.
5. Plan: line 1 in Roman capitals (spaced wide), line 2 in script, line 3 in italic small letters.
6. Add a Sketch layer. Sketch the three lines with Pencil-2. Place line 2 in the middle, larger than the others.
7. Add an Ink layer. Ink the Roman capitals with thick and thin strokes and serifs.
8. Ink the script line with a free arm. Join the letters.
9. Ink the italic line on slanted guides. Keep the slant even.
10. Add one flourish under the script line.
11. Hide Sketch and Guides. Check: do all three hands work together?
12. Fix the weakest word. Then press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/23-three-hands-card.kra.

## Check your work
- Are the three lines clearly different styles?
- Is the main word the largest or boldest?
- Is the card balanced with even margins?

## Make it yours
- Make a birthday card for a friend.
- Make a title card for a school project.

## New words
- **harmony** — parts that look right together
- **hand** — a style of lettering
- **emphasis** — making one word stand out
