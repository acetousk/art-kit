# 022. Script Capitals and Flourish

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/22-script-capitals.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 021
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF pages 16 (right half), 17 (left half) and 22 (right half); Brown, PDF pages 215 and 219

## The idea
Script capitals are bigger and freer than small letters. Speedball's plates show them in groups with the same shape. The plate says to use a free, full-arm movement and do not lean on the pen. The Round Hand Script Alphabet plate shows how capitals meet small letters. Brown's examples show more flourish.

> "Do not try to work too fast—use a free, full-arm movement and do not lean on your pen." (Speedball, PDF p. 16, right half)

## Look
Speedball page 16, right half: see the rows of capitals grouped by shape. Page 17, left half: see the groups A N M, P B R and H K S L. Page 22, right half: see the full alphabet and the words Washington State National Bank.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open 21-round-hand.kra and Save As 22-script-capitals.kra.
2. Hide Script. Draw a capital band on Guides at y = 160 and 480. Keep the Slant layer.
3. Add a paint layer named Caps. Pick Ink-2 Fineliner at 12 px.
4. Warm up: draw big ovals and loops with a full arm swing. Do not lean on the stylus.
5. Draw the capitals in groups: A N M, then P B R, then H K L S.
6. Draw C E G O and D V W U.
   You should see: the capitals share one slant and one oval shape.
7. Write your first name with a script capital and small letters.
8. Add one flourish from the first capital under the name.
9. Write a place name, such as Washington, in script.
10. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/22-script-capitals.kra.

## Check your work
- Do all capitals share the same slant?
- Does the capital meet the small letter smoothly?
- Does your flourish pass under the name without hiding letters?

## Make it yours
- Write the name of your town in script.
- Design a monogram from two initials.

## New words
- **flourish** — a loose decorative curve added to a letter
- **monogram** — two or more letters joined in one design
- **oval** — a stretched circle shape
