# 008. Monoline Lowercase Letters and Numbers

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/08-monoline-lowercase.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 007
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Speedball, PDF page 7 (right half); Speedball, PDF page 9 (right half); Speedball, PDF page 20 (left half)

## The idea
Small letters live in three zones. The body sits between the top line and the base line. Ascenders go above. Descenders go below. We call the body height the x-height. In this course the x-height is 4 cells, the ascender adds 4 cells, and the descender goes down 3 cells. Build most letters from o and a stem.

> "With this method of alignment a child can produce good lettering" (Speedball, PDF p. 9, right half)

## Look
Page 9, right half, bottom: see the row aceimnorsuvwxz between the lines. Page 20, left half: see the Beginner's Speedball Alphabet with numbers and the construction lines.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open lettering-sheet.kra and Save As 08-monoline-lowercase.kra.
2. On Guides, draw band 1: y = 80, 240, 400, 520. These are ascender, x-height, base and descender lines.
3. Draw band 2 at y = 680, 840, 1000, 1120. Add a paint layer named Small.
4. Use Ink-2 Fineliner at 10 px.
5. Band 1: draw a b c d e f g h i j k l m.
6. Make o-shapes with the Ellipse tool.
7. Band 2: draw n o p q r s t u v w x y z.
   You should see: ascenders reach line 1 and descenders reach line 4.
8. Use the same circle for a, b, c, d, e, g, o, p, q.
9. Use the same stem for b, d, h, k, l, p, q.
10. Add numbers 0 to 9 in a row between y = 1240 and y = 1560.
11. Compare band 1 with band 2. Are the x-heights the same?
12. Fix any letter that sits too high or low. Use Ctrl+Z and draw it again.
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/08-monoline-lowercase.kra.

## Check your work
- Do all body letters stop at the x-height line?
- Do b, d, h, k, l reach the ascender line?
- Do g, j, p, q, y reach the descender line?

## Make it yours
- Write the word handwriting in monoline.
- Make the o wider. Does it still match the n?

## New words
- **x-height** — the height of the small letter x, the body of small letters
- **body** — the main part of a small letter, between base and top lines
- **numeral** — a number sign, like 4 or 7
