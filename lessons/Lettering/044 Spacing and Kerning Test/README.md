# 044. Spacing and Kerning Test

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/44-spacing-test.kra
**Builds on:** lessons 041 and 042
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 88, 138, 139, 140, 141 and 142

## The idea
FontForge says space between glyphs is part of the design. Each glyph has space on its left and right, called side bearings. The bottom line is to design, look, adjust, look again. Space your glyphs against strings of n and o. Kerning is a correction for one pair, such as To or Va. The word space matters too.

> "The bottom line is to 'design, look, adjust, look again'." (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 139)

## Look
FontForge page 139: see the H and O that measure equal but look unequal. Page 141: see the strings Hooooo and naxna. Page 142: see To and Va before and after kerning.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a 4000 by 1200 px image. Save it as 44-spacing-test.kra.
2. Open font-lower.kra. Select the Glyphs layer. Rectangular Select the n cell. Press Ctrl+C.
3. Go to the test file. Press Ctrl+V. Copy and paste o the same way.
   You should see: each paste is a new layer.
4. Choose the Move tool. Set Shortcut move distance to 5 px in Tool Options. Move layers with arrow keys.
5. Test string 1: n n o n n o o n o. Move each glyph until the white looks even.
   You should see: a smooth, even rhythm.
6. Write the gap you chose for n-n and for o-o. Round pairs should need less gap.
7. Test string 2: H O O H. Space them by eye.
8. Open font-caps.kra. Copy and paste T, o, V and a. Place To and Va in a row.
9. Find a gap that is too big. Move o closer to T until it looks right. This is your kerning.
10. Do the same for Va.
11. Write the pairs and values on a Notes layer with the Text tool.
12. Test the word space. Write two words with a gap as wide as your o. Move if needed.
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/44-spacing-test.kra.

## Check your work
- Do equal measured gaps look uneven for H and O?
- Did To and Va need less space than other pairs?
- Is your word space about as wide as o?

## Make it yours
- Test the pairs AV, WA and LT.
- Test the word hello at a very small size.

## New words
- **side bearing** — the space on the left or right of a glyph
- **kerning** — extra change of space for one pair of letters
- **rhythm** — the even beat of stems and spaces
