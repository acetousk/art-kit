# 039. Type DNA, a, d, h, e, s, i

**You make:** ~/Art/Lettering/font-lower.kra  (add to)
**Builds on:** lesson 038
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Design With FontForge, PDF pages 90, 91, 108, 109 and 110

## The idea
FontForge says to resist the urge to draw all the letters at once. First pick a few letters that carry the design. The set adhesion has enough DNA. It has round, stem, arch and diagonal parts. FontForge builds d from the stem of h plus o, and i from the stem of n. Make each new letter from parts you already drew.

> "It may be tempting to rush and populate your font as rapidly as possible with all the letters — resist this urge!" (Design With FontForge, PDF p. 90)

## Look
FontForge page 90: read the list of letters and why each is chosen. Page 108: see the d made from h and o. Page 110: see the small change that fixes the join.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open font-lower.kra. Keep the Glyphs layer and the 50 px size.
2. i is in row 2, third cell. Draw a stem from (4, 7) to (4, 15).
3. Click once at (4, 3) for the dot of i.
4. h is in row 2, second cell. Draw a stem from (4, 2) to (4, 15). Add the n arch.
   You should see: h looks like a taller n.
5. d is in row 1, fourth cell. Draw an o from (4, 7) to (12, 15).
6. Add a stem to d from (12, 2) to (12, 15).
7. Draw a in cell 1: an o and a stem from (12, 7) to (12, 15).
8. e is in row 1, fifth cell. Draw an o and a bar from (4, 11) to (12, 11).
9. Select the rectangle (11, 12) to (14, 14). Press Delete to open the lower right of e.
10. s is in row 4, first cell. Draw one stroke from (11, 8) up through (8, 7) to (5, 9).
11. Continue across to (11, 13), then down through (8, 15) to (5, 14).
12. Copy the six letters into a line: a d h e s i o n. Fix any odd weight.
13. Press Ctrl+S to save to ~/Art/Lettering/font-lower.kra.

## Check your work
- Do a and d share the same o shape?
- Is the stem of h the same height as the stem of d?
- Do all letters have the same stroke weight?

## Make it yours
- Write the word design with your letters.
- Redraw s two more times. Choose the best.

## New words
- **type DNA** — the few letters that carry the design of the rest
- **derive** — to make one letter from the parts of another
- **terminal** — the end of a stroke
