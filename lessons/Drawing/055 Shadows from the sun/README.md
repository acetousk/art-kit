# 055. Shadows from the sun

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/055-sun-shadows.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 054  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 184, 187, 190

## The idea
When the sun is at the side, its rays are parallel. A wall's shadow on level ground is drawn by sloping rays over the top. When the sun is ahead of you, shadows spread out from a point on the horizon. That point is directly under the sun. Build the shadow from rays and shadow lines. "The vanishing point for the shadows will be immediately under the sun on the horizon line." (Vicat Cole, PDF 187)

## Look
PDF 184, Fig. 303: the shadow of a wall with the sun at the side. PDF 187, Fig. 307: shadows with the sun ahead. PDF 190, Fig. 313: the pseudo-sun when the sun is behind you.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 4000 by 2600 px. Add a horizon guide and mark the main point P.
2. Part A. Draw a tall wall that recedes to P. Mark A and B at its base.
3. Draw a horizontal line to the right from A and from B. These are the shadow lines on the ground.
4. Draw one sloping ray from the near top corner. Make it meet the line from A.
5. Draw a parallel ray from the far top corner. Make it meet the line from B.
6. Join the two ends. Fill the shadow with a gray tone.
   You should see: The wall's shadow on the ground.
7. Part B. Draw a box on the ground. Put the sun above the horizon.
8. Drop a vertical from the sun to the horizon. Mark that point S.
9. Mark the ground under each top corner of the box.
10. Draw a line from S through each mark. Draw a ray from the sun over each top corner.
11. Mark where each ray meets its line. Join those points to complete the shadow.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/055-sun-shadows.kra.

## Check your work
- With the sun at the side, are the rays parallel?
- Does the shadow point S sit directly under the sun?
- Are the shadow ends where a ray meets a shadow line?

## Make it yours
- Do the box again with the sun lower. See the shadow lengthen.
- Draw a fence and its shadow.

## New words
- **ray** — a line of light from the sun.
- **shadow point** — the point where shadow lines seem to meet.
- **cast shadow** — the shadow an object throws on a surface.
