# 013. Block in with ovals and silhouettes

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/013-block-in.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 012  
**Time:** 40 min  
**Read:** Line and Form (Crane), PDF pages 28–30

## The idea
Start a drawing with big masses. Crane blocks in the main parts with ovals. Then he draws the careful outline on top. Next, fill the shape with solid black to see the silhouette. Good outline means good sense of mass. "good outline means good perception of mass" (Crane, PDF 30)

## Look
PDF 28: the method of blocking in masses with a series of ovals. PDF 30: how to fill a form with black silhouette, and why the outline goes outside or inside the edge.

## Steps
1. Pick an object with two or three main parts. A pear, a bird toy, or a watering can will work.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 2000 by 1500 px.
3. Open the Layers docker. Make a new layer. Name it ovals.
4. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset. Draw two or three ovals for the main masses.
5. Make a new layer. Name it outline.
6. Draw the careful outline over the ovals. Correct as you go.
7. Hide the ovals layer. Click its eye icon.
8. Make a new layer. Name it silhouette.
9. Pick a large brush. Fill the whole shape with solid black.
10. Hide the other layers. Look only at the silhouette.
11. Show the outline layer. Compare it with the silhouette.
   You should see: The outline follows the edge of the silhouette.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/013-block-in.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you block in the big masses first?
- Does the silhouette show the shape clearly?
- Does your outline match the edge of the silhouette?

## Make it yours
- Block in a seated cat with ovals.
- Block in a bicycle.

## New words
- **mass** — a big shape that holds the form.
- **silhouette** — a solid dark shape with no details inside.
- **block in** — draw the big shapes before the details.
