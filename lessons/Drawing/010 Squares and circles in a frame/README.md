# 010. Squares and circles in a frame

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/010-squares-circles.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 008  
**Time:** 40 min  
**Read:** Composition (Dow), PDF pages 43–46

## The idea
Dow says to cut a square or a circle into smaller spaces with lines. The edge cannot change, so you must focus on the lines inside. Make many trial layouts and pick the best. Use lines of equal thickness and no ruler. "Make many trial arrangements" (Dow, PDF 43)

## Look
PDF 44: composition in squares and circles by Della Robbia and a Pompeii tomb relief. PDF 46: units for wood-block printing and stencil work. PDF 45: Dow's three exercises.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2000 px.
2. Pick the Rectangle Tool. Hold Shift. Drag to draw a square. Make six squares in a grid.
3. Pick the Ellipse Tool. Hold Shift. Drag to draw two circles on the right.
4. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and the Ink Fineliner preset.
5. In each square, draw two to four lines that split it into spaces. Use no ruler.
   You should see: Each square is broken into different areas.
6. Do the same in the two circles. You may use curves.
7. Look at all eight. Which has the nicest spacing? Which has the dullest?
8. Make a new layer. Name it final.
9. Draw a big square at the right. Copy your best idea into it. Draw slowly and boldly.
10. If a line goes wrong, press Ctrl + Z. Draw it again.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/010-squares-circles.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you try many layouts before you chose?
- Are all your lines the same thickness?
- Does each space have a reason to be there?

## Make it yours
- Make a stained glass window design from one square.
- Divide a circle like a pie. Then change the angle of the cuts.

## New words
- **composition** — how you arrange shapes and spaces on a page.
- **space cutting** — dividing an area with lines.
- **harmony** — parts that fit well together.
