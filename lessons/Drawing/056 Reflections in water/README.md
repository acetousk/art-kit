# 056. Reflections in water

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/056-reflections.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 055  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 172–173, 175

## The idea
A reflection mirrors the object below the water line. Drop a vertical line from each point. Measure the same distance below the water as the point is above it. Join the points as they join on the object. Level lines in the reflection go to the same vanishing point as the original lines. "The reflection of each point will be on a vertical line dropped from that point." (Vicat Cole, PDF 172)

## Look
PDF 172, Figs. 281–283: a stick and a post with their reflections. PDF 173, Figs. 284–286: leaning posts. PDF 175, Illus. LXI: a drawing of a punt with its reflection.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 4000 by 2600 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Draw a flat water line across the canvas, below the horizon.
3. Draw an upright stick that stands in the water. Mark point 1 where it meets the water.
4. Mark the top of the stick as 2. Use the Measure Tool to find the height from 1 to 2.
5. Drop a vertical from the top. Measure the same distance below the water. Mark point 3.
6. Join 3 to 1.
   You should see: The reflection is the same length as the stick, below the water.
7. Draw a post with a flat top. Mark its near and far water points.
8. Reflect each top corner on a vertical line. Measure the same distance below.
9. Check: do the level lines in the reflection go to the same vanishing point?
10. Draw a post that leans. Guess where a pebble dropped from its top would hit the water.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/056-reflections.kra.

## Check your work
- Is each reflection point on a vertical line from its object point?
- Is each point as far below the water as above it?
- Do the level lines go to the same vanishing point?

## Make it yours
- Draw a bridge with its arch reflected.
- Draw a boat with its reflection.

## New words
- **reflection** — an upside-down image in water.
- **water line** — where the water meets an object.
- **vertical** — straight up and down.
