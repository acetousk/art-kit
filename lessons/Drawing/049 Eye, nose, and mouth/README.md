# 049. Eye, nose, and mouth

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/049-features.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 048  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 132, 135, 140, 141, 146, 149

## The idea
Features are also built from wedges. The nose is a row of wedges on a bony base. The eye has three planes around the eyeball. The upper lid covers part of the eye. The mouth has a flat upper lip and a rounder lower lip. "The nose is made of a series of wedges based on its bony structure." (Bridgman, PDF 140)

## Look
PDF 135: eyes in many views. PDF 141: noses in many views. PDF 149: mouths in many views.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 3200 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
2. Draw the eyeball as a sphere. Draw the upper lid over it. The lid covers part of the eye.
3. Draw the three planes around the eye: the bridge, the brow, and the cheek.
4. Draw the nose as three wedges. Add the bulb at the tip and the two wings.
   You should see: A nose built from wedges.
5. Draw the mouth. Make the upper lip flat and angular. Make the lower lip rounder.
6. Draw the groove between the nose and the upper lip.
7. Draw the three features in side view.
8. Add a tone on the shade side of each feature.
9. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/049-features.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the nose made of wedges?
- Does the upper lid cover part of the eye?
- Is the lower lip rounder than the upper lip?

## Make it yours
- Draw eyes that look left and right.
- Draw a smiling mouth.

## New words
- **wedge** — a shape that tapers from thick to thin.
- **eyeball** — the round part of the eye.
- **lid** — the skin that covers the eye.
