# 024. Equal posts going into depth

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/024-posts.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 023  
**Time:** 40 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 42–43

## The idea
Posts in a row look smaller and closer as they go away. How do you place each post? Use a diagonal. A diagonal across a rectangle passes through its center. Draw lines from the top, bottom, and middle of post 1 to the vanishing point. Then find each next post step by step. "to find the centre of a gate or any other rectangular form we draw diagonal lines from corner to corner" (Vicat Cole, PDF 42)

## Look
PDF 42, Figs. 51–58: a gate and how to add a new half by a diagonal. PDF 43, Fig. 59: a row of posts found with diagonals.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2000 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Pick the Assistant Tool. Add a Vanishing Point on the horizon, near the middle.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset. Draw post 1 at the left. Make it tall.
4. Draw lines from the top, bottom, and middle of post 1 to the vanishing point.
5. Draw post 2 a little farther in. Place it by eye.
6. Draw a diagonal from the top of post 1 through the middle of post 2.
   You should see: The diagonal reaches the ground line. This is the place for post 3.
7. Draw post 3 there. Draw a diagonal from the top of post 2 through the middle of post 3.
8. Keep going for six posts.
   You should see: Each post is shorter and closer than the one before.
9. Join the tops with one line. Join the bottoms with one line.
10. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/024-posts.kra.

## Check your work
- Do all tops meet at the vanishing point?
- Did you use a diagonal to place each next post?
- Does each gap look smaller than the one before?

## Make it yours
- Draw a row of street lamps.
- Draw an avenue of trees.

## New words
- **diagonal** — a line from one corner to the opposite corner.
- **receding scale** — lines to the vanishing point that show how heights shrink.
- **row** — a line of things placed in order.
