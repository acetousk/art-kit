# 047. The head as a block

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/047-head-block.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 028 and 042  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 108, 116–117, 119, 129

## The idea
Bridgman builds the head from a block. The front and back are oblong. The sides are square. A line down the middle shows both sides are alike. Add the neck as a round column. "On the ground plan of a square, however, any form may be built." (Bridgman, PDF 116)

## Look
PDF 119: heads drawn as blocks. PDF 129: heads in side and three-quarter views, with block lines.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 3200 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
2. Draw an oblong, one and three-quarters times as tall as it is wide. This is the front of the block.
3. Draw the center line from top to bottom.
4. Draw the sides of the block as squares in perspective. Use the cube skills from lesson 028.
   You should see: A tall block seen at an angle.
5. Draw the neck as a round column under the block. It leans a little forward.
6. Draw the brow line, the base of the nose, and the chin line across the block.
7. Draw a second block turned to the side.
8. Mark where the ear sits on the side of the block.
9. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/047-head-block.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the front taller than it is wide?
- Does the center line split the front in half?
- Does the neck look round?

## Make it yours
- Draw a head looking up and down.
- Draw a head at three angles.

## New words
- **block** — a simple box shape used to build a form.
- **center line** — a line through the middle of a shape.
- **bilateral** — with two sides that match.
