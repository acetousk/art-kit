# 054. Steps and slopes

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/054-steps-slopes.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 027 and 031  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 32–33, 64–66

## The idea
Lines that slope up go to a point above the horizon. Lines that slope down go to a point below it. A flight of steps is a sloping plane. One sloping line touches the top corners of the steps. The other touches the inside corners. "we can represent the height and depth of all the steps in a flight, by using four such sloping lines" (Vicat Cole, PDF 64)

## Look
PDF 64, Fig. 116: steps seen from the side, between two sloping lines. PDF 66, Fig. 117: steps seen from the front, with the uphill point above the level point.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 4000 by 2600 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset. Draw one step from the side.
3. Draw a sloping line through the outer corners of the step. Extend it up and to the right.
4. Draw a second line through the inner corners. Make it parallel to the first.
5. Draw an upright between the lines for step 2. Draw the tread.
6. Repeat until you have six steps.
   You should see: Every step corner touches one of the two sloping lines.
7. Now draw steps from the front. Mark the main point P on the horizon.
8. Draw the first step as a front face. Draw its tread toward P.
9. Draw a sloping line through the corners of the left side. Extend it up to a vertical line through P.
10. Mark that meeting point U. This is the uphill point.
11. Draw the sloping line on the right side to U as well.
12. Add uprights between the sloping lines. Draw the treads toward P.
13. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/054-steps-slopes.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the step corners touch two parallel lines in the side view?
- In the front view, do the sloping lines meet above the level point?
- Do the treads run to the level point?

## Make it yours
- Draw a staircase going down.
- Draw a hill road with the uphill point.

## New words
- **uphill point** — where lines that slope up seem to meet.
- **riser** — the upright part of a step.
- **tread** — the flat part you step on.
