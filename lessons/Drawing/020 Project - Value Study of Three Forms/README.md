# 020. Project - Value Study of Three Forms

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/020-value-study.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 012 to 019  
**Time:** 55 min  
**Read:** Composition (Dow), PDF page 92; Line and Form (Crane), PDF pages 233, 240

## The idea
Now build one full study. You will draw a cube, a sphere, and a cylinder on a table. Light each from one side. Use a scale of five values and a tint of lines behind. Let the deepest shade run to black. "let one’s lines … run into solid blacks in the deepest interstices of the forms" (Crane, PDF 240)

## Look
Look at your files from lessons 012, 017, and 019. Use the same scale and the same light side.

## Steps
1. Place a box, a ball, and a can on a table. Put a lamp on one side.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2400 px.
3. Make a layer named sketch. Draw the three forms lightly. Add a table line.
4. Make a layer named values. Fill the background with the lightest gray.
5. Fill the lit planes of the forms with the lightest tones.
6. Fill the shade planes with middle grays.
7. Fill the cast shadows with a dark gray.
8. Add the deepest dark where forms meet the table.
   You should see: All three forms now look solid.
9. Make a layer named lines. Add a tint of horizontal lines behind the forms.
10. Add diagonal shade lines on one form.
11. Hide the sketch layer. Look at the whole.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/020-value-study.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the light on the same side for all three forms?
- Do you see at least four values?
- Do the cast shadows touch the objects?

## Make it yours
- Draw the same study with the lamp on the right.
- Do the study again with different objects.

## New words
- **study** — a drawing made to learn.
- **light source** — where the light comes from.
- **contrast** — a big difference between light and dark.
