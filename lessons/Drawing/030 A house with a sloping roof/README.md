# 030. A house with a sloping roof

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/030-house.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 029  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 87–90

## The idea
A simple house is a box with a roof on top. The roof end is a triangle. Its top point sits above the middle of the end wall. Find the middle with diagonals. Norton sets the eye level one fourth of the way up the wall. The slanting roof edges go to a point above the eye level. "the level of the eye should be one-fourth way up the height of its rectangular part" (Norton, PDF 88)

## Look
PDF 87, Fig. 109: the finished house. PDF 88, Figs. 111–112: the model and the construction. PDF 90, Fig. 114: books show how roof ends vanish upward.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2200 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Draw the nearest vertical edge of the house. Put the guide one fourth of the way up it.
3. Find VP1 and VP2 with the top and bottom edges. Draw the box of the house.
   You should see: A box with three faces: two walls and a top.
4. On the end wall, draw the diagonals. Find the center.
5. Draw a vertical line up from the center. Mark the roof peak a little above the top edge.
6. Draw the sloping roof edges from the peak to the corners of the wall.
7. Draw the ridge from the peak to VP2.
8. Find the other peak. Draw a vertical from the center of the far end to meet the ridge.
9. Draw the other sloping edges to complete the roof.
10. Add a door in the middle of the front wall. Draw it to VP1.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/030-house.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the eye level about one fourth up the wall?
- Does the roof peak sit over the middle of the end wall?
- Does the ridge go to a vanishing point on the horizon?

## Make it yours
- Add a chimney or a dormer.
- Draw a barn with a steeper roof.

## New words
- **gable** — the triangle of wall under the end of a sloping roof.
- **ridge** — the top line of a roof.
- **oblique** — slanting, not level and not vertical.
