# 007. Slow brush lines

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/007-brush-lines.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 006  
**Time:** 30 min  
**Read:** Composition (Dow), PDF pages 25–26, 45

## The idea
Dow teaches line with the Japanese brush. Draw slowly. Move the whole hand and arm in one sweep. Keep your eye on the line as it grows. Never erase an ink line. If you make a mistake, begin again. "Draw very slowly. Expressive line is not made by mere momentum, but by force of will controlling the hand." (Dow, PDF 26)

## Look
PDF 26, the exercise: start with straight lines, then curves, then irregular lines. PDF 28 shows lines by masters such as Michelangelo and Leonardo.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 2400 by 1600 px.
2. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool. Pick the Ink Sumi-e or Ink Brush Rough preset.
3. Set Smoothing to No Smoothing.
4. Draw ten straight lines. Go slowly. Watch the line grow.
   You should see: Straight in direction, but with life in the line.
5. Draw ten curved lines. Go slowly.
6. Draw ten irregular lines, like a mountain edge or a river bank.
7. If you make a mistake, press Ctrl + Z. Begin the line again.
8. Draw one wave line across the canvas. Try to make it even.
9. Pick your best line in each group. Put a dot next to it.
10. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/007-brush-lines.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you draw each line slowly?
- Did you move your whole arm?
- Did you begin again after a mistake?

## Make it yours
- Draw a bamboo stalk with five straight lines.
- Copy the curve of a hill or a wave.

## New words
- **expressive** — showing life or feeling.
- **momentum** — speed that carries your hand along.
- **sweep** — one smooth, long arm movement.
