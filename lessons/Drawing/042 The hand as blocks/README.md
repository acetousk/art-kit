# 042. The hand as blocks

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/042-hand-blocks.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 013  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 20–21, 38–39

## The idea
Draw the hand as simple blocks first. The hand has two masses. One is the palm. The other is the thumb. The wrist is narrower, with a step down from the arm. Mark the action side and the straight side. "The masses of the hand are two — one that of the hand proper, the other that of the thumb." (Bridgman, PDF 20)

## Look
PDF 39: hands drawn as blocks with straight edges. PDF 41: many hands in different turns. PDF 47: block masses next to the finished hands.

## Steps
1. Rest your left hand on the desk. Keep the book open next to Krita at PDF 39.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2400 px.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
4. Draw the palm as a flat block, a little wider at the knuckles. Use light lines.
5. Draw the wrist as a narrower block. Show a step down from the back of the arm.
6. Draw the thumb as a wedge that joins the palm at an angle.
7. Draw the four fingers as one block that tapers.
   You should see: A hand made of four blocks with no detail.
8. Mark the action side with a short line. This is the side with the greatest angle.
9. Draw two more hands in different turns. Use blocks only.
10. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/042-hand-blocks.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you draw the palm and the thumb as two masses?
- Is there a step-down at the wrist?
- Did you find the action side?

## Make it yours
- Draw your hand holding a cup, with blocks.
- Draw a hand in a fist as blocks.

## New words
- **mass** — a big shape that holds the form.
- **block** — a simple box shape used to build a form.
- **action side** — the side of a form with the greatest angle.
