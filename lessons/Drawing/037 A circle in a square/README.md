# 037. A circle in a square

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/037-circle-in-square.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 036  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 94, 96; Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 114–115

## The idea
It is hard to draw an ellipse by feel when it is tilted. First draw the square. A circle touches the square in the middle of each side. Add four more points near the corners, where the circle crosses the diagonals. Then draw the ellipse through the eight points. Krita can check your ellipse. "The greatest diameter of the ellipse so formed is slightly below the centre line of the square." (Vicat Cole, PDF 96)

## Look
Vicat Cole PDF 94, Figs. 154–157: the plan of a square with a circle, and the points on the diagonals. PDF 96, Fig. 159: the near edge with points 1 and 2. Norton PDF 114–115, Figs. 157–161: a circular frame in a square frame.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2400 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Draw a flat square in one point. Use a main point P and lines like lesson 025.
3. Draw the two diagonals. Mark the center where they cross.
4. Draw a line through the center parallel to the front edge. Draw another toward P.
5. Mark four points where these lines touch the sides of the square.
6. On the near edge, mark two points a little less than one sixth in from each end.
7. Draw lines from these two points to P. Mark where they cross the diagonals.
   You should see: Eight points around the center.
8. Draw the ellipse through all eight points. Make it a smooth curve.
9. Pick the Assistant Tool. Choose Perspective Ellipse. Click the four corners of the square.
10. Compare Krita's ellipse with yours.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/037-circle-in-square.kra.

## Check your work
- Does the ellipse touch the middle of each side?
- Is the widest part slightly below the center line?
- Is your ellipse close to the assistant's ellipse?

## Make it yours
- Draw a round table top.
- Draw a manhole cover in a road.

## New words
- **guiding points** — points you mark to help draw a curve.
- **diagonal** — a line from one corner to the opposite corner.
- **center line** — a line through the middle of a shape.
