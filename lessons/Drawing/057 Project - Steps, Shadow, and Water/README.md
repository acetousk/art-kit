# 057. Project - Steps, Shadow, and Water

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/057-steps-shadow-water.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 054 to 056  
**Time:** 60 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 31, 64–66, 172, 184

## The idea
Combine three ideas in one scene. Draw a stone landing with steps that go down to still water. Add a post. Show its shadow with the sun at the side. Show its reflection in the water. Use the rules where they help. "Rules are to be considered as fences placed only where trespass is expected." (Vicat Cole, PDF 31, quoting Reynolds)

## Look
Look at your files from lessons 054, 055, and 056. Keep the same horizon and the same sun side.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 4000 by 2600 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Draw a stone landing as a low box. Use two vanishing points on the horizon.
3. Draw a flight of steps going down from the landing to the water. Use two sloping lines.
   You should see: Steps that go down into the water.
4. Draw a water line across the picture.
5. Draw a post on the landing, at the left of the steps.
6. Draw the shadow of the post. The sun is on the left.
7. Draw the reflection of the post. Use the vertical drop and equal distance.
8. Draw the reflection of the lowest step.
9. Make a layer for tone. Fill the landing, steps, and sky with light tones.
10. Fill the shadow with a dark tone. Fill the water with a middle tone.
11. Draw a frame around the best part of the scene.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/057-steps-shadow-water.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the shadow and the sun side agree?
- Is the reflection as long as the post?
- Do the steps fit between two sloping lines?

## Make it yours
- Draw a boathouse by a lake.
- Draw a pier at sunset.

## New words
- **landing** — a flat place at the top of steps.
- **still water** — water with no waves.
- **scene** — a picture of a place.
