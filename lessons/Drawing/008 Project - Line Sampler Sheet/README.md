# 008. Project - Line Sampler Sheet

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/008-line-sampler.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 002 to 007  
**Time:** 50 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF page 20; Composition (Dow), PDF page 26; Line and Form (Crane), PDF pages 31, 37

## The idea
Now put your line skills on one sheet. You will make six panels. Each panel shows a different skill: level lines, vertical and slanted lines, ovals, feeling lines, bold and thin line, and slow brush lines. This sheet shows how well you control your pen. Keep it to compare with a later sheet. "Line is, indeed, as I have before termed it, a language" (Crane, PDF 37)

## Look
Look at your lessons 002 to 007 files. Find the best line in each one. Match it to a panel.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2000 px.
2. Use the Rectangle Tool to draw six equal boxes. Put three in each row.
3. Label the boxes: Level, Upright, Ovals, Feeling, Thick and Thin, Brush.
4. In Level, draw eight horizontal lines. Use a pencil preset.
5. In Upright, draw four vertical and four slanted lines.
6. In Ovals, draw six ovals from wide to tall. Draw each in one stroke.
7. In Feeling, draw one calm wave and one sharp zigzag.
8. In Thick and Thin, draw a leaf. Use Ink GPen and vary pressure.
9. In Brush, draw five slow curves with an Ink brush preset.
   You should see: All six boxes have clean, confident lines.
10. Look at your sheet. Circle one panel you like best. Circle one to practice more.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/008-line-sampler.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you use a different line type in each panel?
- Are your oval ends round and even?
- Did you pick one panel to practice more?

## Make it yours
- Do the same sheet again next month. Compare.
- Add a seventh panel with hatch lines.

## New words
- **sampler** — a sheet that shows many skills in small samples.
- **panel** — one box on a page.
- **control** — making your hand do what you want.
