# 053. Gesture - quick time studies

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/053-time-studies.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 052  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 42–43; Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 41, 45

## The idea
Norton asks for time studies. Choose a time, such as fifteen minutes. Draw freely and do not erase. Stop when the time ends. Then find the errors. Fix them in your next try, not on that drawing. "Instead, make those points right in your next attempt at the same object." (Norton, PDF 43)

## Look
Norton PDF 42, Fig. 40: a time study of a cylindrical object. Bridgman PDF 41 and 45: many quick hand studies.

## Steps
1. Rest your hand in a pose. Count slowly to sixty while you look.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2400 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
3. Draw the pose without erasing. Stop when you finish counting.
4. Change the pose. Count slowly to thirty, then draw again.
5. Draw four more poses. Keep each one quick.
   You should see: Six quick studies, each with lively lines.
6. Look at your first two-minute study. Write two errors beside it.
7. Draw the same first pose again for two minutes. Fix the two errors.
8. Compare the first and the second study.
9. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/053-time-studies.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you keep each study short?
- Did you avoid erasing?
- Did you fix the errors in a new drawing, not the old one?

## Make it yours
- Draw your pet asleep in five minutes.
- Draw a cup in two minutes, and then again.

## New words
- **time study** — a drawing made in a fixed time.
- **quick sketch** — a fast drawing with simple lines.
- **error** — a mistake in a drawing.
