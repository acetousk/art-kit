# 050. The foot

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/050-foot.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 045  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 202, 205, 207, 209

## The idea
The foot is built like the hand. The outside is the heel side. It lies flat on the ground and is lower. The inside is higher. The foot has arches that meet at the heel. The foot keeps flat on the ground in all positions. "the outside of the foot is the heel side" (Bridgman, PDF 202)

## Look
PDF 205: the foot, outer view. PDF 207: the foot, inner view. PDF 209: toes and their pads.

## Steps
1. Sit with a bare foot on the floor. Look at the outer side, then the inner side.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2400 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
3. Draw the foot from the outside as a long wedge. The heel is at the back.
4. Draw the ankle block above the heel.
5. Mark the arch on the inside. It is higher than the outside.
   You should see: A foot with a high inside arch and a flat outer side.
6. Draw five toes as small wedges. Make the big toe the biggest.
7. Draw the foot from the inside. Show the higher arch.
8. Draw the foot from the front. Show the toes as a curved row.
9. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/050-foot.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the outside of the foot lower than the inside?
- Does the foot rest flat on the ground?
- Are the toes shown as wedges?

## Make it yours
- Draw a foot in a shoe.
- Draw a foot lifting off the floor.

## New words
- **arch** — a curve that bends like a bridge.
- **heel** — the back part of the foot.
- **ankle** — where the foot joins the leg.
