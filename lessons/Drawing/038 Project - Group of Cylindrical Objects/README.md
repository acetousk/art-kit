# 038. Project - Group of Cylindrical Objects

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/038-cylinder-group.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 032 to 037  
**Time:** 60 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 44–46

## The idea
Make a group of two or three cylindrical objects. Try several arrangements in small sketches before you choose. Pick objects of different heights. Let one overlap another. Draw the farther objects with lighter lines. "The rendering of two things is more complicated and interesting than that of one alone." (Norton, PDF 46)

## Look
PDF 44, Figs. 41–43: a finder and thumbnail sketches. PDF 45, Fig. 44: a candlestick, a teapot, and a leaning bowl in one group. PDF 46: the teapot ears and the cover.

## Steps
1. Pick two or three objects: a mug, a can, and a bowl will do. Put them on a table.
2. Move the objects around. Try four different groups.
3. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 2400 px.
4. Draw four small frames with the Rectangle Tool. Draw a thumbnail of each group in each frame.
5. Pick the best thumbnail. Look for variety and overlap.
6. Draw a big frame. Mark the table line behind the objects.
7. Mark the top and bottom points of each object on the frame.
8. Draw the ellipses lightly. Keep the same eye level for all of them.
9. Draw the sides. Draw any handles with profile lines.
   You should see: A group where all ellipses follow one eye level.
10. Draw the final lines. Make near lines heavy and far lines light.
11. Add a cast shadow with a gray tone.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/038-cylinder-group.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the ellipses follow one eye level?
- Do the farther objects have lighter lines?
- Does one object overlap another?

## Make it yours
- Add a fourth object, like a pitcher.
- Draw the same group from a higher view.

## New words
- **group** — several objects drawn as one picture.
- **overlap** — one object covers part of another.
- **thumbnail** — a small quick sketch to test a layout.
