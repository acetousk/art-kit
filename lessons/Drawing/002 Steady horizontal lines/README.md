# 002. Steady horizontal lines

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/002-horizontals.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 001  
**Time:** 25 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 20–21

## The idea
Straight lines are the base of all drawing. Draw a horizontal line from left to right. Move your hand from the shoulder. Do not rest it on the wrist or elbow. If your arm feels tight, shake your hands loose. Norton says to sketch hard lines first with a thin, light line. "moving the hand freely from the shoulder, not resting it on the wrist or elbow." (Norton, PDF 20)

## Look
PDF 20, Fig. 5, position A: the hand holds the pencil for horizontal lines. PDF 21, Fig. 6: a table line is a long flat line behind the objects.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl + N and make a new document, 2000 by 1500 px.
2. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil brush preset. Set Smoothing to No Smoothing.
3. Draw a light horizontal line near the top. Go from left to right in one stroke.
4. Draw nine more lines below it. Space them evenly. Keep each one level.
   You should see: Ten lines in a column. Some wobble. That is fine.
5. Shake out your hands. Relax your shoulder.
6. Make a new column on the right. In the Tool Options docker, set Smoothing to Stabilizer.
7. Draw ten more lines. Compare this column with the first one.
8. Hold V and use the Straight Line Tool to draw one line at the bottom. This is your goal line.
   You should see: A perfectly straight line to compare with your hand lines.
9. Look at your two columns. Pick the 3 best lines in each. Why are they best?
10. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/002-horizontals.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you move from the shoulder, not the wrist?
- Did you draw each line in one stroke?
- Is the Stabilizer column straighter than the first column?

## Make it yours
- Draw a row of 10 lines with your other hand. Compare.
- Draw a line as slowly as you can. Then draw one fast. Which is straighter?

## New words
- **stroke** — one pen movement from touch down to lift up.
- **table line** — a flat line for the back edge of a table.
- **smoothing** — a Krita setting that steadies your line.
