# 029. A cube in two positions

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/029-cube-two-positions.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 028  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF pages 72–75

## The idea
Draw the cube turned at 45 degrees. Both sides look alike. Turn it so one side faces you more. One vanishing point moves in and one moves far out. Norton gives an order of lines for drawing. Use a ruler or assistant only to test. "such measuring is only for testing, never for drawing the lines" (Norton, PDF 75)

## Look
PDF 72, Figs. 85–86: the cube at 45 degrees and at 60 degrees. PDF 72, Fig. 87: numbers show the order of the lines. PDF 74, Fig. 92: the testing method.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 4000 by 2400 px. Add a horizon guide.
2. Draw a cube at 45 degrees. Follow the order from lesson 028. Use freehand lines.
3. Pick the Assistant Tool. In the Tool Options docker, choose 2 Point Perspective.
4. Click twice on the horizon, left and right. Click a third time between them, near the middle.
   You should see: A grid of two-point lines on the canvas.
5. Compare the grid with your cube. Where do they differ? Mark the errors.
6. Draw the cube again, at 60 degrees. Use the same eye level.
7. Move the left point closer to the middle. Move the right point farther out.
8. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool. Turn on Assistants in its Tool Options docker.
9. Draw the second cube. Your edges now snap to the assistant.
10. Check: the nearest vertical edges of both cubes have the same length. Use the Measure Tool.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/029-cube-two-positions.kra.

## Check your work
- Are the two cubes the same size?
- Do the edges of each cube meet on the horizon?
- Did you draw by eye first, then use the assistant to test?

## Make it yours
- Draw a cube at 30 degrees.
- Draw two cubes side by side at the same angle.

## New words
- **45 degrees** — an angle halfway between a side view and a front view.
- **center of vision** — the point on the horizon straight in front of you.
- **test** — check a drawing, not make it.
