# 051. Project - Head and Hand Study

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/051-head-hand-study.kra  
**Builds on:** lessons 042 to 050  
**Time:** 60 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 20–21, 61, 117, 128, 129

## The idea
Put head and hand together in one drawing. Build the head from a block and planes. Build the hand from blocks and wedges. Keep the same light on both. Use three values. Your own face in a mirror is a good model. "it will be found that the front and back are oblong and the sides are square" (Bridgman, PDF 117)

## Look
Look at your own files from lessons 047 to 049. Look at PDF 129 for head blocks.

## Steps
1. Sit near a mirror. Rest your hand under your chin. Put a lamp on one side.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3200 by 3200 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
3. Draw the head block in three-quarter view. Add the neck.
4. Draw the center line and the brow, nose, and chin lines.
5. Place the eyes, nose, and mouth with simple wedges.
   You should see: A head with simple features on a block.
6. Draw the hand as blocks. Rest it under the chin.
7. Draw the fingers as wedges. Draw the thumb.
8. Draw the forearm as a wedge.
9. Make a new layer. Fill the lit planes with a light tone.
10. Fill the shade planes with a middle gray. Add dark under the chin.
11. Hide the block lines. Look at the whole.
12. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/051-head-hand-study.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the head and hand have the same light?
- Is the hand built from blocks and wedges?
- Does the head look solid, not flat?

## Make it yours
- Draw a head with two hands.
- Draw the same pose from the other side.

## New words
- **study** — a drawing made to learn.
- **light source** — where the light comes from.
- **construction** — building a drawing from simple forms.
