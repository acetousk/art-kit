# 044. The thumb

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/044-thumb.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 043  
**Time:** 45 min  
**Read:** Constructive Anatomy (Bridgman), PDF pages 52–53, 59, 63

## The idea
The thumb leads the hand. Its base is a pyramid of muscle. The middle part is narrow. The end is pear shaped. The fingers form arches around the base of the thumb. This helps you draw a hand in a grip. "The mass of the thumb dominates the hand." (Bridgman, PDF 52)

## Look
PDF 59: studies of thumbs. PDF 63: the pad between the thumb and first finger. PDF 53 text: the thumb as three parts, the base, the middle, and the end.

## Steps
1. Make a thumbs-up shape with your hand. Look at it.
2. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2400 px. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset.
3. Draw the thumb as three blocks: a pyramid at the base, a narrow middle, and a pear-shaped end.
4. Mark the base of the thumb with a dot.
5. Draw three arcs around the dot. They go through the knuckles, the middle joints, and the fingertips.
   You should see: Three arcs that share one center.
6. Draw four fingers that follow these arcs.
7. Draw the web of skin between the thumb and first finger.
8. Draw a hand holding a ball. Put the center dot at the thumb base.
9. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/044-thumb.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the thumb's base a pyramid?
- Do the fingers curl around the thumb base?
- Does the thumb look strong?

## Make it yours
- Draw a hand gripping a bike handle.
- Draw a thumb pressing a button.

## New words
- **pyramid** — a form with a flat base and a point.
- **web** — the skin between the thumb and the first finger.
- **arch** — a curve that bends like a bridge.
