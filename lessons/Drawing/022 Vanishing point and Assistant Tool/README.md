# 022. Vanishing point and Assistant Tool

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/022-vanishing-point.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 021  
**Time:** 40 min  
**Read:** Perspective for Artists (Vicat Cole), PDF pages 20, 24, 31–32; Freehand Perspective and Sketching (Norton), PDF page 60

## The idea
Parallel lines that go away from you seem to meet at one point. This is the vanishing point. If the lines are level, the point is on the horizon. A railway track is the classic case. The rails are parallel, yet they look closer far away. Krita can show these lines with an assistant. "All Receding Level Lines in Nature appear (if continued) to end on the Horizon." (Vicat Cole, PDF 32)

## Look
Norton PDF 60, Fig. 66: a railroad track. Vicat Cole PDF 20, Figs. 18–19: the rails get closer and meet at the eye level. PDF 24, Fig. 28: aiming down a level gun barrel to the principal vanishing point.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2000 px.
2. Click View ‣ Show Rulers. Drag a guide from the top ruler to make a horizon in the middle.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a pencil preset. Make sure Smoothing is set to Stabilizer.
4. Draw two rails. Start wide apart at the bottom. Make them meet at one point on the horizon.
5. Draw ten railroad ties across the rails. Make each one shorter and closer than the last.
6. Pick the Assistant Tool. In the Tool Options docker, choose Vanishing Point.
7. Click on the horizon, at the point where your rails meet. Click again if the assistant is not complete.
   You should see: A point on the horizon with lines coming out of it.
8. Check your rails. Do they follow the assistant lines? Mark any that do not.
9. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool. Turn on Assistants in its Tool Options docker.
10. Draw a road with two edges. They now snap to the assistant.
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/022-vanishing-point.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the rails meet on the horizon?
- Do the ties get smaller and closer as they go away?
- Did you draw the rails before you turned on snapping?

## Make it yours
- Draw a hallway with doors that get smaller.
- Draw a long fence going away.

## New words
- **vanishing point** — the spot where parallel lines seem to meet.
- **receding** — going away from you.
- **assistant** — a Krita guide that helps you draw straight lines.
