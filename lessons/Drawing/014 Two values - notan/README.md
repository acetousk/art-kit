# 014. Two values - notan

**You make:** ~/Art/Drawing/014-notan-two-values.kra  
**Builds on:** lesson 010  
**Time:** 40 min  
**Read:** Composition (Dow), PDF pages 17, 19, 63–64

## The idea
Notan is a Japanese word that means dark, light. It is about how dark and light shapes sit together. With only black and white, you can make a pattern with beauty. Widen some lines in a pattern and a new beauty appears. This is dark and light. "The term NOTAN, a Japanese word meaning “dark, light”, refers to the quantity of light reflected, or the massing of tones of different values." (Dow, PDF 17)

## Look
PDF 19: a Venetian lace in two values. PDF 64: the exercise “Notan of Line,” where some lines are made wider.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document, 3000 by 2000 px.
2. Pick the Rectangle Tool. Hold Shift. Draw three squares.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and the Ink Fineliner preset.
4. In each square, draw a line pattern. Use straight or curved lines.
5. Pick a wide brush. Widen two or three lines in each pattern.
   You should see: Some lines are now thick and some thin.
6. Set the foreground color to black. Pick the Fill Tool. Spaces must be closed by lines.
7. Click spaces in the first square to fill them with black. Leave the rest white.
8. Fill different spaces in the second square. Try another black and white plan.
9. Fill the third square a third way.
10. Look at the three squares. Which is best balanced? Which has too much black?
11. Press Ctrl + Shift + S. Save as ~/Art/Drawing/014-notan-two-values.kra.

## Check your work
- Does each square use only black and white?
- Did you try three different plans?
- Can you say why one plan looks better?

## Make it yours
- Make a notan of a leaf and its shadow.
- Make a notan of a letter of the alphabet.

## New words
- **notan** — the pattern of dark and light shapes.
- **value** — how light or dark a color is.
- **balance** — dark and light spread in an even way.
