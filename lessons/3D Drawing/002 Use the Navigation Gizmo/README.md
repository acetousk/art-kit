# 002. Use the Navigation Gizmo

**You make:** no file. You move the view only.
**Builds on:** lesson 001
**Time:** 20 min
**Read:** Blender Manual, Navigation Gizmo; Viewpoint; Navigation

## The idea
The navigation gizmo lets you turn and move the view with the pen. You do not need the numpad. Quote: "The navigation gizmo can be found in the top right of the editor." The View menu has the same actions. Use the gizmo first, and use the View menu when you need an exact view.

## Look
The gizmo sits at the top right of the 3D Viewport. The round Orbit gizmo is at the top. Four buttons are under it: zoom, pan, camera view, and projection.

## Steps
1. Open Blender. Use the scene that opens first.
2. Find the navigation gizmo at the top right of the 3D Viewport.
3. Drag the round Orbit gizmo with the pen. The view turns.
4. Click one axis label on the gizmo. The view aligns to that axis.
5. Click the same axis label again. The view shows the other side.
6. Click the zoom button under the gizmo. Drag with the pen to zoom.
7. Click the pan button. Drag with the pen to move the view.
8. Click the Toggle Camera View button. Click it again to go back.
9. Click the Toggle Projection button. Click it again to switch back.
10. Open the View menu. Choose Frame All.
11. You should see: every object fits inside the 3D Viewport.

## Check your work
- Does a click on an axis label align the view?
- Can you zoom with the pen on the zoom button?
- Can you turn Camera View on and off?

## Make it yours
Pan the view to one corner of an empty space. Use the gizmo only. Note how many steps it took.

## New words
- **Gizmo** — a small control on screen that you drag or click.
- **Orbit** — turn the view around a point.
- **Projection** — the way 3D is shown flat: perspective or orthographic.
