# 010. Draw with Brush Strength and Pressure

**You make:** add to 009-materials.blend (save as 010-pressure.blend)
**Builds on:** lesson 009
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Blender Manual, Draw Brushes

## The idea
Strength sets how strong a brush stroke is. Pressure changes Strength with the pen. Quote: "Adjusts the strength based on the stylus pressure." Turn on the pressure icon to use the pen force. A light touch gives a thin, faint line. A hard touch gives a thick, strong line.

## Look
Look at the Strength setting in the Tool Settings. Look at the small pressure icon next to it. The icon turns on pressure for that setting.

## Steps
1. Open the file from lesson 009. Stay in Draw Mode.
2. Choose the Brush tool in the Toolbar.
3. In the Tool Settings, find the Strength value.
4. Click the pressure icon next to Strength. It turns on.
5. Draw one line with a light touch.
6. Draw one line with a firm touch.
7. Compare the two lines.
8. Press Ctrl-Z once. Then draw a line again.
9. You should see: the firm line is thicker or darker than the light line.
10. Save the file as ~/Art/3D Drawing/010-pressure.blend.

## Check your work
- Did the pressure icon turn on?
- Did a firm touch make a stronger line?
- Did you save the file?

## Make it yours
Draw a small bird. Use light pressure for the wings and firm pressure for the body.

## New words
- **Stylus** — the pen for a tablet.
- **Pressure** — how hard the pen touches the tablet.
- **Strength** — how strong a brush stroke is.
