# 053. Brand Sheet Colors Type Logo

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/053-brand-sheet.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 047 and 052
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 59,73; A Book of American Trade-Marks (Sinel 1924), PDF page 5; Krita Manual, Vector Layers; Krita Manual, Palette Docker; Krita Manual, Text Tool

## The idea
A brand sheet shows the rules in one place. It has the logo, a small set of colors, and two fonts. Hass says to build a palette focused enough to set the right mood but not too tied to one color. Use two fonts that contrast. Anyone who gets this sheet can make a matching poster.

> "Create a palette that is focused enough to create the right atmosphere and energy level for your project" (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 59)

## Look
Hass page 59: read the last paragraph on palettes. Page 73: read the sentence on pairing serif and sans serif. Sinel page 5: re-read about flexibility.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 2400 px at 150 ppi.
2. Draw your garden club logo as vector shapes from your chosen concept. Use lesson 046 as a guide.
3. Place the logo at the top left. Add the name next to it in a font.
4. Pick three colors: one dominant, one accent, one neutral. Use rules from lessons 026 and 027.
5. Make three swatches with the Rectangle Tool. Name each one with the Text Tool.
6. Add a tint of the dominant color as a fourth swatch.
   You should see a small, calm palette.
7. Pick two fonts that contrast, such as a serif for titles and a sans serif for text.
8. Write a sample title and a sample paragraph in them.
9. Show the logo on the dominant color, on white, and on dark.
10. Add three short rules, such as "Keep empty space around the logo".
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/053-brand-sheet.kra.

## Check your work
- Do your colors work on both screen and print?
- Do your two fonts contrast?
- Does the logo work on all three backgrounds?

## Make it yours
- Make a brand sheet for a made-up bakery.
- Add a fourth color and test if it earns its place.

## New words
- **brand** — the look and feel that make a group known
- **palette** — the set of colors you choose
- **swatch** — a small sample patch of color
