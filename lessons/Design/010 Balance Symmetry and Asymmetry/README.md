# 010. Balance Symmetry and Asymmetry

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/010-balance.kra and ~/Art/Design/010-balance-asymmetric.kra (new files)
**Builds on:** lesson 009
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 81,90; Composition (Dow 1913), PDF page 38; Krita Manual, Mirror Tools; Krita Manual, Shape Selection Tool

## The idea
A balanced page feels stable. Symmetry puts the same shapes on both sides of a center line. It feels calm and safe. Asymmetry balances unlike things, so it feels alive. Hass says large shapes can balance small ones, and strong colors can balance neutrals. Dow says a symmetric design is also an easy way to get order.

> "large shapes balance small ones; intense colours balance neutrals." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 81)

## Look
Page 81: read the two paragraphs on Balance. Page 90: read the Bilateral paragraph and how to make it less plain. Dow page 38: read the Symmetry paragraph.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Draw the first design half. Draw its matching half by eye across the center line.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool. Draw a leaf and a flower on the left side only.
   You should see the right side copy your strokes.
4. Turn off the mirror tool. Save as ~/Art/Design/010-balance.kra.
5. Make a new document, same size. This is the asymmetric one.
6. Draw one big dark circle near the left. Place it low.
7. Add a small bright red square on the right, high on the page.
   You should see the small bright shape balance the big dark one.
8. Add a thin tall line near the center. Move it with the Move Tool until the page feels steady.
9. Add one small text line in the lower right with the Text Tool.
10. Compare the two pages. Which feels calm? Which feels exciting?
11. Press Ctrl + S on the asymmetric page. Save as ~/Art/Design/010-balance-asymmetric.kra.

## Check your work
- Is your first page the same on both sides?
- Does the small bright shape balance the big dark one?
- Can you say which page suits a bank and which suits a skate shop?

## Make it yours
- Make a symmetric logo for a bakery.
- Make an asymmetric poster for a concert.

## New words
- **symmetry** — the same on both sides of a center line
- **asymmetry** — balanced but not the same on both sides
- **axis** — the center line of a design
