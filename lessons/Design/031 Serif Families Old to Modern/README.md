# 031. Serif Families Old to Modern

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/031-serif-families.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 030
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 64–67; Krita Manual, Text Tool; Krita Manual, Text Properties Docker; Krita Manual, Zoom Tool

## The idea
Serif letters have small feet. Hass sorts them by age. Humanist fonts from Venice are heavy and even. Old style fonts have more contrast and finer serifs. Transitional fonts have flat serifs and bigger contrast. Modern fonts, or Didones, push contrast to the limit.

> "Modern fonts are also known as Didones and take the contrast started by the transitional fonts much, much further" (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 67)

## Look
Page 64: read about Humanist type. Page 65: Old Style. Page 66: Transitional. Page 67: Modern. Find the example font names at the end of each part.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Open the Text Properties docker. Look at the Font Family list.
3. Find an old, calm font, such as Garamond or Palatino, if your list has them.
4. Pick the Text Tool. Type the word "Harvest" large in that font. This is your old style.
5. Type "Harvest" again in a font like Baskerville or Times. This is your transitional.
6. Type "Harvest" a third time in a font such as Bodoni or Didot.
7. If you have none, pick the font with the thinnest hairlines.
8. Pick the Zoom Tool. Zoom in on the letter "H" in each word.
   You should see thicker thick strokes and thinner thin strokes from the first to the third.
9. Pick the Straight Line Tool. Draw a line through the thin and thick parts of each "H".
10. Write under each word: "contrast low", "medium", or "high".
11. Look at the serifs. Write "curved feet" or "flat feet" or "hairline feet".
12. Choose one font for a poem book. Write why in a short line.
13. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/031-serif-families.kra.

## Check your work
- Does your third font have the highest contrast between thick and thin?
- Can you see how the serif feet change from word to word?
- Did you pick a font on purpose for the poem book?

## Make it yours
- Write your last name in each of the three fonts.
- Pick a font for a museum poster.

## New words
- **serif** — a small foot on the end of a letter stroke
- **contrast** — the gap between thick and thin strokes
- **x-height** — the height of the small letters
