# 029. Color Poster Project

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/029-color-poster.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 021 to 028
**Time:** 55 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel 1903), PDF pages 118–120; Krita Manual, Vector Layers; Krita Manual, Blending Modes; Krita Manual, Text Tool

## The idea
Plan a poster for a fall market. First choose one color scheme: one color, a pair, or a triad. Then use Vanderpoel's rules. Pick one dominant hue and one small bright accent. Use warm shapes in front and cool shapes behind. Write the scheme in a note on the file.

> "a small quantity of black will much improve the combination" (Color Problems, PDF page 120)

## Look
Pages 118 to 120: re-read the ways to change quality and quantity and to add black, white, gray, or gold. Plates XXXIX to XLIII follow on pages 237 to 245.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi. Save it as ~/Art/Design/029-color-poster.kra.
2. Choose a scheme and type it in a small text line, for example "dominant orange, accent blue".
3. Make a notan layout first. Use black, white, and gray for a pumpkin, a leaf, or a basket.
4. Add a Paint Layer above set to Multiply. Paint your dominant hue over the light areas.
5. Add one small accent in the complement. Put it at the focal point.
6. Make the back shapes cooler and the front shapes warmer.
   You should see depth in the picture.
7. Check any two colors that fight. Fix them by changing quality or quantity.
8. Add a thin black line or edge to one area to help the colors agree.
9. Add a Title, Subtitle, and text block with the Text Tool. Keep the type color from the scheme.
10. Squint. Is one color dominant? Is the accent small?
11. Delete the scheme line if you like, or keep it as a note.
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/029-color-poster.kra.

## Check your work
- Does one hue rule the poster?
- Is the accent small and at the focal point?
- Is the title easy to read on its color?

## Make it yours
- Make a second poster with the same layout but a different scheme.
- Make a version for a spring event.

## New words
- **scheme** — a plan for the colors you will use
- **accent** — a small bright touch
- **dominant** — strongest; the one that rules
