# 041. Poster Color and Strong Line

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/041-poster-color-line.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 040
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF pages 46–47,84; The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF page 264; Krita Manual, Bezier Curve Tool; Krita Manual, Fill Tool; Krita Manual, Layers

## The idea
A poster's color does not need to copy nature. A red lion is more striking than a gold one. Kauffer says color gives shapes the power to cause the right feeling. A strong outline is like cement that binds the shapes. Japanese prints taught poster artists few colors.

> "line in the poster is on the one hand a kind of cement for the design, strengthening its construction by binding all the shapes together" (The Art of the Poster, PDF page 47)

## Look
Page 84: read the four traits of Japanese prints. Page 264: see Kauffer's black, gray, and white Winter Sales poster. Page 47: read the paragraph on line.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick a scene of your town: a bus, a hill, a house.
3. Add a Vector Layer. Draw 4 to 6 big shapes for it with the Bezier Curve Tool.
4. Pick three flat colors and black. Do not use natural colors on purpose, such as a red sky.
5. Fill each shape with one color.
6. Add a thick black outline to the shapes. Use the Bezier Curve Tool with a size of 12 px.
   You should see the outline bind the shapes together.
7. Hide the outline layer. Look at the poster. Show it again.
   You should see the poster look finished with the outline.
8. Add a headline in heavy lettering. Match its color to the shapes.
9. Add one small text line. Keep it simple.
10. Squint. Is there one color that dominates?
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/041-poster-color-line.kra.

## Check your work
- Do you use only three colors and black?
- Does the outline help join the shapes?
- Does your headline match the design?

## Make it yours
- Redo the poster with a different set of three colors.
- Draw the same poster with no outline. Which is stronger?

## New words
- **outline** — a line around the edge of a shape
- **flat color** — one even color with no shading
- **palette** — the set of colors you choose
