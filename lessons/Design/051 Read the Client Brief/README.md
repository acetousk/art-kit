# 051. Read the Client Brief

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/051-client-brief.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 001 and 042
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 37–39; Krita Manual, Text Tool; Krita Manual, Reference Images Tool

## The idea
Now you work for a client. Hass says that a problem well stated is half solved. Start with questions about the client, the audience, the message, and the formats. Then do some research. Write the problem in one sentence. You will brand a school garden club.

> "A problem well-stated is half-solved." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 37)

## Look
Hass page 37: read the list of questions to ask a client. Page 38: read the research list. Page 39: read the six steps for stating a problem.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 2400 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Text Tool. Type the client name: Green Hands Garden Club.
3. Add: a school club for ages 10 to 14.
4. Add: they sell seedlings at a spring sale.
5. Add: they need a logo, colors, fonts, a sale poster, and a seed-packet sticker.
6. Add: the audience is neighbors, parents, and kids. The message is "Grow something".
7. Add: the budget is none. The approver is the club teacher.
8. Answer the questions of Hass page 37 in your own words. Write four short answers.
9. Do research. Write what three garden or farm logos you know look like. Note what works and what is dull.
10. Write the problem in one sentence, such as "We need a club to look friendly and easy to spot."
11. Turn the sentence into a question: "How do we make the club look friendly and easy to spot?"
12. Restate the question in three other ways. Write each in one line.
13. Pick the one that feels most useful. Circle it with the Ellipse Tool.
14. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/051-client-brief.kra.

## Check your work
- Can you say the message and the audience?
- Did you list what works and what is dull in other logos?
- Is your problem written as a clear question?

## Make it yours
- Write a brief for a real club at your school.
- Ask a friend to be the client and interview them.

## New words
- **client** — the person or group you design for
- **brief** — a short plan that states the job
- **research** — finding out facts before you design
