# 024. Warm Cool and Advancing Colors

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/024-warm-cool.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 023
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel 1903), PDF pages 53,59,169; Krita Manual, Ellipse Tool; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool

## The idea
Some colors seem to come toward you. Others seem to go away. Red, orange, and yellow are warm and advance. Blue and violet are cool and retire. Vanderpoel says violet works well as a background, because it pushes forward any brighter color on top. Use this to make depth without drawing it.

> "Red is a warm color and an advancing one." (Color Problems, PDF page 59)

## Look
Page 53: read about violet as a retiring color. Page 59: read about red. Page 169, Plate V: see small orange on green on violet, like a box inside a box.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Rectangle Tool with Fill set to Foreground color. Fill the whole page with violet.
3. Draw a large green square in the middle. Draw a smaller orange square inside.
   You should see the orange come toward you.
4. On a new layer, draw three circles of the same size in a row: one red, one yellow, one blue.
5. Put them on a gray background. Squint.
   You should see the blue circle sit farther back.
6. On a new layer, make a landscape with the Polygon Tool. Fill the far hills with pale blue.
7. Fill the middle hills with green. Fill the front with a warm yellow-orange.
   You should see a feeling of distance.
8. Swap: fill the front with pale blue and the far hills with orange.
   You should see the picture look flat.
9. Write "advancing" and "retiring" in small text near the right shapes.
10. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/024-warm-cool.kra.

## Check your work
- Did the orange square come forward from the green?
- Did the blue circle seem to sit back?
- Did the swapped landscape look flatter?

## Make it yours
- Make a sea poster. Use warm color for the closest wave.
- Design a calm room scene using mostly cool colors.

## New words
- **advancing color** — a color that seems to come forward
- **retiring color** — a color that seems to go back
- **warm** — red, orange, and yellow colors
