# 003. Plane Shapes That Hold Things

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/003-planes.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 002
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 56–57; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool; Krita Manual, Ellipse Tool; Krita Manual, Polygon Tool; Krita Manual, Arrange

## The idea
A plane is a flat shape with clear edges. Shapes can be made by hand, so they look organic. They can also be exact, so they look geometric. Planes group things together. They also separate one part of a page from another. Hass says a line that closes becomes a shape.

> "A plane is a flat surface that has defined borders." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 57)

## Look
Page 57, Figure 3.3 at the top: see a group of flat shapes. Find the sentence about planes acting as a separating device.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Add a Vector Layer: click the arrow next to + in the Layers docker.
3. Pick the Rectangle Tool. Hold Shift and drag to make a square. Set Fill to Foreground color in Tool Options.
4. Pick the Ellipse Tool. Hold Shift and drag to make a circle. Use a second color.
5. Pick the Polygon Tool. Click three points, then press Enter to close a triangle. Use a third color.
6. Pick the Bezier Curve Tool. Draw a blobby, hand-made shape. Close it by clicking the first point.
   You should see an organic shape beside the geometric ones.
7. Make a group: put three small shapes close together at the top left.
8. Make a divider: draw a long thin rectangle to split the page in two.
   You should see the page read as two parts.
9. Pick the Shape Selection Tool. Select all shapes. Open the Arrange docker and click Align Left.
10. Click Distribute centers equidistantly horizontally if you picked three or more shapes in a row.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/003-planes.kra.

## Check your work
- Can you point out one geometric shape and one organic shape?
- Did your small group of shapes read as one cluster?
- Did your long rectangle divide the page into two parts?

## Make it yours
- Build a face using only planes. No lines.
- Make a map of your room from flat shapes.

## New words
- **geometric** — made from exact shapes like squares and circles
- **organic** — shaped by hand, like things in nature
- **group** — things placed so they look related
