# 026. Harmony One Pair Three

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/026-harmonies.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 025
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel 1903), PDF pages 62,110,231,233; Krita Manual, Polygon Tool; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool; Krita Manual, Fill Tool

## The idea
Vanderpoel's first harmony is the simplest: one color with its tints and shades. Next come pairs of colors, called dyads. Then come triads, three colors that sit well together. You will color one simple picture three ways. Then you can see which harmony fits which mood.

> "the first kind of color harmony, one-color combinations" (Color Problems, PDF page 62)

## Look
Page 62: read about one-color combinations. Page 110: read about dyads and triads. Page 231, Plate XXXVI: see six good pairs. Page 233, Plate XXXVII: see good triads.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Draw a simple house with the Polygon Tool and the Rectangle Tool. Include a roof, wall, door, window, and sun.
3. Select all and copy. Paste two more copies. Move them to make three houses in a row. Make them small.
4. House 1: color with one hue only. Use tints and shades of green.
5. House 2: color with one pair, such as blue and orange. Use tints and shades of them.
6. House 3: color with a triad of red, yellow, and blue. Use one strong color and two lighter ones.
   You should see three different moods.
7. Fill a rectangle under each house with its main color.
8. Label each house with the Text Tool: "one color", "pair", "triad".
9. Write one word for the mood of each house.
10. Choose which house you like best, and mark it.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/026-harmonies.kra.

## Check your work
- Does house 1 use only one hue?
- Do houses 2 and 3 use the colors the label says?
- Can you say which harmony feels calmest?

## Make it yours
- Color the same house with a pair you pick from Plate XXXVI.
- Make a house for a cold winter day.

## New words
- **harmony** — colors that look good together
- **dyad** — a pair of colors
- **triad** — a group of three colors
