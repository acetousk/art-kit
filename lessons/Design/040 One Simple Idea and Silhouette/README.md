# 040. One Simple Idea and Silhouette

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/040-silhouette-poster.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 039
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF pages 67,106,205,213; Krita Manual, Polygon Tool; Krita Manual, Bezier Curve Tool; Krita Manual, Fill Tool; Krita Manual, Text Tool

## The idea
The best poster is often the simplest. Kauffer says to see not how much can be put in, but how much can be left out. A silhouette is a flat dark shape. It gives strong contrast and reads from far away. Use a bulky shape and few words.

> "The best poster is apt to be the simplest : strong and bold or delicate and suggestive" (The Art of the Poster, PDF page 67)

## Look
Page 205: see the dancer silhouette and the red script. Page 213: see the black eagle and lion on a pale ground. Page 106: read the paragraph on silhouettes.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Choose one subject that is bulky and easy to read, such as a cat, a ship, or a tree.
3. Add a Vector Layer. Use the Bezier Curve Tool or the Polygon Tool to draw its outline.
4. Close the shape. Fill it with solid black using the Fill Tool or a fill in Tool Options.
5. Look for thin parts that vanish. Make them thicker.
6. Make the background one flat color. Pick a color that contrasts strongly with the black.
   You should see a clear shape from far away.
7. Pick the Text Tool. Write a slogan of four words or fewer.
8. Place the slogan in the empty space. Use one color from the page.
9. Add a small line for the details in text size.
10. Squint. Remove one thing that is not needed.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/040-silhouette-poster.kra.

## Check your work
- Can you read the silhouette from far away?
- Is your slogan four words or fewer?
- Did you remove at least one thing you did not need?

## Make it yours
- Make a poster of your hand as a silhouette.
- Try a white silhouette on a dark ground.

## New words
- **silhouette** — a flat dark shape with no inner detail
- **slogan** — a short phrase that sticks in the mind
- **economy** — using as little as needed
