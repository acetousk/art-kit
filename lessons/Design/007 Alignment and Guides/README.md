# 007. Alignment and Guides

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/007-alignment.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 003
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 71–72; Krita Manual, Grids and Guides Docker; Krita Manual, Snapping

## The idea
Alignment means lining up the edges or centers of things. When items share one edge, the eye sees order. Your page then looks planned. Hass lists four text alignments: flush left, flush right, centered, and justified. In Krita, guides give you invisible lines to snap to.

> "Alignment refers to lining up the top, bottom, sides, or middle of a text, composition, or grouping of graphic elements on a page." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 72)

## Look
Page 71: read the line "If everything is equal, nothing stands out." Page 72: find the four kinds of horizontal alignment in the first paragraph.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Choose View ‣ Show Rulers. Choose View ‣ Show Guides.
3. Drag from the left ruler onto the canvas to make a vertical guide. Place it at about 200 px.
   You should see a thin line on the canvas.
4. Press Shift + S to open the Snapping pop-up. Turn on Guides.
5. Pick the Text Tool. Click near the guide and type a title. Use the Shape Selection Tool to move it.
   You should see the text snap to the guide.
6. Make four more lines of text in a column. Snap each one to the same guide. This is flush left.
7. Make a second vertical guide near the right edge. Make three lines that snap to it. This is flush right.
8. Make a guide in the exact center. Make three centered lines. Use the Arrange docker to Align Horizontally Center.
9. Compare the three columns. Which looks tidy? Which looks tense?
10. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/007-alignment.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the left lines share one clean edge?
- Can you name which alignment you like best for a short poem?
- Did every item snap to a guide instead of floating?

## Make it yours
- Make a bus schedule with all times flush left.
- Design a menu with centered items. Does it feel formal?

## New words
- **alignment** — lining up edges or centers
- **flush left** — text with a straight left edge
- **guide** — a thin helper line you can snap to
