# 009. Emphasis and Movement

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/009-emphasis-movement.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 008
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 73–74; Krita Manual, Polygon Tool; Krita Manual, Transform Tool; Krita Manual, Layers

## The idea
Emphasis means one focal point wins first. Make it bigger, darker, brighter, or more unusual than the rest. Movement tells the eye where to go next. A diagonal line toward the upper right feels like going forward. Overlapping shapes that get smaller and lighter feel like depth.

> "Emphasis is created in graphic design by making only one focal point and clearly emphasizing it" (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 73)

## Look
Page 73: read the Emphasis paragraph, then the start of Movement and Figure 3.18. Page 74: find the sentence about diagonals toward the upper right corner.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Add a Vector Layer. Pick the Ellipse Tool. Draw a big dark circle in the lower left.
3. Draw a second circle behind it, smaller and a lighter color, a little up and right.
4. Draw a third circle, smaller and lighter again. Make five circles in all.
   You should see a path rising to the upper right.
5. Pick the Shape Selection Tool. In the Arrange docker, use Bring to front and Lower.
6. Order the circles so the biggest is in front.
7. Add one tiny bright shape, such as a yellow triangle, at the end of the path.
8. Squint at the page. Which shape wins first? Is it the one you want?
9. Add a title with the Text Tool in the upper right. Keep it small and dark.
10. Now test the opposite. Save, then flip the page with Image ‣ Mirror Image Horizontally. Look at it.
   You should see the movement now go to the upper left, and feel less forward.
11. Press Ctrl + Z to undo the flip. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/009-emphasis-movement.kra.

## Check your work
- Is there only one clear focal point?
- Do the shrinking circles lead the eye along a path?
- Did the flip change how forward the movement felt?

## Make it yours
- Make a rocket poster where overlapping shapes show it flying away.
- Use a diagonal path of footprints to lead to a door.

## New words
- **emphasis** — making one thing stand out first
- **movement** — the path the eye follows
- **depth** — the feeling that some things are far away
