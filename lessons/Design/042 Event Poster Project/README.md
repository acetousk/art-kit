# 042. Event Poster Project

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/042-event-poster.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lessons 007 to 041
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF pages 62,84; Krita Manual, Vector Layers; Krita Manual, Grids and Guides Docker; Krita Manual, Text Tool

## The idea
Design a real poster for a real event, such as a school fair or a film night. Use your AIDA plan. Use one idea, a silhouette or strong shape, three colors and black, and clear type levels. Use a grid for placing. Kauffer says a poster can win by the play of lines and masses. Use that.

> "simplicity of composition, restricted use of colour, the importance of a predominant pattern, and contrast by isolation" (The Art of the Poster, PDF page 84)

## Look
Page 84: re-read the four traits of the Japanese print influence. Page 62: read the part about human interest and abstract design.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 2400 px at 150 ppi. Save it as ~/Art/Design/042-event-poster.kra.
2. Write who, what, when, and where in a small text note. Choose one thing a person must remember.
3. Make four thumbnails in one corner. Pick the best.
4. Set guides for margins and a grid. Use the Grids and Guides docker.
5. Add a Vector Layer. Draw your main shape or silhouette big, with strong contrast.
6. Add a flat background color. Use three colors and black only.
7. Add the title with the Text Tool in display type. Add a subtitle.
8. Add the details in a small text block. Align it to a guide.
9. Add the call to action, such as "Come on Friday".
   You should see a clear path: attract, interest, action.
10. Squint and step back. Check the focal point, the balance, and the type levels.
11. Fix one weak area.
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/042-event-poster.kra.

## Check your work
- Does the poster attract attention in one second?
- Can a person find the date and place fast?
- Do you use only three colors and black?

## Make it yours
- Make a second poster for a different event.
- Make a small version of the same poster as a post for a screen.

## New words
- **call to action** — words that tell people what to do
- **layout** — how the parts are placed on the page
