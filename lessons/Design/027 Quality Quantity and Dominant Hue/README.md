# 027. Quality Quantity and Dominant Hue

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/027-quantity.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 026
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel 1903), PDF pages 119–120,239,247; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool; Krita Manual, Fill Tool; Krita Manual, Layers

## The idea
Two harsh colors can be fixed. Vanderpoel gives rules. Change the quality by adding white, black, or gray to one. Change the quantity by using more of one and less of the other. Add a third color or a little black. Let one hue dominate the whole design.

> "a small amount of a dark shade of one color balance a much larger amount of a light tint of another color" (Color Problems, PDF page 119)

## Look
Page 119: read the numbered ways, from Third to Fifth. Page 120: read about adding black, and Seventh. Page 239, Plate XL: see a big blue field and a small yellow patch. Page 247, Plate XLIV: see a pattern ruled by one hue.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Rectangle Tool. Make four equal panels, 2 by 2.
3. Panel 1: fill half with strong yellow and half with strong blue. They fight.
4. Panel 2, quantity: fill almost all of it with blue. Add a small yellow patch.
5. Panel 3, quality and quantity: fill most of it with a pale blue tint. Add a small dark yellow-brown shade.
   You should see a quiet, richer pair.
6. Panel 4, black: use the same colors as panel 1. Add thin black lines between them.
   You should see the colors get along.
7. Add a Paint Layer on top. Set its Blending Mode to Multiply.
8. Paint a light yellow wash over all four panels. This is a dominant hue.
9. Write the rule name under each panel.
10. Which panel is the best? Mark it.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/027-quantity.kra.

## Check your work
- Did panel 1 look harsh?
- Did black lines help panel 4?
- Did the yellow wash join the colors in panel 4?

## Make it yours
- Fix two colors you dislike together using one of the rules.
- Make a pattern ruled by a dominant green.

## New words
- **dominant hue** — the one color that rules a design
- **quantity** — how much of a color you use
- **quality** — how pure or bright a color is
