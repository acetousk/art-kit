# 025. Complements Make Colors Glow

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/025-complements.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 024
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel 1903), PDF pages 77,87,92,217; Krita Manual, Ellipse Tool; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool

## The idea
Two colors are complements when they sit opposite on the color wheel. Together they look brighter. Red and blue-green are a pair. Vanderpoel tells how the painter Turner painted a red buoy in a calm sea of gray-green. The red then pulled all the eyes. A small amount of a complement is a strong accent.

> "painted in the foreground of his picture a scarlet buoy" (Color Problems, PDF page 92)

## Look
Page 77: read how red and blue-green mix to gray. Page 87: read about the chromatic circle. Page 92: read about Turner and the buoy. Page 217, Plate XXIX: see complements arranged in a circle.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Ellipse Tool. Draw a big circle. Divide it into six wedges with lines from the Straight Line Tool.
3. Fill the wedges in wheel order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet.
4. Draw lines across the center that join opposite wedges.
   You should see red face green, orange face blue, yellow face violet.
5. Below, make three pairs of squares. Put a small square of one color inside a large square of its complement.
6. Look at each pair for a while.
   You should see both colors glow.
7. Make a calm picture: a sea in pale gray-green made with the Rectangle Tool.
8. Add one tiny red buoy on the sea.
   You should see the buoy become the focal point.
9. Write the Turner story in two short lines with the Text Tool.
10. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/025-complements.kra.

## Check your work
- Are red and green opposite on your wheel?
- Did a small red spot win the eye in the green sea?
- Can you name a complement pair for blue and one for yellow?

## Make it yours
- Design a poster for a pumpkin sale with an orange and blue pair.
- Pick a pair for your own logo.

## New words
- **complement** — the color opposite another on the wheel
- **accent** — a small bright touch
- **color wheel** — colors in a circle in order
