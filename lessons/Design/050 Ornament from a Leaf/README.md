# 050. Ornament from a Leaf

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/050-leaf-ornament.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 049
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** The Grammar of Ornament (Jones 1856), PDF pages 14,35,303,305; Krita Manual, Multibrush Tool; Krita Manual, Vector Layers; Krita Manual, Bezier Curve Tool

## The idea
Jones says to use flowers and leaves as the base for ornament, but in a simplified, conventional form. Do not copy the plant. Pull out its shape and make it flat. Check how the Egyptians made the lotus into a pattern. Then take one leaf and make it a flat motif. Repeat it in a ring or a border.

> "Flowers or other natural objects should not be used as ornament, but conventional representations founded upon them" (The Grammar of Ornament, PDF page 14)

## Look
Page 14: read Proposition 13. Page 35, Plate IV: see the lotus drawn as flat patterns. Pages 303 and 305, Plates XCI and XCII: see leaves drawn from nature, full size.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 1800 px at 150 ppi.
2. Look at Plate XCI on page 303. Pick one leaf shape, such as the horse-chestnut.
3. Add a Vector Layer. Pick the Bezier Curve Tool. Draw the outline of the whole leaf in a big size.
4. Simplify it. Cut the outline to five to seven strong curves. Remove every small bump.
5. Add the center vein and four side veins as thin lines.
6. Fill the leaf with one flat color. Fill the veins with a lighter color.
   You should see a flat motif, not a copy.
7. Copy the motif. Paste it small, in a ring of 8 around a center.
8. Or use the Multibrush Tool (Q), Symmetry mode, with 8 copies, to draw the motif in a ring.
9. Add a small round shape at the center. Add dots between the leaves.
10. Squint. Does the ornament read as one shape?
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/050-leaf-ornament.kra.

## Check your work
- Is your leaf flat and simple, not a copy?
- Do the repeats sit evenly around the center?
- Can you say what you removed from the real leaf?

## Make it yours
- Make a ring from a flower you know.
- Turn the leaf motif into a border strip.

## New words
- **conventional** — simplified into a flat stylized form
- **motif** — one repeated design unit
- **rosette** — a round pattern with parts around a center
