# 004. Texture and How a Surface Feels

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/004-textures.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 003
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 61–62; Krita Manual, Freehand Brush Tool; Krita Manual, Brush Preset

## The idea
Texture is how a surface looks and feels. A smooth, shiny surface feels cold and exact. A rough, matte surface feels warm and soft. Hass says all patterns can be made from dots and lines. In Krita you can build textures from marks. Choose a texture that fits your message.

> "All patterns can be reduced to dot and line and are organized by a grid system of some kind." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 62)

## Look
Page 61, Figure 3.8 at the middle: see a texture close up. Page 62: read what glossy paper feels like, and what rough paper feels like.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Rectangle Tool. In Tool Options set Fill to Not filled. Draw six equal boxes in two columns.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool (B). In Brush Presets choose a pencil brush preset.
4. Box 1: fill it with dots only. Press one tap for each dot.
5. Box 2: fill it with short straight lines, all the same way.
6. Box 3: fill it with crossing lines, like a net.
7. Box 4: fill it with small circles that touch.
8. Box 5: fill it with wavy lines, like water.
9. Box 6: scribble dense and rough, like stone.
   You should see six different surfaces made from dots and lines.
10. Pick the Text Tool. Under each box, write one word for how it feels: smooth, bumpy, soft, and so on.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/004-textures.kra.

## Check your work
- Did you use only dots and lines to make every texture?
- Does each word you wrote match its texture?
- Could you choose one texture for a "cold" poster and one for a "warm" poster?

## Make it yours
- Make a texture that looks like your favorite food.
- Repeat one texture so it looks like a pattern on cloth.

## New words
- **texture** — how a surface looks and feels
- **matte** — dull and soft, not shiny
- **glossy** — smooth and shiny
