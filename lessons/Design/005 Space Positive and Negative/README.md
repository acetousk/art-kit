# 005. Space Positive and Negative

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/005-negative-space.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 003
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 59–61; Krita Manual, Selections; Krita Manual, Fill Tool

## The idea
Positive space holds the things you see first. Negative space is the quiet area around them. Hass names three kinds of figure and ground. In a stable one, the figure is clear. In a reversible one, both shapes act as figures. In an ambiguous one, the eye cannot find a start.

> "Without negative space, there is no positive space" (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 59)

## Look
Page 60, Figure 3.6: see how much quiet space surrounds the subject. Page 61, Figure 3.7, the FedEx truck: find the arrow between the E and the x in the white space.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Make a stable picture first. Pick the Ellipse Tool and draw a black circle in the lower left.
   You should see a clear shape on a calm white page.
3. Make a second picture on a new layer: fill the whole page with 40 black dots of many sizes.
   You should see no clear first place to look.
4. Hide the second layer by clicking its eye icon in the Layers docker.
5. Make a reversible picture. Pick the Rectangle Tool. Draw a black square in the middle.
6. Pick the Polygon Tool. Draw a white triangle that cuts into the black square from the left.
7. Draw a black triangle that cuts into the white page on the right.
   You should see the black and white shapes trade places as you look.
8. Pick the Text Tool. Write "stable", "ambiguous", "reversible" in small type, away from the art.
9. Toggle the layers on and off. Decide which design pulls your eye best.
10. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/005-negative-space.kra.

## Check your work
- In your stable picture, is the empty page calm and quiet?
- In your dot picture, did the eye fail to find a start?
- Did the reversible shapes seem to swap roles?

## Make it yours
- Draw an animal where the empty space makes a second shape.
- Take a photo-like scene in your head. Where would you leave empty space?

## New words
- **negative space** — the quiet empty area around the main thing
- **figure** — the main shape you notice first
- **ground** — the space behind and around the figure
