# 017. Similarity and Proximity

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/017-similarity-proximity.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 016
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 74–75,77; Krita Manual, Grids and Guides Docker; Krita Manual, Ellipse Tool

## The idea
Gestalt means unified whole. The mind wants to join things into groups. Things that look alike group together. This is similarity. Things that sit close also group together. This is proximity.

> "Similarity of medium, shape, size, colour, or texture will trigger a sense of similarity." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 75)

## Look
Page 74: read the six Gestalt principles at the bottom. Page 75, Figure 3.19: see rows of squares that join by look. Page 77, Figure 3.22: see dots that form a big shape by closeness.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Open the Grids and Guides docker (Settings ‣ Dockers). Set X spacing and Y spacing to 100 px.
3. Turn on Show Grid and Snap to Grid.
4. Pick the Ellipse Tool. Hold Shift. Draw a black circle that fills one grid cell.
5. Copy and paste the circle to make a 6 by 6 block of black circles in the top half.
6. Select every circle in the second and fifth rows. Make them red.
   You should see red rows jump out, though all are the same size.
7. In the bottom half, make a new block of black circles.
8. Place the circles in columns 1 to 3 closer together.
9. Move three circles far away to the right edge.
   You should see a big group and three loose "falling" circles.
10. Now combine both ideas. Make a heart or an arrow from red circles in the top block.
11. Use the Text Tool. Write "similarity" above and "proximity" below.
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/017-similarity-proximity.kra.

## Check your work
- Did the red rows read as lines?
- Did the close circles read as one group?
- Could you make a shape from circles that are only alike in color?

## Make it yours
- Make a school crest from only dots.
- Make a crowd of dots where one dot is different.

## New words
- **Gestalt** — a theory about how the mind groups what it sees
- **similarity** — things that look alike seem to belong together
- **proximity** — things that sit close seem to belong together
