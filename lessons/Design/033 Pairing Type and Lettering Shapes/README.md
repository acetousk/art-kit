# 033. Pairing Type and Lettering Shapes

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/033-pairing-lettering.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 032
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF page 73; Composition (Dow 1913), PDF page 65; The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF page 32; Krita Manual, Text Tool; Krita Manual, Text Properties Docker

## The idea
Two fonts work best when they differ clearly. Hass says to pair a serif with a sans serif. If two fonts are too much alike, they look like a mistake. Dow says a block of letters forms a tone. Spacing of the letters comes first. Kauffer says that on a poster the lettering is part of the ornament, so it must match the design.

> "As contrast in elements diminishes, the elements begin to feel similar, and the level of visual interest decreases." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 73)

## Look
Hass page 73: read the sentence about serif and sans serif contrast. Dow page 65: read the paragraph "Lettering". Kauffer page 32: read the last paragraph on lettering.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Text Tool. Type a title in a bold serif font: "Autumn Fair".
3. Under it, type a subtitle in a light sans serif font.
4. Type three lines of details in the same sans font, smaller.
   You should see a clear pair with strong contrast.
5. Now make a bad pair on the right side. Use two serif fonts that look nearly alike.
6. Compare the two sets.
   You should see the bad pair look like a mistake.
7. Now make a lettering shape. Type the word "FAIR" in very heavy letters.
8. In the Text Properties docker, change Letter Spacing until the letters are even. Try tight and loose.
9. Squint at "FAIR". Does it look like one even gray block?
10. Fix any gap that looks too big or too small.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/033-pairing-lettering.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the good pair differ clearly?
- Does the bad pair look like a mistake?
- Does your "FAIR" look like an even block when you squint?

## Make it yours
- Pair fonts for a bakery menu.
- Design your initials as one lettering shape.

## New words
- **pairing** — using two fonts together on one design
- **letter spacing** — the gap between letters
- **tone** — how dark or light a block of text looks
