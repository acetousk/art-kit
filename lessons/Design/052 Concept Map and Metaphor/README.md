# 052. Concept Map and Metaphor

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/052-concept-map.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 051
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 44–45,47; Krita Manual, Freehand Brush Tool; Krita Manual, Text Tool

## The idea
A concept is not a message. It is an idea that puts the message in a fresh, memorable form. A concept map is a web of ideas around one word. Metaphor is one tool: it says one thing is another. Use it to find your idea. Then draw many thumbnails and pick one.

> "Brainstorm as many ideas as possible, using a mix of words and images." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 47)

## Look
Hass page 44: read the steps for a concept map and the first devices. Page 45: read Metaphor and Metonymy. Page 47: read the thumbnail steps.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 2400 x 1800 px at 150 ppi.
2. Open ~/Art/Design/051-client-brief.kra to re-read the brief.
3. Type "Green Hands" in the center with the Text Tool. Circle it.
4. Add ten words around it with the Text Tool, such as seed, hand, soil, sun, and bee.
5. Join them with lines. Add three sub-words to at least three of them.
6. Pick three words. For each, write a metaphor: "a hand is a leaf".
7. Use the Freehand Brush Tool. Draw 12 tiny thumbnails of marks from the metaphors.
8. Mix words and images. Do not judge yet.
9. Pick the three best. Circle them.
10. Write one line for each about the message it sends.
11. Choose your concept. Write it in one sentence.
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/052-concept-map.kra.

## Check your work
- Does your map have at least ten words?
- Does one metaphor surprise you?
- Does your chosen concept say "Grow something"?

## Make it yours
- Make a concept map for your own name.
- Try a pun or a hidden shape in one thumbnail.

## New words
- **concept** — an idea that gives a message form
- **metaphor** — saying one thing is another
- **concept map** — a web of linked ideas
