# 049. Seamless Tile in Wrap Around Mode

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/049-tile.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 048
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** The Grammar of Ornament (Jones 1856), PDF pages 141,145; Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF page 62; Krita Manual, View Menu; Krita Manual, Patterns Docker; Krita Manual, Fill Tool

## The idea
A pattern repeats a small tile. When the tile is seamless, you cannot see the edges. Hass says that when a pattern is seen as a whole, its parts melt away into a larger field. Krita's Wrap Around Mode shows the image as if tiled. A mark that goes off one edge comes back on the opposite edge. Jones shows many tile designs from the Alhambra.

> "When the pattern is seen as a whole, its individual components melt away and lose their identity to the larger field of the pattern." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 62)

## Look
Jones pages 141 and 145: see tiles from the Alhambra with repeating star and diamond shapes. Hass page 62: read the paragraph on pattern.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 600 x 600 px at 150 ppi.
2. Choose View ‣ Wrap Around Mode.
   You should see the canvas repeat in every direction.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool and a hard round brush. Draw a leaf shape in the middle of the tile.
4. Draw a second shape that crosses the right edge.
   You should see it appear again on the left edge.
5. Draw a third shape that crosses the top edge and comes in at the bottom.
6. Add small dots in the gaps to balance the density.
7. Zoom out. Look at the repeat.
   You should see no seams and a clear rhythm.
8. Fix any gap that looks empty or any shape that looks too crowded.
9. Add a flat background color on a layer under the shapes.
10. Choose File ‣ Export. Save it as a PNG named 049-tile.png in ~/Art/Design.
11. Open the Patterns docker. Use the open-file button to import 049-tile.png.
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/049-tile.kra.

## Check your work
- Can you see any seam where the tile edges meet?
- Does the pattern have an even rhythm?
- Is the tile imported in the Patterns docker?

## Make it yours
- Make a tile from your logo mark.
- Make a geometric tile from squares and triangles.

## New words
- **tile** — one small unit that repeats
- **seamless** — having no visible join
- **wrap around** — the image repeats past each edge
