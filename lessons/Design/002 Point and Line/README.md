# 002. Point and Line

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/002-point-line.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 001
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 55–56; Composition (Dow 1913), PDF page 17; Krita Manual, Freehand Brush Tool; Krita Manual, Straight Line Tool; Krita Manual, Bezier Curve Tool

## The idea
Point and line are the first two building blocks of design. A point marks a place. It can pull the eye to one spot. A line joins two points or shows a path. A line can be real or only implied, like a row of dots. Dow, who taught art in 1913, calls line the harmony of lines and spaces.

> "A line connects two points, or traces the path of a movement." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 56)

## Look
Page 55: read the words focal point. Page 56, Figure 3.2 at the top: see how simple lines make a picture. Dow, page 17: find the three words Line, Notan, Color in the numbered list.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl + N and make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Straight Line Tool. Draw two horizontal lines across the page to make three bands.
3. Pick the Freehand Brush Tool (B). In the Brush Presets docker, choose a hard round brush. Set size to 40.
4. Band 1: click one single dot, away from the center.
   You should see the eye go to that dot first.
5. Click nine more dots of the same size around the band. Look again.
   You should see the first dot lose its power.
6. Band 2: draw a straight line with the Straight Line Tool. Draw a wavy line with the Bezier Curve Tool.
7. Under them, click seven dots in a row, with equal gaps.
   You should see the eye join the dots into a line.
8. Band 3: draw a thin line with brush size 6.
9. Next to it, draw the same line with brush size 80.
   You should see the thick line look like a shape.
10. Draw twelve thin lines close together in a block. See the block as one shape.
11. Pick the Text Tool. Label the bands "point", "line", and "line becomes plane".
12. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/002-point-line.kra.

## Check your work
- Does one dot among many lose its power as a focal point?
- Did your row of dots look like a line?
- Did a very thick line begin to look like a plane?

## Make it yours
- Draw a path of dots that leads the eye to a small star.
- Use only lines to draw your pet or a favorite object.

## New words
- **focal point** — the first place the eye goes
- **implied line** — a line the eye sees, though none is drawn
- **plane** — a flat shape with edges
