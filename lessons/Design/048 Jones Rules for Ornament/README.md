# 048. Jones Rules for Ornament

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/048-ornament-study.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 047
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** The Grammar of Ornament (Jones 1856), PDF pages 14,41,65; Krita Manual, Straight Line Tool; Krita Manual, Bezier Curve Tool; Krita Manual, Grids and Guides Docker

## The idea
Owen Jones, in 1856, collected ornament from many lands. He wrote a list of propositions. Four help you now. Ornament should rest on a geometric construction. Harmony balances straight, slanted, and curved lines. Lines should flow out of a parent stem, and curved joins should meet in tangent.

> "All ornament should be based upon a geometrical construction." (The Grammar of Ornament, PDF page 14)

## Look
Page 14: read Propositions 8, 10, 11, and 12. Page 41, Plate VII: see Egyptian borders. Page 65, Plate XV: see Greek frets drawn on a square grid.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 1200 px at 150 ppi.
2. Open the Grids and Guides docker. Set X spacing and Y spacing to 50 px.
3. Turn on Show Grid and Snap to Grid.
4. Pick the Straight Line Tool. Set brush size to 10.
5. Draw a Greek fret in a strip 5 cells high. Use Plate XV as your model.
6. Keep all lines on the grid.
   You should see a spiral of right angles.
7. Repeat the fret unit four times across to make a border.
8. Turn off Snap to Grid. Pick the Bezier Curve Tool. Draw a long wavy stem under the fret.
9. Add leaves and curls that branch off the stem. Each should start as a tangent to the stem.
   You should see lines that flow, with no sharp bumps.
10. Repeat the unit to make a vine border of three repeats.
11. Pick the Text Tool. Write "geometry" under the fret and "flow" under the vine.
12. Add Proposition 8 and Proposition 11 in small text near the matching border.
13. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/048-ornament-study.kra.

## Check your work
- Is your fret built on the grid?
- Do the vine curves join each other without corners?
- Does every branch grow from the main stem?

## Make it yours
- Draw a fret border for a bookmark.
- Make a border with a wave and a zigzag together.

## New words
- **ornament** — decoration added to a surface
- **tangent** — touching a curve and going the same way
- **proposition** — a rule stated for discussion
