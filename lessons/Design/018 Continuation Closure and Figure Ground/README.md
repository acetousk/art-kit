# 018. Continuation Closure and Figure Ground

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/018-closure-marks.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 017
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 76,78; Krita Manual, Ellipse Tool; Krita Manual, Polygon Tool; Krita Manual, Fill Tool

## The idea
Continuation means the eye follows a path, even past the page edge. Closure means the mind finishes a shape that is not complete. Figure and ground means the mind can see the shape and the space around it as two things. Designers use these tricks in marks and logos. They make a small shape say more than it draws.

> "Closure is a design technique that uses the mind’s tendency to complete incomplete shapes" (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 76)

## Look
Page 76, Figure 3.21: see a triangle appear between three cut circles. Page 78, Figure 3.23: see black and white squares trade roles.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Add a Vector Layer. Divide the page into three bands with the Straight Line Tool.
3. Band 1, continuation: draw a curve with the Bezier Curve Tool.
4. Add dots along it. End the dots at a star.
5. Look at it.
   You should see the eye follow the dots to the star.
6. Band 2, closure: use the Ellipse Tool to draw three black circles in a triangle pattern.
7. Use the Polygon Tool to draw three white wedges that cut a corner from each circle.
8. Point the cuts at the center.
   You should see a white triangle that is not drawn.
9. Band 3, figure and ground: draw a black square with the Rectangle Tool.
10. Use the Text Tool to write one big white letter inside the square. Use a heavy font.
11. Draw a small black shape that cuts into the white letter. Fill a gap with the Fill Tool.
   You should see the letter and a second shape in the white space.
12. Write the three names in small text beside the bands.
13. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/018-closure-marks.kra.

## Check your work
- Did your eye follow the dots to the star?
- Can you see the triangle that has no outline?
- In band 3, did you see both the letter and the shape around it?

## Make it yours
- Make a bird that is only five dots and two lines.
- Make a letter that hides a small animal in its white space.

## New words
- **continuation** — the eye follows a path or direction
- **closure** — the mind finishes an incomplete shape
- **figure and ground** — the shape and the space around it
