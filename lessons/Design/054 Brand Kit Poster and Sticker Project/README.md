# 054. Brand Kit Poster and Sticker Project

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/054-brand-poster.kra and ~/Art/Design/054-brand-sticker.kra (new files)
**Builds on:** lessons 042, 047, and 053
**Time:** 60 min
**Read:** Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF pages 49–50; The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF page 58; Krita Manual, Vector Layers; Krita Manual, Transform Tool; Krita Manual, File Menu

## The idea
Now put the brand to work. Make a sale poster and a round seed-packet sticker for Green Hands. They must look like one family. Hass says to test every step against the goals. Ask what this accomplishes. Kauffer says a trademark must be bold and clear on a poster.

> "Every step of a project should be evaluated in terms of the goals you have defined." (Design Fundamentals, PDF page 50)

## Look
Hass page 49: read about implementing solutions. Page 50: read the two questions in Evaluation. Kauffer page 58: read the paragraph on trade marks and brand names on posters.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1800 x 2400 px at 150 ppi.
2. Press Ctrl + S. Save it as ~/Art/Design/054-brand-poster.kra.
3. Open ~/Art/Design/053-brand-sheet.kra. Copy the logo. Paste it into the poster as a new Vector Layer.
4. Use your dominant color as the background. Use your accent for one shape.
5. Add the headline "Grow something" in your title font with the Text Tool.
6. Add the date, place, and "Spring Seedling Sale" in your text font. Set three clear type levels.
7. Align text to a guide. Leave plenty of empty space.
   You should see a clear order of reading.
8. Place the logo big and bold in the lower right.
9. Check against your brief. Is the message clear? Does it follow the brand sheet?
10. Press Ctrl + S. Then make a new document 1000 x 1000 px for the sticker.
11. Draw a circle with the Ellipse Tool. Fill it with the dominant color. Put the logo in the center.
12. Add "Grow something" in small text under the logo.
13. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/054-brand-sticker.kra.

## Check your work
- Do the poster and sticker look like one family?
- Do they use only the brand colors and fonts?
- Can you answer "What does this accomplish?" for each choice?

## Make it yours
- Make a third piece, such as a seed packet label.
- Make a social media square from the same brand.

## New words
- **brand kit** — the logo, colors, fonts, and pieces of a brand
- **evaluate** — to judge against a goal
