# 039. What a Poster Must Do

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/039-poster-plan.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 038
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** The Art of the Poster (Kauffer 1924), PDF pages 58,61; Design Fundamentals (Hass 2015), PDF page 43; Krita Manual, Rectangle Tool; Krita Manual, Text Tool; Krita Manual, Layers

## The idea
A poster is seen at a distance and in a hurry. It must attract attention, hold interest, create desire, and lead to action. This is the old AIDA plan. Kauffer's book of 1924 says a poster has two means to catch the eye: design and colour. Plan each of the four steps before you draw.

> "the poster relies upon two means of attracting attention : Design and Colour." (The Art of the Poster, PDF page 61)

## Look
Kauffer page 61: read the list of four steps. Page 58: find the paragraph about posters being simple and mostly pictorial. Hass page 43: read AIDA.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 3000 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick a real event, such as a school fair or a movie night.
3. Type its name in small text at the top.
4. Draw four equal rectangles with the Rectangle Tool. Each is a thumbnail of a poster. Set Fill to Not filled.
5. In each box, sketch a different layout with a pencil brush preset. Use only shapes and word blocks.
6. Under each box, write which step it does best: attract, interest, desire, or action.
7. In each box, mark the focal point with a red dot.
   You should see one clear red dot in each thumbnail.
8. Write in a note the one thing a person must remember.
9. Choose the best thumbnail. Mark it with a star.
10. Write why in one sentence.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/039-poster-plan.kra.

## Check your work
- Does each thumbnail have one focal point?
- Does your best thumbnail attract attention in one second?
- Can you say the one thing to remember?

## Make it yours
- Plan a poster for a lost-and-found board.
- Plan a poster that urges kids to recycle.

## New words
- **AIDA** — attract, interest, desire, action
- **thumbnail** — a very small first sketch
- **call to action** — words that tell people what to do
