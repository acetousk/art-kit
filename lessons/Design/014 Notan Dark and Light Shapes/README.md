# 014. Notan Dark and Light Shapes

**You make:** ~/Art/Design/014-notan-lines.kra (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 013
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Composition (Dow 1913), PDF pages 63–64,69; Krita Manual, Fill Tool; Krita Manual, Straight Line Tool

## The idea
Notan is a Japanese word that means dark and light. Dow used it for the harmony of dark and light shapes. Notan is not shading. It is flat black next to flat white. Start with a simple line design, then make some spaces black. Try several versions.

> "Recognition of Notan as an individual element will simplify the difficulties of tone-composition" (Composition, PDF page 64)

## Look
Dow page 63: read why he chose the word notan. Page 64: find the plate called Notan of Line at the bottom. Page 69: read how to start with two values.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new document 1500 x 2000 px at 150 ppi.
2. Pick the Straight Line Tool. Draw a simple design of crossing lines in a square. Use 5 to 7 lines.
3. Save the file as ~/Art/Design/014-notan-lines.kra.
4. Press Ctrl + A, then Ctrl + C. This copies the page. Add a new Paint Layer for each try.
5. Pick the Fill Tool. In Tool Options, set Fill Mode to Contiguous Region.
6. On layer 2, fill some closed spaces with black. Leave others white.
7. Paste a copy of the line design on layer 3. Fill a different set of spaces black.
   You should see a new picture from the same lines.
8. Make a third version, and fill so no two black spaces touch.
9. Make a fourth version. Fill so the black spaces make a diagonal band.
10. Show each layer one at a time. Pick the best. Hide the rest.
11. Press Ctrl + S. Save as ~/Art/Design/014-notan-lines.kra.

## Check your work
- Are your blacks flat, with no gray?
- Do the black spaces connect so the whole looks like one shape?
- Can you say why you chose your best version?

## Make it yours
- Start from a plaid and make five notan versions.
- Make a notan of your initials.

## New words
- **notan** — a design of flat dark and light shapes
- **value** — how light or dark a shape is
- **two values** — only black and white
