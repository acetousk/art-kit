# 005. Line Art and Mirror Check

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/005-line-art.kra  (new file, copied from lesson 004)
**Builds on:** lesson 004
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "Tips to fix your drawing and linearts on Krita", timeline items Mirror and Liquify deformation

## The idea
A flipped picture shows errors that you miss. Revoy uses a mirror check in Krita. He says: "The Mirror tool in Krita is cruel and reveal the truth" (David Revoy, Tips to fix your drawing and linearts on Krita). Then he fixes the shape. Liquify, in the Transform Tool, can push lines into a better shape.

## Look
In the video page, read the timeline items Mirror and Liquify deformation. Each one is short.

## Steps
1. Open ~/Art/Revoy/004-thumbnail-sketch.kra. Do not save over it.
2. Press File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/005-line-art.kra.
3. Add a new paint layer above sketch. Name it lineart.
4. Pick c4) Bristles Lineart - deevad 25. Draw clean lines over the sketch.
5. Hide the sketch layer.
6. Choose View ‣ Mirror View, or press M. Look at the flipped picture.
   You should see: Shape errors look clear when the picture is flipped.
7. Press M again to turn Mirror View off.
8. Pick the Transform Tool. In its Tool Options, open the Liquify tab.
9. Push the lines to fix one bent shape.
10. Save the file.

## Check your work
- Does a flipped view show shape errors? (Yes.)
- Is the line art on its own layer? (Yes.)
- Must you redraw the whole picture to fix one shape? (No.)

## Make it yours
- Fix one shape three times. Compare the results.
- Flip an old drawing. Find one error and fix it.

## New words
- **line art** — the final clean lines of a drawing
- **mirror view** — a flipped view of the canvas
- **liquify** — push pixels to change a shape
