# Revoy lessons (Krita)

David Revoy makes Pepper&Carrot. He paints it in Krita. He shares his brushes and his making-of posts for free.

| Unit | Lessons | What she learns |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 His brushes | 001–003 | Install the bundle. Try its brush groups. |
| 3.2 His methods | 004–013 | Sketch, line art, flat colors, shading, light, effects. |
| 3.3 A page, start to finish | 014–020 | Plan, sketch, panel, color, finish. Projects: 017, 020. |

Her files go in ~/Art/Revoy/. The brush bundle is in ~/Library/Painting/Brushes/.
Read the Source line in each lesson. It gives the credit and the license.
