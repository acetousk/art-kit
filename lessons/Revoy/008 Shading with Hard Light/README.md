# 008. Shading with Hard Light

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/008-shading.kra  (new file, copied from lesson 006)
**Builds on:** lesson 006
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "Shading workflow for comics - Krita", sections Setup the layer stack and Hard-Light blending mode; "Overview of my Krita Digital Painting Workflow − end 2022", section Shading

## The idea
Revoy shades on a gray layer. He sets the layer to Hard Light. Middle gray stays see-through. Dark paint then makes shadows, and light paint makes highlights. He says: "adding the hardlight, the mid-grey and the selection by color label can be done within a minute" (David Revoy, Shading workflow for comics).

## Look
In the comic video page, find the timeline item Hard-Light blending mode. In the overview, read the Shading part.

## Steps
1. Open ~/Art/Revoy/006-flat-colors.kra. Do not save over it.
2. Press File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/008-shading.kra.
3. Click the silhouette layer. Choose Layer ‣ Quick Clipping Group.
   You should see: A group holds the silhouette. A new layer sits above it.
4. Rename the new layer shade.
5. Set the RGB color to 127, 127, 127. Pick the Fill Tool. Fill the shade layer.
6. Set the shade layer blending mode to Hard Light.
   You should see: The gray layer looks see-through over the flats.
7. Pick a dark violet. Paint shadows on shade with a soft brush.
8. Pick a pale yellow. Paint light on the other side.
   You should see: Shadows darken, and the light side brightens.
9. Hide the shade layer, then show it again.
10. Save the file.

## Check your work
- Is middle gray see-through with Hard Light? (Yes.)
- Does the shade stay inside the shapes? (Yes.)
- Must the shade be a separate file? (No.)

## Make it yours
- Shade the same flats with a warm light from the left.
- Shade a bottle with cool shadows and one warm highlight.

## New words
- **Hard Light** — a blending mode; middle gray stays see-through
- **clipping group** — a group that limits shade to the shapes below
- **highlight** — the lightest part of a shape
