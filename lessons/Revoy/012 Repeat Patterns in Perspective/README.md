# 012. Repeat Patterns in Perspective

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/012-perspective-pattern.kra  (new file, copied from lesson 011)
**Builds on:** lesson 011
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "Paint faster repetitive patterns in perspective, step by step"; Krita Manual, Painting with Assistants

## The idea
A floor pattern takes long to paint by hand. Revoy paints one tile. Then he copies it and moves the copy in perspective. He says: "Painting complex patterns for background in perspective can really be tedious and a long task" (David Revoy, Paint faster repetitive patterns in perspective).

## Look
In the video page, read the timeline items Painting the texture from scratch and Pasting the texture and transformation.

## Steps
1. Open ~/Art/Revoy/011-background.kra. Do not save over it.
2. Press File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/012-perspective-pattern.kra.
3. Add a new paint layer. Name it tile.
4. Pick the Assistant Tool. Add a vanishing point assistant on the floor.
5. Pick b3) Basic Oval Brush - deevad 25. Paint one tile with a few simple shapes.
6. Press Ctrl+C, then press Ctrl+V to copy the tile.
7. Pick the Transform Tool. Move and scale the copy along the perspective lines.
   You should see: The copy follows the perspective lines.
8. Repeat steps 6 and 7 until the floor is full.
9. Save the file.

## Check your work
- Does one tile repeat across the floor? (Yes.)
- Does the assistant help the copies line up? (Yes.)
- Must you draw every tile new? (No.)

## Make it yours
- Paint a brick wall with one brick and a vanishing point.
- Paint a tiled bathroom floor with one tile.

## New words
- **vanishing point** — the point where parallel lines meet
- **tile** — one small shape that repeats
- **perspective** — a way to draw depth on a flat surface
