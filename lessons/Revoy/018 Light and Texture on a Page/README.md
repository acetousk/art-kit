# 018. Light and Texture on a Page

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/018-light-texture.kra  (new file, copied from lesson 017)
**Builds on:** lesson 017
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "Overview of my Krita Digital Painting Workflow − end 2022", section Shading; Pepper&Carrot ep06_The-Potion-Contest/E06P03.kra (reference only)

## The idea
Revoy finds the key light and the shadows first. Then he adds texture above the paint. He says: "I usually start by finding the good couple of Key-light and shadows." (David Revoy, Overview of my Krita Digital Painting Workflow). A texture layer adds grain. It should stay subtle.

## Look
Open E06P03.kra and look at the top layers in the Layers docker. Find the texture layer. Do not change the file.

## Steps
1. Open ~/Library/Painting/Pepper-and-Carrot/ep06_The-Potion-Contest/E06P03.kra.
2. Look at the layer stack. Close the file. Click No if Krita asks to save.
3. Open ~/Art/Revoy/017-project-page-shading.kra.
4. Press File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/018-light-texture.kra.
5. Add a new paint layer above all layers. Name it texture.
6. Set the texture layer blending mode to Soft Light SVG.
7. Pick y) Textured Random Noise - deevad 25. Pick a gray color.
8. Paint a light grain over the whole page.
   You should see: The page gets grain and more depth.
9. Lower the texture layer opacity until the grain looks subtle.
10. Save the file.

## Check your work
- Is the texture above the shading? (Yes.)
- Must the texture be strong? (No.)
- Does the key light still show? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Add texture to the background only.
- Use a different textured brush from the bundle. Compare the grain.

## New words
- **texture layer** — a layer with grain or surface detail
- **grain** — small specks that make a surface look real
- **Soft Light SVG** — a blending mode that keeps light and dark areas soft
