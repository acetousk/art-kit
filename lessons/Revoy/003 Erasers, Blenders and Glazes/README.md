# 003. Erasers, Blenders and Glazes

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/003-blend-erase.kra  (new file)
**Builds on:** lesson 002
**Time:** 30 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "Krita brushes 2025-01 bundle", sections Intro and FAQ

## The idea
The bundle has more than pencils. It also has erasers, blenders, and glazes. A blender mixes colors on the canvas. A glaze adds a thin layer of color. Revoy keeps copies of common tools. He says: "Having duplicates of the presets in my pack allows me to slightly update their behavior if I want."

## Look
In the Brush Presets docker, find the names that start with a1), a2), k) and d).

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N. Make a new file, 2000 by 1400 pixels.
2. Pick b2) Basic Size Fill Area - deevad 25. Fill a solid shape.
3. Pick k) Blender Rake Smudge - deevad 25. Drag it across one edge of the shape.
   You should see: The edge looks soft and mixed.
4. Pick d) Glazing Round - deevad 25. Paint a thin layer over part of the shape.
5. Pick a1) Eraser Tip - deevad 25. Erase a small corner.
6. Pick a2) Eraser Kneaded Soft - deevad 25. Lift color from one soft patch.
   You should see: The soft eraser makes a gentle edge.
7. Press Ctrl+Z if a step went wrong.
8. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/003-blend-erase.kra.

## Check your work
- Does a blender mix the colors on the canvas? (Yes.)
- Does an eraser remove paint? (Yes.)
- Do you need one brush for every job? (No.)

## Make it yours
- Paint a fruit with only a blender and a glaze.
- Compare the hard eraser with the soft eraser on one shape.

## New words
- **blender** — a brush that mixes nearby colors
- **glaze** — a thin layer of color over another color
- **eraser** — a brush that removes paint
