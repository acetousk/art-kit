# 011. Backgrounds with Less Detail

**You make:** ~/Art/Revoy/011-background.kra  (new file, copied from lesson 010)
**Builds on:** lesson 010
**Time:** 35 min
**Read:** David Revoy, "A background painting tip that saved me tons of time."

## The idea
Backgrounds can take too long. Revoy adds detail to only part of a background. He paints the rest almost flat. He says: "Managing the amount of details was always a difficult topic for me" (David Revoy, A background painting tip that saved me tons of time).

## Look
In the article, read the first two paragraphs. Note the idea of partial detail.

## Steps
1. Open ~/Art/Revoy/010-color-balance.kra. Do not save over it.
2. Press File ‣ Save As. Use ~/Art/Revoy/011-background.kra.
3. Add a new paint layer at the bottom. Name it background.
4. Pick a flat sky color. Pick the Fill Tool. Fill the background layer.
5. Pick b3) Basic Oval Brush - deevad 25. Paint simple hill shapes.
6. Pick a small brush. Add detail only near the main character.
7. Open the Gaussian Blur filter from the Filters menu. Use it on the hill layer.
   You should see: The hills look far away.
8. Save the file.

## Check your work
- Does Revoy paint most of a background flat? (Yes.)
- Does blur push a background back? (Yes.)
- Must every part of a background have full detail? (No.)

## Make it yours
- Paint a room with detail only near the window.
- Paint a night version of the same background with less detail.

## New words
- **flat** — one even color with no detail
- **background layer** — the lowest layer, behind everything
- **Gaussian Blur** — a blur filter that softens a layer
