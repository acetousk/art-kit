# 019. Warm and Cool Colors

**You make:** ~/Art/Painting/019-warm-cool.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 018
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Color Problems (Vanderpoel), PDF pages 53, 55, 57, 85; Krita Manual, Digital Color Mixer

## The idea
Some colors feel warm, like yellow and orange. Some feel cool, like blue and violet. Warm colors seem to come forward, and cool colors seem to sink back. Light has a color too. In sun, snow looks yellow and its shadow turns blue. Use warm light with a cool shadow, or the reverse.

> "the shadow will also be seen to be more or less blue" (Vanderpoel, PDF page 85).

## Look
Page 53: violet and its warm or cool kinds. Page 55: blue and cold. Page 57: yellow and warm. Page 85: snow with sun and shadow.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open ~/Art/Painting/015-sphere-light.kra. Save it as 019-warm-cool.kra.
2. Make the lit side warm: paint pale yellow-orange on the upper left of the ball. Use Alpha Lock.
3. Make the shadow side cool: paint blue-violet on the lower right.
   You should see: The ball looks like it sits in sunlight.
4. Click the shadow layer. Turn on Alpha Lock. Paint the shadow blue-violet.
5. Duplicate the ball and its layers. Group them. Move the copy to the right.
6. On the copy, flip it: make the lit side cool blue and the shadow side warm orange.
   You should see: It now looks like moonlight with a warm lamp.
7. Make a third ball in gray. Compare all three.
8. Use the Color Sampler Tool with Ctrl. Pick a warm light color from the first ball.
9. Open the Digital Color Mixer. Mix your light color with blue. Look at the in-between colors.
10. Note which pair of balls feels like day, and which like night.
11. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use the path ~/Art/Painting/019-warm-cool.kra.

## Check your work
- Do warm colors seem to come forward? (Yes.)
- Can a shadow take a cool color in warm light? (Yes.)
- Does the same ball look different in warm and cool light? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Paint an apple in morning light and in evening light.
- Pick colors from a real photo. Find the cool and warm in the shadows.

## New words
- **warm color** — a color like red, orange, or yellow
- **cool color** — a color like blue, green, or violet
- **complement** — the color across the color wheel
