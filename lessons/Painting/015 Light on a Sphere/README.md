# 015. Light on a Sphere

**You make:** ~/Art/Painting/015-sphere-light.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 013
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Krita Manual, Mixing Colors; Krita Manual, Krita 4 Preset Bundle Overview; Krita Manual, Layers

## The idea
A ball shows light and shadow in clear parts. Pick one light direction. The side facing the light is bright, with a small highlight. The light turns slowly into shadow. The darkest band is the core shadow, and a little bounced light lifts the edge. Work in gray first, so you only think about value.

> "glazing with the multiply blending mode to create nice shadows" (Krita Manual, Mixing Colors).

## Look
Mixing Colors: look at the glazing picture of the shadows. Preset Bundle Overview: read the line about Multiply under Adjustments.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new 2000 by 2000 canvas. Fill the background with light gray.
2. Add a layer named ball. Pick the Elliptical Selection Tool. Hold Shift and drag a big circle.
3. Fill the circle with mid gray. Deselect.
4. Turn on Alpha Lock for the ball layer.
5. Decide: light comes from the upper left. Put a small arrow in a corner with a pen to remind you.
6. Pick a soft airbrush preset. Paint darker gray on the lower right.
   You should see: The shadow side forms.
7. Paint the core shadow: a dark band a little inside the lower right edge.
8. Paint a thin band of lighter gray on the very edge.
   You should see: That is bounced light.
9. Paint light gray on the upper left. Make the center of that area almost white.
10. Add one small dot of white for the highlight. Use a small hard brush.
11. Add a layer named shadow under the ball. Fill a dark gray oval on the floor.
12. Set the shadow layer to Multiply.
13. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use the path ~/Art/Painting/015-sphere-light.kra.

## Check your work
- Is the highlight in the part that faces the light? (Yes.)
- Is the core shadow the darkest band on the ball? (Yes.)
- Is the cast shadow on the side opposite the light? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Paint the ball three more times with the light at the top, right, and bottom.
- Make a rough ball and a shiny ball. Change only the highlight.

## New words
- **highlight** — the brightest spot where light hits a shiny thing
- **core shadow** — the darkest band on the side facing away
- **bounced light** — soft light that reflects back onto a shadow edge
