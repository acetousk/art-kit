# 028. Painted Backgrounds

**You make:** ~/Art/Painting/028-painted-background.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 014
**Time:** 45 min
**Read:** Pepper&Carrot ep10_Summer-Special/E10P01.kra; Krita Manual, Krita 4 Preset Bundle Overview; Krita Manual, Group Layers

## The idea
A background sets place and mood. Depth comes from layers. Far things are paler, bluer, and have less detail. Near things are darker, warmer, and sharper. In Revoy's page from episode 10, the sky and the bg are separate layers inside an art group. You build a scene back to front, so each layer is simple.

> "Lighten - Brightens only the area with the selected color: a good brush to paint depth of field (sfumato) and fog." (Krita Manual, Krita 4 Preset Bundle Overview).

## Look
E10P01.kra: the group art holds, from top: post, paint-over, Lines, the pasted layers, sky, bg. Look at the sky layer alone.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Open ~/Library/Painting/Pepper-and-Carrot/ep10_Summer-Special/E10P01.kra. Do not save over it.
2. Open the group art. Hold Alt and click sky.
   You should see: You see the sky alone.
3. Alt-click bg.
   You should see: You see the ground alone. Alt-click again to go back.
4. Find the layer Lines. Read its blending mode.
   You should see: It is Grain Merge.
5. Close the file. Click No when Krita asks to save.
6. Make a new 2400 by 1600 canvas. Add a layer named sky.
7. Draw a Linear gradient from a pale blue to a warm cream.
8. Add a layer named far mountains. Use a Freehand Selection. Fill with a pale blue-gray.
9. Add a layer named fog. Pick the Lighten airbrush in the Adjustments family.
10. Paint soft fog at the base of the mountains.
11. Add a layer named mid hills. Fill with a darker blue-green. Add small rock shapes with a Dry Painting preset.
12. Add a layer named near land. Fill with the darkest green. Paint grass blades along the top edge.
13. Add trees on a layer. Make the near ones big and dark. Make the far ones small and pale.
14. Group all layers. Name the group art.
15. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use the path ~/Art/Painting/028-painted-background.kra.

## Check your work
- Are far things paler and bluer than near things? (They should be.)
- Is each depth level on its own layer? (It should be.)
- Does the Lighten airbrush add fog? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Paint a desert at dusk with three layers of dunes.
- Paint a city skyline with three layers of buildings.

## New words
- **depth** — the feeling of far and near in a flat picture
- **atmosphere** — air, fog, and haze that soften far things
- **mood** — the feeling a picture gives
