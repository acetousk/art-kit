# 001. Your First Canvas

**You make:** ~/Art/Painting/001-first-canvas.kra
**Builds on:** nothing
**Time:** 25 min
**Read:** Krita Manual, Create New Document; Krita Manual, Navigation

## The idea
A canvas is the blank page you paint on. You choose its size when you make it. A bigger canvas holds more detail, but it is slower. Krita saves your work as a .kra file. You also learn to move around the canvas: zoom, pan, rotate, and mirror. These moves help you check your work all the time.

> "Mirroring is a great technique that seasoned digital artists use to quickly review the composition of their work" (Krita Manual, Navigation).

## Look
Create New Document: find the Dimensions tab and the Content tab. Navigation: find the list under "Navigating the Canvas" (Panning, Zooming, Mirroring, Rotating).

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Press Ctrl+N.
   You should see: The New Document dialog opens.
2. Under Custom Document, open the Dimensions tab.
3. Set the width to 2400 and the height to 1600. Use pixels.
4. Set the resolution to 150 ppi.
5. Open the Content tab. Type the name first canvas. Keep the white background.
6. Click Create.
   You should see: A white canvas fills the middle of the window.
7. Press the + key to zoom in. Press the - key to zoom out.
8. Hold Space and drag with the pen.
   You should see: The canvas slides under your pen.
9. Press Ctrl+[ to rotate the canvas. Press 5 to reset it.
10. Press B to pick the Freehand Brush Tool. Draw a spiral in the middle.
11. Press M to mirror the view.
   You should see: Your spiral flips. Press M again to flip it back.
12. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use the path ~/Art/Painting/001-first-canvas.kra.

## Check your work
- Does the M key change your painting, and not only the view? (No: it only flips the view.)
- Does a bigger canvas let you paint more detail? (Yes.)
- Does the 5 key reset the canvas rotation? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Make a tall canvas and a wide canvas. Draw the same shape on both.
- Mirror the view while you draw a face. Find what looks wrong.

## New words
- **canvas** — the page you paint on in Krita
- **pixel** — one tiny colored square; a picture is a grid of them
- **ppi** — pixels per inch; how tightly pixels pack
