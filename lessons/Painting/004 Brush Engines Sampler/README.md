# 004. Brush Engines Sampler

**You make:** ~/Art/Painting/004-engine-sampler.kra
**Builds on:** lesson 003
**Time:** 40 min
**Read:** Krita Manual, Pixel Brush Engine; Krita Manual, Color Smudge Brush Engine; Krita Manual, Bristle Brush Engine; Krita Manual, Krita 4 Preset Bundle Overview

## The idea
A brush engine is the part of Krita that decides how a brush makes marks. The Pixel engine is the default. It stamps its tip along the stroke. The Color Smudge engine pushes and mixes paint that is already on the canvas. Dry presets cover old paint. Wet presets and Blender presets mix with it.

> "This brush paints impressions of the brush tip along your stroke with a greater or smaller density." (Krita Manual, Pixel Brush Engine).

## Look
Preset Bundle Overview: read Dry Painting, Wet painting, and Blender. Color Smudge Brush Engine: read the first paragraph.

## Steps
1. Open Krita. Make a new 2400 by 1600 canvas.
2. Pick a Basic preset. Paint three wide bands: red, yellow, and blue. Make them touch.
3. Pick a Blender preset. Drag from the red band into the yellow band.
   You should see: The two colors smear into orange.
4. Drag a second Blender stroke across the yellow and blue bands.
   You should see: You get green.
5. Pick a Dry Painting preset. Paint a stroke over the bands.
   You should see: It covers the colors and does not mix.
6. Pick a Wet painting preset. Paint the same stroke below it.
   You should see: It picks up the colors under it.
7. Pick a preset from the Textures family. Paint a stroke. Look at the grain.
8. Under the bands, draw a row of six boxes with a pencil preset.
9. In each box, paint a small leaf with one family: Basic, Dry, Wet, Blender, Texture, Pencil.
10. Write the family name under each box with Ink Fineliner.
11. Look at all six leaves. Pick the one you like best.
12. Save the file with File ‣ Save As. Use the path ~/Art/Painting/004-engine-sampler.kra.

## Check your work
- Does a Dry Painting preset mix with old paint? (No.)
- Does a Blender preset paint a new color by itself? (No: it moves paint that is already there.)
- Is the Pixel engine the default brush engine in Krita? (Yes.)

## Make it yours
- Paint a sunset sky. Use a Wet preset to blend the colors.
- Make a second sampler with the soft airbrush and the Bristle family.

## New words
- **brush engine** — the part of Krita that decides how a brush paints
- **blender** — a brush that moves paint instead of adding it
- **dab** — one stamp of the brush tip
